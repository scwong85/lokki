# M21 — Senate Square, part 1 (levels S1–S3)

Branch `m21-senate-a`. First levels of the new Senate Square district (`docs/design/levels-senaatintori.md`).

## Built
- **Cathedral steps** landmark (40 treads, three lanes with low rails, landing at the top), Alexander II statue and a hot-dog cart; `cheese` prop (round bread cheese).
- Level infrastructure: `district`, per-level `start` position, `bells` schedule (the bell rings, everyone looks at the tower, `bellSnatch` / `bellRung` events), repeatable `gullInZone` triggers.
- **S1 Cheese Run** (Leipäjuusto Downhill: roll the cheese down the right lane into the hot-dog cart), **S2 Say Pigeon!** (be in three graduation photos), **S3 Ding Dong** (snatch sandwiches while the bell rings; ring the bell yourself).
- Each level has a hidden `???` to-do and a co-op twist.

## Exit criteria
- The three levels unlock in order after Harbour Day and can be finished in about 5 minutes each.
- Tests: `tests/unit/senate.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/senate.spec.ts`.
