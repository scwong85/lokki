# M26 — Co-op controls on touch and gamepad

Branch `m26-coop-controls`. Closes the controls debt left by M25 (taunt and emotes were keyboard only).

## Built
- **Touch**: a hold **Taunt** button and an **Emote** button (each tap cycles wave, laugh, point, facepalm). They only show when there is a partner (online co-op or `?coop`). They sit in the top-right row beside Lock and Hawk-eye.
- **Gamepad**: **right stick click** held = taunt, **left stick click** = emote (cycles in turn).
- The Controls screen lists both for keyboard (T, 1 to 4), touch and controller.

## Not done
- The two new touch buttons are not in the drag-to-arrange set (they keep fixed spots).
- The `?coop` debug second gull has no emote or taunt buttons on its pad.
