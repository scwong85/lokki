# M18 — Market Square Act 2: skill levels 9–14

## Goal
Six levels from `docs/design/levels-kauppatori.md` (Act 2, "Showing off"): each one **lends you the skill it teaches** for the level (you do not need to have earned it, nothing is saved from the loan) and builds its headline puzzle around it. Unlock order: 8 → 9 → … → 14.

| 9 | **Caw of the Wild** (`caw-of-the-wild`) | Mimic (V) | **"Group Photo"**: a tour group of eight stands around the square; crow calls make them walk over to see what it is. Each level also has a hidden two-player to-do in online co-op, and its skill as the reward.

## Built
- **Level-granted skills** (`LevelDef.skills`, `SkillSet.grantForLevel`): lent for the level, not saved, the passive effects apply too.
- **Tour groups follow sounds:** people with role `tour` walk to a noise (crow call), then stand there.
- **Vendors hear:** a noise near a vendor makes him stare at it and ignore gulls for `deter.vendorDistract` seconds (flock call, crow, a clattering bottle). Event `vendorDistract`.
- **Hidden lines** (`hiddenLines`): visible only while hawk-eye is on.
- **Pigeon rules** (`pigeonRules`): a disguised gull that flies or runs loses the disguise.
- **Thermals** (`LevelDef.thermals`, `FlightInput.lift`): lift for a gliding gull inside the column, shown as pale columns; events `thermal`, `altitude80`.
- **New triggers:** `npcsInZone` (people with a role in a zone), `propInZone` with a `count` (and height for stacking), `carryLanded` with a zone.
- **New quest events:** `caw`, `hawk`, `flock`, `lineHit`, `vendorDistract`, `disguiseEnded`.
- Timed quests restock their props when they reset (`heist`, `express`, the bottle).
- Tests: `tests/unit/act2.test.ts` and `tests/smoke/act2.spec.ts` (each level's special rule in the browser).

## Playtest checklist
- [ ] L9: crow calls bring the group over; six in the frame; splat the photographer.
- [ ] L10: H shows the lines; flying through them hurts; the golden ice cream can be carried out.
- [ ] L11: chairs are liftable (E), stack into a tower (the physics may wobble).
- [ ] L12: G makes the vendors stare; you can take the three items inside the minute.
- [ ] L13: B makes you a pigeon; walking keeps it, flying/running ends it; the Warden ignores you while disguised.
- [ ] L14: thermals lift you toward the Uspenski dome (follow the pale columns); you can land on the dome with the bun before it goes cold (`quest.expressTime`).
- [ ] Each level about five minutes.

## Known limits
- Not played by hand: distances, timings and strengths come from the design and script tests.
- Simplifications of the design doc: the café tower puzzle has no waiter taking chairs back; Group Photo has no phone-ringtone twist; Thermal Express has no steam meter (a plain timer instead).
