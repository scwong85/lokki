# M47 — Chains I: five fetch levels rebuilt as chains, and the stuck-level fix

Branch `m47-chains`. Plan: `docs/design/chain-levels.md` (C4, first part).

## Fixed first (playtest: "taunt only works for a few seconds after the snatch, then the level is stuck")
- **The grudge lasts**: someone who is angry or upset and still wants comfort stays that way until they have bought it (the mood no longer fades after 30 s).
- **Taunting always works on them**: hold T and an angry person starts following you again, from any state and up to 50 m away, even after they had given up the chase.
- **The stall cannot run dry**: if the goods are gone (taken, smashed), the stall puts out a fresh one when someone buys.
- **Full hands**: a person who holds something (the student's cap) drops it to take the treat; before, they could never buy.

## Rebuilt as chains (one objective, five steps, `src/data/levels/chains.ts`)
Same skeleton as Map and Cone (provoke, lead to a stall while angry, they buy comfort food, take it, get away), each with its own cast, stall and way of provoking:
- **Vappu Eve (L15), "Cap Race"**: splat the student with the cap in his hand **twice** (one splat annoys him, a second within half a minute makes him furious and he chases you), lead him to the bun stall, take his bun.
- **Herring Market (L16), "A Good Soup"**: rob the fisherman's fish, lead him to the soup stall, take his bowl.
- **Wedding Bells (L17), "Bouquet Blues"**: snatch the bride's bouquet, lead her to the flower stall, take her new one.
- **Frozen Harbour (L18), "Polar Plunge"**: splat a swimmer twice, lead them to the doughnut stand on the ice, take the doughnut.
- **The Emperor's New Look (S6), "Hard Hat, Soft Heart"**: splat the foreman twice, lead him to the florist's stand, take his flowers.
The other quests of those levels stay (balloons, the ice walk, the secrets); the old jar swap, ring delivery, doughnut raid and wardrobe raid are gone. Rewards and unlocks are unchanged.

## Also
- Splatting the same person twice makes them angry (new); an angry person who craves something chases the gull.
- Level lists: Map and Cone and these levels keep their place in the order.
