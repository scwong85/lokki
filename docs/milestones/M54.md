# M54 — Prank buttons and Hide-and-BOO

Branch: `m54-prank-boo`.

## Built
- **Prank buttons.** Level `switches` gain effects `alarm` (a loud noise everyone hears) and `soak` (a sprinkler: everyone within 4.5 m of the switch's `target` is soaked, drops what they hold and runs; the fright then spreads, see M53). Peck with E, as with the jukebox. Tuning: `button.*`.
- **Hide and BOO.** Level `hides` are bushes. Crouch still (ground, under 2.5 m/s) inside one and people more than 1.2 m away cannot see you. Tap T (honk) while hidden for a BOO: everyone within 5 m drops what they hold and panics. Events `boo`, `boo:<role>`. Tuning: `hide.*`.
- **Levels:** M9 Big Red Button, M10 Peekaboo (series "Mischief (new)").
