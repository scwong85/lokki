# M27 — Havoc Hour prototype (H0 + H1)

Branch `m27-havoc-proto`. Plan: `docs/design/havoc-hour.md`, `docs/design/implementation-plans.md` (A, H0 and H1).

**Purpose: find out in one evening whether chaining mischief is fun.** If it is not, stop here (decision gate).

## Built
- **Havoc Hour** level (last in the level select, `?level=havoc-hour`): three minutes in the Market Square with every skill lent, the city on alert (start heat 15) and no to-do list.
- **Chain scoring** (`src/gameplay/havoc/scoring.ts`, pure): every kind of mischief has a base value (snatch 100, splat 80, photobomb 150, bell heist 150, tram stopped 150, perfect decoy 200 ...). Chain window 6 s. Each **different** kind in the window raises the multiplier (cap x8); repeating a kind scores half and does not raise it. Being caught by the city (spray, swat, line, net, drone) **breaks the chain** but keeps your points. Co-op events (decoy, pass, spotted, rescue) are worth extra.
- **Screen**: score, chain meter, clock and rank at the top; pop-ups for each bit of mischief ("Snatched! +300 x3"); a results card with score, rank, top mischief and biggest chain. Best score is saved (`havocBest`).
- **Run log** (`src/gameplay/runLog.ts`): every game event goes through one wrapper around `quests.onEvent` and is recorded (the later headlines and awards read it).
- Tuning group `havoc` (run length, chain window, multiplier cap, repeat factor).
- The clock starts on your first move. R restarts (a new run), L opens the level list.

## Not in this prototype
- No director or events: the Market Square cast is the same as free play. Senate Square and Esplanadi actors are not part of this run.
- No front page, daily edition or nest. Online guests do not see the score (host/single player only).
- Style tags ("unseen", "mid-air") only exist as the existing `snatchUnseen` event.
