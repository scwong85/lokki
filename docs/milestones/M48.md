# M48 — Mischief toys: Slip and Pied Piper, and the mechanics study

Branch `m48-mischief`. Study and plan: `docs/design/mischief-mechanics.md`.

## Why
Playtest: the chain levels all use the same mechanic (provoke, lure, stall, snatch). The study lists 15 "toys" (a toy is the thing a level is built around) and a rule: neighbouring levels never share their core toy. This milestone builds the first two, with a showcase level each.

## Built
- **Slip.** A splat on the ground is a wet patch for 40 s; a free wet fish (a prop tagged `slippery`) counts too. A walking person who steps in one slips: arms up, a long slide, what they hold flies out of their hand, and they are angrier. People can **patrol** a route (`NpcSpawn.patrol`).
  - **M1. Wet Floor** (level list, "Mischief (new)"): splat the quay on the hi-vis inspector's route (the zone is marked), watch him walk into it, take his lunch (a fresh one flies out if his was already taken), get away.
- **Pied Piper.** People marked `follows` fall in behind a taunting gull within 22 m, keep following 6 s after you let go, and queue at 1.2 m gaps. A level can list `parade` stops that fire an event when enough followers are in a place, and send them off.
  - **M2. Pied Piper**: gather four tourists with T, parade them to the west end, the east end, then onto the wrong ferry, and wave them off.
- Slipped people laugh at you (emote, wiggle, polaroid "Whoops!"). Dropped items are no longer "stolen" (`props.drop`), which also fixes full-handed buyers' dropped caps.
- Tuning: `mood.followRange`, `mood.followHold`.
- Tests: 9 unit tests (slip, follow, patrol, level data) and two browser tests that play both levels through the real game.

## Not done
The other toys in the catalogue (tablecloth yank, prank button, hide and BOO, gust, domino, impersonation), and converting the existing stall-chain levels to different toys (section 3 of the study).
