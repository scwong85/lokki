# M32 — World enrichment W4 (start): things that move

Branch `m32-world-w4` (on top of M31). Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W4).

## Built (no extra draw calls by day)
- **Trees sway**: the treetops lean with the wind and sway, each tree with its own phase. It is done in the vertex shader of the tree material (`swayTrees` in `src/render/motion.ts`), so all 260 trees cost nothing extra.
- **Boats bob and roll** at their moorings (`bobBoats`), the same way.
- **Street lamps glow at dusk and night**: warm orbs at the lamp bulbs, driven by the sky preset's `windowGlow`. The orb mesh is hidden by day (so no cost), one draw call while lit.
- Tuning group `motion`: `treeSway`, `swaySpeed`, `boatBob`, `lampGlow` (0 turns each off).

## Not done yet (rest of W4)
- Flags and bunting, steam from stalls and chimneys, falling leaves or blossom, weather presets, ambient sound zones, cloud shadows on the ground, grass tufts and flower beds.
- Shadows of swaying trees do not sway (the shadow pass uses the still shape); at this size it is not noticeable.
