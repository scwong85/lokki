# M115 to M117 — Act 1 on the new formula: 1.3 Market Day Heist, 1.4 One Bad Afternoon, 1.5 The Warden

Built together on one branch (`m115-act1-v2`), stacked on M114. Plan: `docs/design/act1-v2.md`. Each stage is a collection (rows in courses, delivered to the market hall roof) built from the older chains: the old clusters are **moved** across the map with `src/data/levels/relocate.ts` (people, patrols, bushes, switches, stations, wet-floor zones), so the same mechanics now cost a flight each way. The old stages stay in the Playground (`strings-attached-v1`, `mood-swings-v1`, `the-warden-v1`).

## 1.3 Market Day Heist (M115)
Heat, vendors with spray bottles and scare lines are live from the start. Six rows in three courses:
- **Casing the joint:** the Soup Man's bowl (tug, far west) and the Peekers' cone (hide in the bush, BOO, far east).
- **The inside job** (the vendor's corner, fixed in the city): the Bun Man's bun (the vendor sprays a tugging gull) and the Rich Man's lunch (get the vendor to soak him).
- **The getaway:** the Balloon Man's balloon (air snatch) and the Queue's cone (peck the alarm and the sprinkler button, 44 m apart).
Secret: Most Wanted (heat 60). Co-op: Team tug, Wet wingman. Reward: Hawk-eye.

## 1.4 One Bad Afternoon (M116)
Four rows in two courses, four moods used four ways: the Lost Tourist's cone (take his map, taunt him to the stall, **he buys it, and only that cone counts**: new `Station.tagBought`), the Inspector's lunch (splat his route, he slips), a Dancer's cone (peck the jukebox) and the Waiter's bun (ring the service bell). Co-op: Decoy and thief, Duet. Reward: Mimic.

## 1.5 The Warden (M117)
Boss day, the end of Act 1: the Warden is deployed from the start. Three rows (the panicking Queue's cone, the Bowlers' cone, a bun off the Diners' table by tablecloth yank) in two courses, then the boss chain (his sandwich at lunch, the hunt, a dodged swing, a splat with his umbrella down) opens once the rows are done. Co-op: Pincer, Volley. Reward: Quick bowels.
