# M104 — The punchline photo

From `docs/design/hooky-objectives-research.md` (c), row 8: the reward is the reaction, so the photo of a finished to-do should show the victim's face, not the middle of the screen. No slow motion and no camera move (both were removed in M87 for feeling wrong): only the picture.

## Built
- **Victim-framed polaroids:** a to-do with a victim is photographed half a second after it is done (`juice.punchlineDelay`, when their reaction is showing), cropped and zoomed onto the victim's head if they are on screen (else the middle of the screen as before). The caption is the wish ("Make the Maître lose his dignity in front of the guests.").
- **The discovery double take** gets a photo too: "Chef: "WHERE IS IT?"".
- `cropRect` (pure): the photo rectangle around a focus point, zoomed, always inside the screen.

## Known issue found on the way (not fixed here)
From a low camera near the quay (around the open paving south of the market tents, where the trial puzzles are), the sea is drawn over the paving: people and the gull look as if they stand in water, although the game treats the ground as land and the land polygons cover it. From higher up it looks right. It is a rendering problem (the sea plane against the land layer), older than these levels. Worth its own small fix.
