# M89 — Routines, part 2: people stop at the landmarks

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §3; the second slice (after M81's strollers).

## Built
- **Stops:** strollers and ferry passengers now walk a routine of 3 landmarks (`src/data/pois.ts`, `src/gameplay/routine.ts`): they walk to a spot on a ring round it (different for each person, walkable-checked), face it, and linger, then go on.
  - Kauppatori: the Havis Amanda statue (photo), the ferry (watch), the café (rest), the clock (watch), two stalls.
  - Senate Square: the emperor's statue (photo), the foot of the steps (watch), the bell tower (photo), the library (watch), a spot to rest.
  - Other districts keep the plain wander until their landmarks are listed.
- **Stances:** *photo* (phone held up), *watch* (a hand to the brow) and *rest* (a slouch) show in the pose; a fright ends it and they carry on afterwards.
- **Music beats guarding.** Found while testing: people shielding an ice cream from the gull never came to the boom box or the jukebox (shielding does not end while the gull is in view), so Block Party could stall. Now anyone calm enough to be frightened is drawn in, stops watching the gull and dances. A unit test covers it.
