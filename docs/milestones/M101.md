# M101 — Victim beats

From `docs/design/hooky-objectives-research.md` (c), rows 3 and 4: the victim's reaction is the payoff, so it has to escalate, the victim has to find out, and the switches need more ways in.

## Built
- **Reactions that escalate** (`Reaction.nth`, `Reaction.repeat`, `reactionPlays` in `src/data/reactions.ts`): a reaction can play on the 1st, 2nd or 3rd time its event happens, or every time. Before, every reaction played once per level, so the Chef's bell had one line and then silence.
  - P1, the bell: *"Hello? ...Who rang?"* (puzzled), then *"WHO keeps ringing this bell?!"* (cross, the guests point), then *"That is IT. I am never answering that bell again."* (facepalm, the guests laugh).
- **The discovery beat:** someone who minds an item (a watcher) and comes back to its spot finds out it is gone, even if they never saw who took it: a puzzled **?**, then a moment later anger and a line. That only happens once per theft, and again if the item comes back and goes again. `tuning.watch.discoverRange`, `discoverDelay`, `doubleTake`.
  - "My pie...? WHERE IS MY PIE?", "Who has taken the ceremonial scissors?", "Where is my golden fish? It was RIGHT HERE." The guests laugh. Quest event `discovered:<role>`.
- **Switches you can hit:** a splat, or a thrown or dropped thing that lands on a switch (within 1.4 m), sets it off like a peck (`hit:<id>` event). Ring the Chef's bell from the sky, set off the alarm with a bottle.
- The tablecloth yank can throw any kind of item (`goods.type` is any prop).
- A small queue for delayed beats (the double take).
