# M43 — Playtest fixes: bell heist, title list

Branch `m43-fixes` (stacked on `m42-outro`).

## Fixed
- **Bell Heist (S3) could not be completed** if a sandwich was snatched while the bell was quiet: the picnickers' two sandwiches were the only ones. Now, when the bell rings, any picnicker who lost the sandwich (snatched out of turn, dropped, eaten) gets a fresh one and is calm again ("The picnickers have fresh sandwiches"). `Npcs.restockHeld(role, release)`.
- **The title screen's level list could not be scrolled** (45 levels, and on phones `body.touch` switches touch scrolling off). The list now scrolls (max 38% of the height, the card itself scrolls on short screens), opts back in to `touch-action: pan-y`, opens at the current level and has district headings. The same opt-in is applied to the level list, options, skills, credits and level cards on touch.
- The title list now passes each level's district (it had been grouping everything under Kauppatori).

## Tests
`tests/smoke/fixes43.spec.ts`: a picnicker that lost a sandwich gets a fresh one when the bell rings; the title list scrolls, is `pan-y` and ends with the last level.
