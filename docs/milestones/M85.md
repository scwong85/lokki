# M85 — Stage 2.5: a bun for the pigeons

Playtest: "level 2-5 pigeon do not accept bun".

## Cause
The pigeon mechanism itself works (a bun dropped by the statue befriends the gang, tested through the real snatch and drop). But stage 2.5 reused the old level's cast with `slice(0, 3)`, which dropped the picnicker who holds a bun, so the only bun left in the stage was the library student's, which is also one of the three snacks of the library to-do. Use it for the library (or eat/lose it) and the pigeons can never be won.

## Fix
- The picnicker with the bun is back in 2.5, and a spare bun lies by the statue; the step's hint says where to find one.
- `tests/smoke/pigeon-bun.spec.ts`: a bun exists that is not the library's; snatching the student's bun and dropping it by the statue befriends the gang; a bun on the ground does too.

## Note
If it still fails for you, tell me where you stood and whether you carried, dropped (E) or threw (Q) the bun: a bun on the plinth top (above 1.4 m) does not count.
