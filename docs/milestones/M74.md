# M74 — Act 2 finale: stage 2.5 The Key to the City

Branch: `m74-act2-finale`. Plan: `docs/design/act2.md`.

## Built
- **2.5 The Key to the City** (the end of Senaatintori; golden dusk): the pigeons, the bell (every 30 s), the tram, the quiet library and a concert's silent breaks are all in the square. Chains with joke cards and reactions:
  - *Ceremony Crashers*: win the Pigeon Gang with a bun, pose for the front-page photo, wait for the bell, take the golden key while everyone looks at the tower, give it to the pigeons.
  - *Shhh!*: take three snacks from the revising students quietly (walking, not flying), get shushed, splat the librarian.
  - *Jumbotron*: get on the big screen three times, ring every phone in the silent break with a caw (Mimic, earned in 1.4), take the conductor's baton and conduct.
  - Co-op (required): Ceremony pincer (one taunts, the other takes the key) and Encore (two splats).
- **Pigeon Diplomacy**, the new skill (reward): the Pigeon Gang is friends from the start in every level that has them (the statue is yours to perch on).
- Clearing 2.5 opens the next act (arc 3, stage 1), which does not exist yet.
- Tests: 2.5 in `tests/unit/stages.test.ts`, the joke card test, `tests/smoke/stage-act2.spec.ts`.

## Playtest fixes (stage 2.5 and the Play button)
- **The conductor sank into the stage.** People always stood at ground height; the conductor stands on a 1 m stage. People can now stand higher (`NpcSpawn.y`): the model, the hand, the poop target, the gull's reach and sight are all measured from where they stand. The conductor is on the stage in both the stage and the old Concert Night level.
- **Could not conduct with the baton.** The baton route (three corners of the stage) only counted when the quest wanted its first checkpoint, but the quest only asks for the last step ("conducted"), so the route never started. A route is now followed when its last event is wanted. Browser test: `tests/smoke/act2-fixes.spec.ts` carries the baton round the stage.
- **The big Play button always started at the Market Square.** The gull was created at the city's default start; only a level change moved it. The gull is now created at the level's own start, so a saved or chosen Act 2 stage begins in Senate Square.
