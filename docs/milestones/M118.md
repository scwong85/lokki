# M118 — Playtest fixes: Bottle Service, and a heist that cannot lock

From the playtest of M113 to M117: 1.1 took about 8 minutes (good); in 1.2 the Bottle Service step "get the ice cream while they watch" could not be finished after a honk (taking the cone she dropped is a grab, not a snatch); in 1.3 only the bush row was solved in 10 minutes.

- **Bottle Service:** her cone is tagged (`watchCone`) and picking it up counts as the step, like a snatch. (Same family as the Clean Freak fix in M112.)
- **Courses can open early:** a course may say `need` (how many rows of the course before must be done). The next course opens, and the "served" beat plays, at that moment. Stage 1.3 uses `need: 1` twice, so one row you cannot solve never locks the others. The stage is still won only with every row.
- **1.3 reordered and softened:** course 1 is the bush and the balloon (the two easiest), course 2 the soup tug and the bun, course 3 the Rich Man's lunch and the queue; the two buttons stand 10 m either side of the queue (were 22 m); the goal line says where to look; the half title shows from 16 m (was 8 m).
- **Not reproduced:** "an item put on the roof before its course opens does not count". Tested in 1.2 with the herring, thermos, punnet and cone placed first: all counted when their courses opened. If it still happens, tell me which item.
