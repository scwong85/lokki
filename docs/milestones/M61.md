# M61 — Stage 1.2 Fish Market

Branch: `m61-fish-market` (stacked on `m60-stage-framework`). Plan: `docs/design/arcs.md`, Act 1 stage 2.

## Built
- **1.2 Fish Market** (act 1, stage 2; reward Fat bird), opened by stage 1.1. Introduces poop and wind, toss and the honk (tug, hiding, switches and the rest stay locked).
- Three objectives, each a chain with a way out: the Clean Freak (splat his sign, take his fish, get away), Bottle Service (make a noise, snatch the watcher's ice cream, get away), Honk! (two people drop their cones, take one, get away). They are the chains of Bombs Away, Nosy Parkers and Honk!, reused as data; the Nosy Parkers cluster stands 14 m east so the three casts do not crowd.
- Co-op (required with two gulls): Spotter and bomber (the Bombs Away one) and Relay (two mid-air passes).
- Tests: stage 1.2 in `tests/unit/stages.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/stage2.spec.ts`.

## Notes
- A honk also makes enough noise to count as the "make a noise" step of Bottle Service. That is fine here (two ways to do it), but worth watching in playtest.
