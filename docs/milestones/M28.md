# M28 — World enrichment W1: baseline, sky and light (+ playtest fixes)

Branch `m28-world-w1`. Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W1), `docs/design/implementation-plans.md` (C1).

## Playtest fixes
- **Turning is slower in the air**: `flight.airTurnRate` 3.8 to 3.0, `airTurnSlow` 0.8 to 0.65 (it had been raised in M16; the first value was 2.4). Tune live in the debug panel.
- **Havoc Hour chain cue is big now**: a large multiplier badge (x2, x3 ...) that pops when it rises and shakes when hot (x4+), a thicker chain meter that blinks red as the chain runs out, a coloured glow around the screen that grows with the multiplier, bigger score pop-ups, and a bell when the multiplier rises.

## World: sky and light
- **Sky dome** (`src/render/sky.ts`): replaces the screen-fixed gradient. A dome that follows the camera with a real horizon, a sun disc and glow, plus 22 drifting low-poly clouds (they move with the wind). Two draw calls.
- **Sky presets as data** (`src/data/skies.ts`): `noon` (today's look), `morning`, `golden`, `dusk`, `night`. `LevelDef.sky` picks one (`night: true` still works). Sky, fog, sun colour and direction, and the tint of the hemisphere light (blue-white above, warm below, so shadows go lilac instead of grey) all follow it. `blendSky` mixes two presets for a run that moves through the day (not used yet).
- Set on levels: Vappu Eve, The Rival and Key to the City golden; Harbour Day, Herring Market and Cheese Run morning; Christmas Market and the Great Heist dusk; Wedding Bells noon; Concert Night and Night Market night.
- **Bug fixed on the way**: the night dimming was overwritten every frame by the graphics update, so night levels only had a dark sky. The sky's `light` factor now scales the lights, and the sun shadow direction follows the preset.
- **Horizon backdrop** (`src/world/backdrop.ts`): about 170 plain, hazy blocks on land beyond the 560 m map edge, merged into one mesh with no collision, so the city no longer ends in bare ground when seen from the air.

## World: draw calls
- **Shopfronts moved into the window atlas** (a 7th row, on top), so each 160 m chunk of buildings is **one mesh instead of two**. That removes one draw call per chunk in the main pass and again in the shadow pass.
- **Render budget test** (`tests/smoke/perf.spec.ts`): fixed views at the market, high over the market, Senate Square and Esplanadi, on both presets, recording draw calls and triangles (written to `test-results/perf-*.json` and printed) and failing if calls jump past a generous ceiling.

| View | Before (high) | After (high) | After (low) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market | 191 calls / 241k tris | **159** / 241k | 112 / 169k |
| Market from 70 m | 193 / 253k | **166** / 253k | 105 / 173k |
| Senate Square | 92 / 137k | **75** / 137k | 46 / 83k |
| Esplanadi | 124 / 210k | **100** / 210k | 62 / 147k |

("Before" already includes the new sky, clouds and backdrop, which add three calls; the pre-M28 baseline was about three lower.) The window and shop look is unchanged in the screenshots.
