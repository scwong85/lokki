# M55 — Tablecloth yank and Service bell

Branch: `m55-cloth-bell`.

## Built
- Two more level `switches` effects (peck/E, like the jukebox):
  - `yank`: a pink-clothed table; E pulls the cloth, the goods (4 buns) fly off with a crash, and everyone within 5 m jumps, drops what they hold and runs (`owlScare:<role>`). Tuning `cloth.*`.
  - `summon`: a gold bell; E rings it and everyone of that role walks to it, leaving their post (fly away: a gull beside the bell makes them wary).
- `scareNear` now reaches people watching the gull too.
- Levels: M11 Tablecloth Trick, M12 Service Bell.
