# M100 — Joke-first trials

From `docs/design/hooky-objectives-research.md`: the trial puzzles were heists, not gags. Steps removed obstacles and delivered items, the cast were generic watchers, and the wronged person never saw what happened. M100 rewrites all three with existing systems, one victim and one trait per to-do, and the victim's reaction as the payoff.

## The three levels now
**P1. The Midsummer Buffet** (the Chef's golden pie)
- *Make the Chef answer the bell for nobody. Three times.* He walks to the service bell, finds nobody ("Hello? ...Who rang?"), the guests laugh; he gets crosser each time.
- *Make the Maître lose his dignity in front of the guests.* A splat on his jacket, a slip on his own route, or a dance he cannot stop: any counts, each with its own line.
- *Make Granny eat the Chef's golden pie, and let him see it.* Granny eats anything sweet near her blanket; the Chef storms over ("MY PIE! Granny!") and the crowd laughs. The level ends on him.
- Hidden: *give Granny a nasty surprise* (salmiakki).

**P2. The Grand Opening** (the Mayor's big day)
- *Get into the Mayor's front-page photo instead of him.* The Reporter counts 3, 2, 1 at the Mayor; be in the frame on the 1.
- *Cut the ribbon before the Mayor can.* His scissors, your beak (a honk from behind, the jukebox, a taunt). He is left miming with empty hands.
- *Make the Mayor lose the first ice cream of summer too.* Furious, he treats himself at the kiosk; take it and he chases you through his own crowd.
- Hidden: *let the Mayor catch you red-handed* (the Reporter gets the shot). Co-op: both gulls in the photo.
- The crowd now stands at the ribbon from the start (the ceremony), so there is no herding step.

**P3. Fire Drill** (the Guard's favourite day)
- *Make the Guard march the whole fish queue to the assembly point, for nothing.* The alarm (or a bun trail); "Head count... all present!"
- *Soak the Fishmonger at his own spotless counter.* The sprinkler, or a splat on his shiny sign: he scrubs in a panic ("My counter!").
- *Make the Fishmonger run into the Guard's fire drill.* Take the golden fish where he can see you and lead him to the green sign: "NO RUNNING DURING A DRILL! Get in line, sir."
- Hidden: *the Guard slips during his own drill.*

## Engine changes (small)
- `ObjectiveDef.victim`; a quest event `arrived:<goal>:<role>` whenever somebody gets where they were sent.
- People sent somewhere stay around there (a fire-drill crowd no longer drifts home).
- **The floating scissors, found:** somebody who keeps their place used to snap their own item back into their hand from anywhere, at hand height. Now an item a gull has carried (`Prop.byGull`) only goes back if it lies within 2.5 m of their spot (`npc.reclaimRange`); things they dropped themselves still come back.
- Verb gating checked: the P levels have no gate, so every verb is available.

## Tests
- `tests/unit/jokeFirst.test.ts`: on every trial puzzle, each step names a victim who has a name tag, the wish names them and no method, every step gets a mocking reaction (laugh, point, question, anger; never hearts), the level does not end on a delivery, and there is a hidden to-do.
- `tests/unit/reclaim.test.ts`, updated `buffet`/`puzzles2` unit and browser tests.
- Not done: taking the player's own actions out of `REACTION_EVENT`. Doing so fails three campaign chains (1.1 Breakfast, 1.3 Big Red Button, 2.4 Captain Gull) whose only "reactions" are the player's own; they get fixed in M105 with the stage rewrite.
