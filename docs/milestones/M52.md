# M52 — Tug of war

Branch `m52-tug`. Design: `docs/design/mischief-mechanics.md` sections 12 and 14.

## Built
- **On the ground, E next to someone's hand starts a tug**: hold E (slow) and mash E (fast); the orange meter shows who is winning. In the air the snatch stays instant.
- **Items say how they can be taken** (`PropDef.grab`): air only (balloon, cap, top hat, scarf), ground only (soup, herring), or both (default).
- **They pull back by mood** (calm 0.12, on guard 0.40, angry +0.25, startled by a honk 0.02, distracted ×0.3). A honk or a noise makes them let go quickly; a **vendor's spray breaks the tug and you lose it**.
- The tugged person stops wandering, faces you and pulls their hand away; if they win they watch you closely (no free retry for 1.2 s).
- Prompt on screen: "Hold E: tug the bowl of salmon soup (mash E to pull harder)", and the controls screen explains it.
- Level **M5. Tug of War**: tug the soup, dive at the balloon, take the bun either way with a vendor next door.
- Tuning: `tug.*` (start, hold, mash, grace, the pulls, reach).
- Tests: 11 unit tests (the meter, pulls, item modes, the tugged person) and two browser tests (what is tuggable or snatchable, and a real hold-E tug that wins plus a spray that loses).

## Not done
A partner joining the tug (co-op), more heat for tugging, per-item strengths, and online guests can only mash (their held key is not sent).

## Playtest questions

2. Is a tug too easy or too hard on someone who is watching you? (`tug.pullGuarded`, `tug.hold`, `tug.mash`)
3. Which items should be air-only, ground-only or both?
