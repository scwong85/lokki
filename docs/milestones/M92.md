# M92 — Breadcrumbs: the bun must visibly work, and levels picked from the menu get their crowd

Playtest: "bun does not affect people in the breadcrumbs".

## What I found
- The mechanism worked in the browser (a bun put down by the gull is eaten), but **only for somebody within 10 m**, and the level's six bystanders were spread 8 to 22 m apart: drop a bun in the middle of nowhere and nobody was close enough, with no sign anything had been tried.
- **A real bug on the side:** levels load in place when picked from the menu, and that path never spawned the background strollers (or used the new level's start for ferry passengers). Strollers only existed when a level was the first one opened. Stage crowds were missing in normal play since M81.

## Fixes
- Food now reaches 18 m (`crowd.crumbRange`), the bystanders stand closer, and the hint says anyone within about 18 m spots it.
- A person who spots a bun shows a **!** over their head and walks over, so you can see it working.
- Strollers are spawned when a level is loaded from the menu too, and ferry passengers walk to the current level's start (`levelStart()`).
- Tests: spotting range and the emote (unit); the real controls (grab with E, put down with E, eaten) and the menu path with strollers (browser).
