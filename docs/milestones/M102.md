# M102 — Rituals: moments you can see coming

From `docs/design/hooky-objectives-research.md` (c), row 5: a visible countdown routine any character can have, so the player can read it, wait for it, and time the mischief (Hitchcock's clock; Goose Game's routine windows).

## Built
- **`NpcSpawn.ritual`** (`src/gameplay/ritual.ts`): every `every` seconds the person counts **3, 2, 1** over their head, then does their thing for `hold` seconds, showing an icon. While it lasts they see nothing (eyes shut, or turned to something with `lookAt`). One `ritual:<role>` event each time; frightened, chasing or busy people skip it. The first time, a line explains it.
- **The trial puzzles use it:**
  - P1: the **Chef** tastes his cooking with his eyes shut (😋, every 24 s): the window to take the pie.
  - P2: the **Mayor** turns to his crowd for a little speech (🎤, every 28 s): the window for the scissors. (The Reporter's photo countdown was already a ritual.)
  - P3: the **Fishmonger** turns to polish his sign (🧽, every 26 s), and the **Guard** checks his watch (⌚, every 20 s).
- The nudges mention it, without saying what to do: "he counts himself in and shuts his eyes", "a man making a speech sees nothing at all".
