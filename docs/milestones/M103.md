# M103 — Neighbours, and secrets that half-reveal

From `docs/design/hooky-objectives-research.md` (c), rows 6 and 7: Goose Game's best gags tie two people together (the neighbours across the fence), and a hidden to-do is strongest as a half-known gap, not a blank "???".

## Built
- **Neighbours:** someone who minds an item and **sees it in somebody else's hands** (up to twice their usual sight range, if they are facing that way) reacts: anger at them, a puzzled look from the holder, and they march over and stop in front of them. Once per culprit. Quest events `sawWith:<watcher>:<holder>` and `arrived:confront:<watcher>`.
  - P1: the Chef, seeing Granny with his pie: *"Is that... MY PIE?"*, then, face to face, Granny: *"Lovely pie, dear. Bit dry."*
- **Hidden to-dos that half-reveal** (`QuestDef.reveal`): near a spot, the "???" line becomes a half title: *"??? …Granny's sweet tooth"*, *"??? …the Mayor's one good moment"*, *"??? …the Guard's big moment"*.
- **The results card** says how many hidden to-dos are left: "Secret to-dos: 1 of 1 found" or "0 of 1 found. 1 still hidden in this level."

## Tests
`tests/unit/neighbours.test.ts` (the Chef sees Granny with the pie when facing her, not with his back turned; he stops in front of her; every trial puzzle's hidden to-do has a reveal), `tests/smoke/neighbours.spec.ts` (the half title appears near Granny).
