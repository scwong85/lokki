# M79 — Dive snatch fix

Branch: `m79-dive-snatch`.

## Fixed
- A dive at somebody's hand took nothing: the fast dive counted as a slam (M53) and bowled the person over, so the item dropped to the floor. Now a **steep** dive (more than 30°, tuning `slam.maxAngleDeg`) at a held item within the beak's reach snatches it; a **shallow**, fast skim along the ground still bowls people over like skittles. Tests: unit (steep and shallow) and `tests/smoke/dive-snatch.spec.ts`; the Strike! tests use a shallow dive.
