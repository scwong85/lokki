# M19 — Act 3, themed days, part 1: levels 15–18

## Goal
The first four "market day" levels from `docs/design/levels-kauppatori.md` (Act 3): remixes with a theme. Unlock order 14 → 15 → … → 18.

| # | Level (`id`) | Theme | Headline puzzle | Other to-dos | Secret `???` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | **Vappu Eve** (`vappu-eve`) | A crowd of students in white caps, balloons | **"Cap Race"**: take the student cap from Havis Amanda (far west) and put it on the Warden (drop it by his lunch bench; he goes for lunch every 110 s) | Pop three balloons (poop on them) | Snatch a cap right off a student |
| 16 | **Herring Market** (`herring-market`) | Autumn market, a herring jury | **"Grandma Wins"**: take the champion's jar from the judge's table, leave grandma's jar for him to taste | Snatch the fisherman's fish | Make the judge say yuck (salmiakki) |
| 17 | **Wedding Bells** (`wedding-bells`) | A good-bird level | **"Something Borrowed"**: the ring box is on the ferry's roof; bring it to the groom at the altar | Snatch the bride's bouquet | Be in the wedding photo (the photographer counts 3, 2, 1) |
| 18 | **Frozen Harbour** (`frozen-harbour`) | Winter: the sea is ice, it snows | **"Polar Plunge"**: take three warm doughnuts the swimmers left on the ice | Walk on the ice for ten seconds | Poop on a winter swimmer |

Each level has a hidden two-player to-do in online co-op, and a skill reward.

## Built
- **Balloons** (new prop): float (hardly any gravity), pop when pooped on (`balloonPop` event). **Ring box** (new shiny prop).
- **Winter levels** (`LevelDef.winter`): the harbour is frozen (pale ice, no waves) so a gull **walks** on it instead of swimming and things lie on it; a light snowfall around you (`winter.snowCount`).
- **A student crowd** (people with white caps), reusing the Warden's lunch schedule, the child/teddy "wants" mechanic (judge, groom), and the selfie photographer.
- Tests: `tests/unit/act3a.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/act3a.spec.ts` (balloons float and pop, the jar swap, the ring gift, the ice).

## Playtest checklist
- [ ] L15: the statue cap, the Warden's lunch banner, dropping the cap by his bench; balloons float over the square and pop.
- [ ] L16: the judge looks away (noise), you take his jar; grandma's jar gets tasted.
- [ ] L17: the ring box on the ferry roof, the groom takes it; the photographer's countdown for the hidden photo.
- [ ] L18: walk on the ice (no swimming), the snow, the three doughnuts.
- [ ] Each level about five minutes.

## Known limits
- Not played by hand. Winter has no footprints that give you away and no sauna steam (the design doc's extras); Vappu has no crane and no 18:00 clock, the Warden's lunch is the window; Herring Market's jury has one judge.
