# M84 — Leftovers: timetables, gossip, secret trails

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §5 (items 4, 5 and 10).

## Built
- **Timetables** (`src/gameplay/timetable.ts`, seeded so a level always runs the same day): a **ferry** comes in (horn, a crowd of up to 6 passengers steps off at the quay and strolls into the square; the same people are reused, capped at 12), a **shower** (rain streaks, and a new puddle near the gull every ~1.4 s that makes people slip), a **gust** (5 s of wind that shoves light loose things along). A level opts in with `LevelDef.timetable`. On now: the Sandbox (all three), stage 1.1 (ferry), stage 2.1 (rain), Block Party (ferry). No campaign stage gets gusts, so puzzle items never blow away.
- **Gossip:** whoever sees a neighbour panic or give chase makes everybody within 14 m wary for 90 s; wary people grow suspicious 2.2× faster. Strollers pass it on too.
- **Secret trails:** a faint slow sparkle shows where a not-yet-won secret `???` to-do begins (within 30 m, or from afar under Hawk-eye). Follows the Highlight option.
- Tuning groups `timetable` and `gossip`.

## Still in the plan, not built
- Photo hunts (optional "take a picture of…" goals) and "three answers per prop kind" (a content pass over every prop).
- More timetable events (a band parade, a busker's set) and opting more stages in once the playtest says how the ferry and the rain feel.

## Exit criteria
- The square changes over time without breaking a puzzle; a seen panic makes people harder to rob for a while.
