# M56 — Gust, Domino, Impersonation, Dog-and-honk

Branch: `m56-gust-domino-disguise-dog` (stacked on M55).

## Built
- **Wing gust.** Fly low (under 3.4 m) and flap: every wingbeat blows light things (mass ≤ 0.8) within 3.2 m below you away from you. `LevelDef.sweep` counts tagged props that end up in a zone (`blown:N`). Tuning `gust.*`. Level M13 Leaf Blower.
- **Domino.** Switch effect `domino`: a row of tiles from the switch to its `target`; E on the first tile starts the fall (0.22 s per tile), the last tile knocks over whoever it lands on, and the knock-down chain (M53) carries on. Level M14 Domino Rally. Tuning `domino.*`.
- **Impersonation.** Switch effect `disguise`: the captain's cap (red cone shown on the gull for 25 s) means nobody sees a gull (`GullInfo.disguise`). Level M15 Captain Gull. Tuning `disguise.*`.
- **Dog and honk.** A honk within 35 m calls park dogs: they run straight to the honker, trampling picnics on the way (`Dog.summon`). Level M16 Hush Puppy (Esplanadi). Stay in the air, a dog chases a gull on the ground.
- Tests: `tests/smoke/mischief6.spec.ts`, unit in `tests/unit/contagion.test.ts`.
