# M44 — Chain levels C1 to C3: the Map and Cone slice

Branch `m44-chain-slice` (stacked on `m43-fixes`). Plan: `docs/design/chain-levels.md`.

## Built
- **Mood** on every person (`calm, annoyed, angry, upset, delighted`), shown as an emote over their head (anger, sweat, facepalm, heart) and fading back to calm after `tuning.mood.time`. A snatch makes them angry; a splat annoys and a second one angers; yuck upsets; a cheer delights.
- **The lure**: an angry chaser gives up after a few seconds, but keeps following a gull that **taunts (hold T)** within `tuning.mood.lureRange`.
- **Stations** (`LevelDef.stations`): a person who craves something (`NpcSpawn.craves`) and is annoyed, angry or upset, and passes a stall that sells it, walks up, waits `tuning.mood.buyTime`, and takes a real cone from the counter into their hand; delighted, and too happy to notice the gull for a while.
- **Chain quests** (`QuestDef.chain`): the to-do note shows one title, the steps done, the one to do now with its hint, and `?` for the rest. Steps are ordinary objectives with `after`, driven by new events: `robbed:<role>` (snatched from them), `atStation:<role>:<station>`, `bought:<role>`, `mood:<role>:<mood>`.
- **C1. Map and Cone** (level list, under "Chain levels (new)", needs level 1): snatch the map, lead the angry tourist to the ice cream stall (hold T), let him buy a cone, snatch the cone, get away, then the victory run. The stall's cones are real props (you can take them directly).
- Level lists got a `series` heading.
- Tests: 8 unit tests for mood, the lure and stations; a browser test drives the chain through the real simulation.
