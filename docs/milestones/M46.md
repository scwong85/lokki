# M46 — Playtest fixes: taunt, no precision landing, side card

Branch `m46-fixes`.

## Fixed
- **Taunting did nothing** in a fresh profile: the first-flight tips were still running, and the tips both blocked the taunt and used T as their skip key. T is now always the taunt (hold it; an angry person keeps following you); **Y skips the tips**. On phones the Taunt button also shows in chain levels (it used to appear only with a partner).
- **Precision landing to-dos removed** (they did not work in playtests): landing on the emperor's head (S5 and S6), the Statue Makeover drops (S6), ringing the bell from the tower (S3 and S10), riding the tram roof (S4 and S10), the Uspenski dome landing (the bun now counts when flown over the dome, no landing), Kalle's perch (The Rival), and the sunscreen on the poet's head (E1). Where a level would have been left thin: S6 now has a **Wardrobe raid** (grab the hat, shades, bouquet and scarf), The Rival has a new hidden **Hat trick** (make Kalle drop a snack three times). The sandbox finale (the ferry cabin roof), the roof-feast (any roof) and the ice walk stay.
- **The level card** moved to the right edge, narrower, without the dimming background, and lets clicks through, so the victory run stays in view. The CLEARED stamp sits above the title.

## Tests
`tests/unit/no-precision.test.ts` (no level asks for the removed things; every level still has a to-do; the dome counts in flight), outro spec checks the card is at the right edge, a browser test for the taunt in a new profile.
