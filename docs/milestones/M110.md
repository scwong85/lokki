# M110 — Real city R3 (roofs): roof colours from the aerial photo

Plan: `docs/design/realistic-city-research.md` (layer 3, "real colour"). Uses the 2025 orthophoto saved in M108 (`data/raw/ortho/`), so it needs nothing that was unreachable.

## Built
- **Sampling** (`scripts/lib/roofSample.ts`, pure): the footprint is sampled on a 0.7 m grid and only points inside it, outside any courtyard, and at least 1.5 m from every edge are kept (0.8 m, then 0.3 m, for small buildings; under 12 samples gives no colour). The edge strip is skipped because the photo is not a true orthophoto: near the edge it shows walls and shadow. The per-channel median is the roof colour, so vents, solar panels and chimney shadows do not matter.
- **Photo reader** (`scripts/lib/orthophoto.ts`): the four tiles as a lookup in game metres. This adds the small dev dependency **`jpeg-js`** (pure JavaScript, MIT), used only by the map build script.
- **Classes** (`src/world/roofColour.ts`, pure): the median becomes one of seven classes by hue, chroma and lightness, and each class has three pastel colours that a building picks from by its id.

  | Class | Rule | Buildings |
  |---|---|---|
  | pale | lightness at least 0.88 (white membrane), or grey at least 0.55 | 36 |
  | grey | grey, lightness 0.38–0.55 | 150 |
  | slate | grey, lightness 0.25–0.38 | 138 |
  | dark | grey, lightness under 0.25 (the bluish bitumen is dark grey, not blue) | 51 |
  | terracotta | red or brown, chroma at least 0.09 | 5 |
  | sand | yellow-beige, chroma at least 0.15 | 1 |
  | copper | green, hue 65–190 | 12 |

  The greys are lightened a little from the real ones so the city stays pastel.
- **Map data:** `scripts/build-world.ts` adds `r` (the class code) to each building in `data/generated/kauppatori.json`: 393 of 397 buildings (4 are too small). Everything else in the file is unchanged.
- **City builder** (`src/world/city.ts`): the roof class colours pitched roofs, flat roofs, mansard roofs and turret roofs, and wins over the era's and the district's roof colours. The hand-styled landmarks keep their roofs. `tuning.render.realRoofs`: 1 on, 0 off; it is read when the city is built, so reload to compare.

## Checked
- A debug picture (`scripts/debug-roofs.ts`) paints each footprint with its sampled colour over the photo: footprints sit exactly on the buildings, courtyards show the photo through. Alignment of the photo and the map is right.
- Tests: `tests/unit/roofColour.test.ts` (11 tests): the classifier on real samples; the greys ordered dark to pale; the sampler on a synthetic roof (edge strip skipped, courtyard left out, small building falls back, nothing if the photo does not cover it, the median ignores a red solar panel); the generated data (over 95% of buildings have a class, mostly greys with a few coloured roofs). 820 unit tests pass; lint and type check pass.
- Before and after from the same three spots (`tests/__screenshots__/real-city-*-roofs-off.png` and `-roofs-on.png`).

## What it looks like
Helsinki's roofs are mostly grey zinc and bitumen, so the city is calmer and greyer from above than the old random orange and lilac. The few real coloured roofs (green copper, red-brown, white) now stand out. If it is too grey for you, tell me: the class colours are in one table (`ROOF_COLOURS`) and `realRoofs` 0 gives the old look.

## Not done
- **Walls:** need the reality mesh. Only one 148 m tile is available and it is not the market, so wall colours are not sampled yet.
- **Real roof shapes:** CityGML was not reachable. Shapes still come from the era rules.
- **Trees over roofs:** a roof partly under a tree gets a greenish median and counts as copper; 12 buildings are copper, so a few may be false.
