# M80 — Seeing things (item visibility)

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §2. Decisions from the user: subtle by default, the scout pulse needs Hawk-eye.

## Built
- Loose items (not carried, held, broken or heavy) show a soft bobbing sparkle when the gull is near (12 m subtle, 25 m strong).
- The item an open objective asks for (`grab:<x>` / `snatch:<x>`, by prop type or quest tag) pulses brighter and shows from 40 m (70 m strong).
- **Scout pulse = Hawk-eye (H):** while it is on, every loose item sparkles from 60 m (90 m strong). No new key.
- Options: "Highlight items" off / subtle (default) / strong, saved with the other options.
- Pure rules in `src/gameplay/itemGlow.ts` (tests in `tests/unit/itemGlow.test.ts`); a 24-sprite pool in `src/render/itemGlow.ts`.

## Not done (left for later)
- Text labels over wanted items, outline on the low preset, bigger quest props: judge from the playtest whether the sparkle is enough.

## Exit criteria
- Items are easy to spot in a level without the sparkle looking noisy; Options can turn it off; Hawk-eye lights the whole scene.
