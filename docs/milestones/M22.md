# M22 — Senate Square, part 2 (levels S4–S6)

Branch `m22-senate-b`. Design: `docs/design/levels-senaatintori.md`.

## Built
- **Trams**: `src/gameplay/tram.ts` (pure logic: runs back and forth, dwells at the stop, rings its bell, halts while something blocks the track) + `TramView`. The roof is a moving platform (`World.movers`): you can land on it and are carried along. Tuning group `tram`.
- **Pigeon Gang**: `src/gameplay/pigeons.ts` (guarding / friends / scattered) + `PigeonView`. They shove lone gulls away from the statue; a bun makes friends, salmiakki scatters them; a disguised gull is ignored. Tuning group `pigeons`.
- New props: sunglasses, scarf.
- **S4 Tram Surfing** (Lunch Express: stop the tram with a bottle, the driver leaves his lunch; ride the roof; hidden: board twice), **S5 Turf War** (Statue Diplomacy: bun or salmiakki, then the emperor's head; hidden: win them over and then scatter them), **S6 The Emperor's New Look** (Statue Makeover: hat, sunglasses, bouquet, scarf in big snap zones; splat a worker; hidden: perch on the finished statue).

## Known limits
- The tram and the gang are simulated on the host/single player only; online guests do not see them move (not synced yet).
- The Photobomb and Pigeon Diplomacy skills from the design doc are not built yet; S4/S5 reward existing skills (Flock call, Disguise).
