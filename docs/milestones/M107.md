# M107 — Stage 1.2 The Gull Picnic (the first ten-minute stage)

Design: `docs/design/stages-10min.md` (section 6). This milestone tests whether a ten-minute collection stage holds up in play (**decision gate** for the other 19 stages).

## What it is
Stage 1.2 is no longer the three-chain Fish Market. It is **The Gull Picnic**: throw the flock a picnic on the roof of the little market hall (the new `KAUPPAHALLI` pavilion on the open paving south of the tents). Everything on the menu belongs to somebody down below.

**The note** has a headline, *The Gull Picnic 0 / 6*, with six rows:
- A row is `???` until you come near its place.
- Nearer, it shows a half title: `??? …something fishy`.
- Right there, it shows the full wish: *Get the Fisherman's catch.*
- When the item lands on the blanket, the row is ticked with the item and its owner.
- The hint ladder (J or the "?") works on the row you are at.

**The six rows.** Each owner has a trait, and each row is solved with a different trick.

| Row | Owner | Trait | Trick |
|---|---|---|---|
| A tablecloth | the Waiter | Every 20 s he counts 3, 2, 1 and tosses the cloth up with a flourish. You cannot snatch it from his hand. | Grab it while it is in the air, or in the second it lies on the ground. |
| Rye bread | the Baker | She never leaves her loaf, but she hurries to the door bell. The bell is out of beak's reach. | Throw (Q) or drop something on the bell, or splat it. |
| Something fishy | the Fisherman | Any splash by his red float is a bite: he reels in, facing the sea, for 7 s. | Poop in the water by the float. The second time he reels in **an old boot**. |
| Coffee | the Captain | His thermos stands by the bollard and he watches it, except when a ferry comes in (about every 85 s here). Then he hurries to the gangway to greet it. | Wait for the horn. |
| Something sweet | the two Berry Sellers | They stand back to back and see nothing between them. | Walk in from the side. If one finds the punnet gone, they blame each other: "YOU ate them!" / "I did NOT!" |
| The main course | the Nervous Diner | His fried vendace cannot be snatched from his hand. | Honk behind him: he jumps and drops it. |

**Guest gulls** land on the roof one per row, so the picnic fills up as you go.

**Midpoint, "Uninvited guests":** at three rows the Pigeon Gang flies up and mobs any gull near the blanket.
- A honk on the roof scatters them, or a cinnamon bun wins them over.
- Either way they come back as polite guests.
- They never take anything away. This is the gentle version of open question 3.

**Finale, "Dinner is served":** once all six rows are done and the raid is seen off, peck the gong on the roof corner.
- The guests tuck in.
- Every victim walks over and shouts up at the roof together: *"MY CLOTH!" "MY LOAF!" "MY HERRING!" "MY COFFEE!"* Up on the roof: *"Caw."*
- The stage is cleared, and the reward is **Fat bird**.

**Side orders** (on the note under their own heading, one line each):
- **The Clean Freak** has moved to the west end of the tents.
- **Honk!** is three tourists on the east quay.
- **Bottle Service** is behind the tents.

The side orders count for the second star, not for clearing.

**Secrets:**
- **Granny's good scarf as a napkin.** She dozes off every 30 s.
- **The old boot** from the second false bite. *"The guests take turns drinking from the boot."*

**Things that are not on the menu** (a ball, a map, sunglasses, flowers, salmiakki, buns, sweets, a teddy, bottles) get a guest comment when put on the blanket: *"A map. Now the pigeons know where we live."*

**Co-op:**
- **Pass it on:** pass something in mid-air.
- **Two seats at the table:** both gulls sit on the roof after the gong.

**The old Fish Market** stays playable in the Playground as *Fish Market (the old 1.2)*, with stage 1.2's mechanics.

## Built (engine)
- **Collection to-dos:**
  - `QuestDef.collect` (the goal, plus a marker on the delivery spot).
  - `ObjectiveDef.row` (its reveal spot, item and owner).
  - `RowScout` in `src/gameplay/collection.ts` tracks what has been seen and what is known (both stick) and which row is in focus.
  - The note renders the rows; `ladderSteps` follows the focused row.
- **Side orders:** `LevelDef.side`. They are needed for the second star, not for clearing.
- **Locked to-do text:** `QuestDef.lockedText`.
- **The flourish:** `Ritual.toss`.
  - The person tosses what they hold straight up at the start of the ritual and catches it at the end if it is within `npc.flourishCatch` (3 m).
  - It happens even when they are on guard.
  - A gull that caught it causes `flourishLost:<role>`.
- **Hit-only switches** (`hitOnly`: only a thrown thing, a dropped thing or a splat sets them off, needing toss or poop rather than peck), `free` switches (no mechanic needed), and switches at a height (`y`).
- **Reactions:**
  - `spawn` (a prop turns up: the boot).
  - `send.stay` (the people sent stay there for good and go now, even mid-chase).
- **Timetable:**
  - The ferry's arrival is a quest event, `ferryIn`.
  - A level can set `ferryGap`.
- **Decor:** `hall` (a solid pavilion whose flat roof is a perch and holds things), `stall` (fruit stall), `float`, and any decor at a height (`y`).
- **Picnic:**
  - `LevelDef.picnic`; `PicnicGuests` in `src/world/picnicGuests.ts` are gull models that glide in and sit round the blanket.
  - The Pigeon Gang can sit on a roof (`GangDef.y`, `ring`).
  - A `picnic` tuning group (guest flight time, blanket zone, raid ring, raid honk range).
- **New items:** tablecloth, rye loaf, thermos, old boot, cone of fried vendace.

## Known issues
- **Co-op guests** (online) do not see the guest gulls or the raid on their screen: the picnic runs on the host. The quest state still syncs.
- **Gathering at the finale:** people walk in straight lines, so the victims' gathering spots are placed on clear walks (a unit test checks this). A victim stuck somewhere else (chasing, panicking) arrives late.
- **Hard to tell from tests:** the waiter's flourish timing (catchable for about 1.3 s in the air and about 1 s on the ground) and the ferry gap (85 s) need playtesting.

## Playtest checklist
- [ ] Does it take at least 10 minutes the first time? (Par time is 9 minutes.)
- [ ] Do the half titles make you want to go and look?
- [ ] Is each row's trick readable from the nudge, or does it need the marker or the full hint?
- [ ] The waiter's flourish: catchable, and funny when he bows to an empty table?
- [ ] The door bell: does throwing (Q) at it feel natural? Is a splat on it too easy?
- [ ] The fisherman: is the boot gag good? Is poop drift by the float fair?
- [ ] The captain: is waiting for the ferry too long?
- [ ] The berry sellers: is walking in between them readable with the red rings?
- [ ] The pigeon raid: pressure or annoyance?
- [ ] The finale: do the victims shouting at the roof land as a punchline?
- [ ] Side orders and secrets: found? Too much on the note?
