# M126 — Latch steps: earlier progress counts (1.5 playtest)

Playtest: in 1.5 the boss chain ("Wait for the Warden's lunch", "Take his sandwich") stayed open although the player had already taken the sandwich while the boss quest was locked.
- `ObjectiveDef.latch`: if the step's event already happened (locked quest, or an earlier step still open), the step completes the moment it opens and the chain moves on.
- Used for the Warden's lunch and sandwich. Dodge, hunt and poop stay live-only.
- Tests: `tests/unit/latch.test.ts`.
