# M24 — Esplanadi, part 1 (levels E1–E3)

Branch `m24-esplanadi-a`. Design: `docs/design/levels-esplanadi.md`. The park runs west from Havis Amanda along z = -65.

## Built
- **Sunbathers** (`src/gameplay/sunbathers.ts`): they wake when your **shadow** crosses their face. The shadow lies north-west of you (sun from the south-east), longer the higher you fly, and fades above ~22 m. Woken ones sit up, swat you away from the towel, then lie down again. Tuning group `sun`.
- **Dogs** (`src/gameplay/dogs.ts`): chase gulls on the ground (never in the air), fetch loose balls and hats straight across whatever is in the way (picnic food flies), bring them back to the owner. Tuning group `dog`.
- **Sprinklers** (`src/gameplay/sprinklers.ts`): stand on the valve box and the lawn sprays for a while; people flee and the cake is left. Tuning group `sprinkler`.
- **Cloud Glide** (new skill, reward of E1): gliding high, your shadow shrinks to almost nothing.
- Landmarks: the Poet statue, two rows of lime trees (canopies are perches). Props: strawberries, ball, sausage, sunscreen.
- **E1 Don't Block My Sun** (Strawberry Raid; sunscreen on the poet's nose; hidden: wake all four at once), **E2 Who's a Good Boy?** (Fetch!: send the dog through the picnic and take a sausage; fetch a hat; hidden: three fetches), **E3 Splash Zone** (Clear the Lawn: start the sprinklers, take the cake; soak three; hidden: soak six).

## Known limits
- Sunbathers, dogs and sprinklers are simulated on the host/single player only (not synced to online guests yet).
- Treetop hiding (heat drops in canopies), the beat, queues, Kalle and Air Snatch come in later Esplanadi milestones.
