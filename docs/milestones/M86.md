# M86 — Pigeons that are already friends still take the bun

Playtest: "pigeon still doesn't accept the bun" (stage 2.5, after M85).

## Cause
Pigeon Diplomacy (the skill won in the Senate Square) makes the gang friends from the start of every level. A bun is only accepted from a gang that is *not* friends yet, so for anyone who already had the skill the bun did nothing, and the step "Win the Pigeon Gang over with a bun" (event `pigeonsFriends`) could never be ticked. M85's bun supply fix was real but not this player's problem.

## Fix
- A gang that is already friends takes a bun dropped by the statue too (new gang event `fed`): the bun is eaten, hearts show, and `pigeonsFriends` fires, so the step ticks. Salmiakki still scatters them.
- Tests: unit (friends take a bun, far buns are ignored) and browser (a2s5 with the skill: `gang.mode` is friends from the start, a bun by the statue ticks the step).
