# M13 — Market Square Act 1: levels 1–4

## Goal
The first four real levels from `docs/design/levels-kauppatori.md`, each about 5 minutes, single player, each teaching one mechanic with a headline puzzle, plus a landing assist (from the M12 playtest).

## How to play them
Title screen → pick a level (or `?level=<id>` in the address). Levels unlock in order: clear one (its main to-dos) to open the next. Free play is always open. Stars: ★ cleared · ★ every to-do incl. the secret `???` one · ★ under the par time. Skills come from clearing a level.

## Built
- **Landing assist** (`landing.magnet*` in tuning): a gull gliding slowly down toward a perch top within reach is turned toward a spot just inside its edge and flares near it, so it settles instead of overshooting. Off while flapping, diving or fast. The statue's top perch is also wider. Pure steering in `src/gull/flight.ts`, nearest-perch query in `src/world/collision.ts`, tested.
- **Selfie routine** (`src/gameplay/selfie.ts`, tuning `selfie.*`): a clock-driven idle / countdown / open cycle shared by the selfie tourist and the blogger; the "open" phase uses the existing distraction mechanism, so guarded rings, grabs and dives work unchanged. `inFrame` decides whether a gull is in the photo (a cone in front of her camera).
- **The Clean Freak** (`role: 'cleaner'`, tuning `cleaner.*`), a scrub timer that grows with each splat; the **brass sign** and **harbour buoy** props (the buoy floats).
- **Sight and sound**: `snatchUnseen` (calm and not seeing the gull), `distract` (a noise makes a tourist holding food look away), `doubleSnatch`.
- **Blogger** and the **raw herring** / **salmon soup** props: a herring left near her is picked up, eaten and reviewed (`review` event, her "yuck").
- **Level data**: extra tourists and props per level (`extraTourists`, `extraProps`), `requires` for the unlock chain, a `carryLanded` trigger kind, level-locked entries on the title screen.
- Tests: `tests/unit/landing-assist.test.ts`, `tests/unit/levels.test.ts` (selfie phases and frame, unlocking, unseen snatches), `tests/unit/npc-manager.test.ts` (Clean Freak, selfie click, blogger), `tests/smoke/levels.spec.ts` (every level: load, selfie countdown and photobomb, sign and buoy, noise distraction, the herring swap).

## After playtest 1 (fixes)
Feedback: clearing a level did not lead to the next one; stealing the fish did not complete the to-do; hitting the buoy did not complete the to-do.
- **Next level:** the level-complete card now says "<Level> cleared!" and offers **Next: <level> →** (Enter also goes on) and **Level list**; the next level starts straight away (no title screen). After the last level it says more are on the way.
- **The fish:** the to-do now counts any snatch of his fish after the sign was splatted (before, it only counted while his scrub timer was still running, which is short). He scrubs longer too (8 s, +3 s per extra splat, up to 18 s).
- **The buoy:** a much bigger target (about 2.5 m across, visibly bigger too), and the poop aim assist now steers drops toward the buoy and the brass sign, like it does toward people, even in a crosswind.

## After playtest 2 (fixes)
Feedback: the buoy still didn't clear; picking a level reloaded into the level dialog again; wanted a way to replay unlocked levels.
- **Buoy**: a real fly-by only counted inside a narrow release window (about +-4 m), which felt like it never registered. Level targets (the buoy, the brass sign) now pull drops in from up to 12 m away (`poop.targetAssistRadius`), all the way, and the landing ring on the ground includes that assist, so what you aim at is what you get.
- **No second dialog**: picking a level (title screen, level list, or the Next button) starts it straight away (`?play` skips the title).
- **Level list** (new): press **L**, or Options > "Choose a level...", or the phone menu, or "Level list" on the level-complete card. Shows every level with its stars and best time; unlocked levels can be replayed from the start; locked ones say what to clear first. It pauses the game.

## Notes and deviations
- **Forgiveness** (design rule): the selfie window is two seconds with a visible countdown; the sign is a 1.5 m target; the blogger's herring only has to land within two metres of her; nothing needs frame-tight timing.
- A level's quests are all **untimed** (calm cities, no heat meter); the timed ice-cream quest and the arms race arrive in level 5.
- The city's own props (the cap on the statue, buns on counters) are still present in every level; they're harmless but unused. A later pass can hide props a level doesn't need.
- The level select is still the list on the title screen (an illustrated postcard is for a later polish pass).

## Exit criteria
- [ ] Each level is distinct and takes about 5 minutes
- [ ] The headline puzzles are understandable without the hints
- [ ] You can finish all four without frustration (landing on the statue and the rooftop feel fair now)
- [ ] Free play still plays as before
