# M6 — Heat and deterrents

## Goal
The city fights back in an arms race, and the difficulty ramp feels fair and funny, not frustrating.

## Built
- **Heat** (`src/gameplay/heat.ts`, pure, tested): 0-100, rises with mischief (a tourist spotting you +4, snatch +25, poop on a tourist +15, breaking something +8, tipping a bin +8, a clatter +2), falls only while **nobody can see you** (after a short delay). Four tier thresholds (20 / 40 / 60 / 80) in the tuning panel. A small meter at the top of the screen shows the heat, the tier marks, what the city has deployed and what the gulls have learned.
- **The arms race, in order** (`src/gameplay/defense.ts`):
  1. **Vendors with spray bottles** (tier 1): three vendors behind the south row of stalls. They wake up when the city is on alert (or you come close), turn to you, **aim, then fire**: the windup (0.6 s) is your chance to dodge. A hit stuns you, drains stamina, pushes you back and makes you drop what you carry. A poop on a vendor puts them out of action for a few seconds.
  2. **Scare lines** (tier 2): thin lines strung over the stalls (the real Helsinki measure). Flying into one tangles you (stun, stamina, dropped item). They are put up visibly over a couple of seconds and are harmless until finished.
  3. **Nets** (tier 3): a net over the stalls catches gulls coming down from above, but the aisles are open at the sides: gulls can fly in **under** it.
  4. **The Gull Warden** (tier 4, invented character): hi-vis vest, cap, umbrella. Patrols the square; when he sees you he alerts, hunts you, and **swats** you with the umbrella when you are close and low (windup 0.5 s, dodgeable). **While his umbrella is up it blocks a poop from above** ("Plink!"); it goes down when he is **distracted by a noise**, mid-swat, or sulking. A poop on him humiliates him (he sulks for 5 s, the heat drops by 20).
- **The city learns / the gulls learn** (`src/gameplay/deterrents.ts`, pure, tested): each deterrent counts your *successful bypasses* (dodged sprays, clean passes through the lines, flights under the nets, dodged swats). It gets weaker as the gulls learn it, and once learned (4 / 8 / 3 / 5 bypasses) it barely works and **the city brings in the next deterrent immediately**, whatever the heat. Toasts say what happened ("The gulls have learned to beat the nets!"). Deterrents stay up until you press R: the city does not forget, even when the heat cools down.
- **Hawk-eye hint** (`H`): briefly lights up lines and nets in orange (4 s, 8 s cooldown, shown in the meter). The lines also glint now and then. The real Hawk-eye skill comes in M8.
- New extras objective: **Poop on the Gull Warden**. New placeholder sounds: hiss, whistle, twang, alarm.
- **Performance:** every prop and person is now one merged mesh for its static parts (vertex colours): the worst view (market, everyone out, all deterrents up) is ~120 draw calls instead of ~200.
- All numbers are in the tuning panel (`heat.*`, `deter.*`).

## After playtest 1 (fixes)
Feedback: could not tell when I was sprayed; dodged but did not know how, and what does "learned" do; the arms race was not obvious because the nets only covered the stalls where no tourists are; hard to poop on the Warden (how do I make a noise? the umbrella keeps blocking); bottles hard to pick up; fps fine.
- **Spray / swat readability:** while a vendor aims, a **red fan on the ground** shows exactly where the spray will land (the Warden gets a red circle where his swat lands). Dodge by leaving it before it fires. Hits now flash the screen (blue spray, orange swat, white line/net), the spray is a bigger water jet, and the toast says what hit you ("Sprayed by a vendor! Dropped the bottle!").
- **"Dodged!" + what learning is:** every clean escape shows a toast with the count ("Dodged the spray! (gulls learning 2/4)") and the first time explains: *every clean dodge teaches the gulls; a learned deterrent barely hurts any more, but the city brings in something new.* The heat meter lists each deployed deterrent with its progress (`spray bottles 2/4`, then struck through as `learned`).
- **The arms race now reaches the tourists:** the scare lines and the net cover the **square in front of the stalls too** (an extra line over the tourists, net over the whole area), and two more vendors stand at the sides of the square. Diving on a tourist from above now runs into the net at heat 60+; the quiet way is under it.
- **Making a noise / the Warden:** anything you **drop or throw hard** now makes a thump that people hear (bottles and bins are louder), so tossing any prop away from the Warden pulls his attention (`physics.thumpSpeed`). He shows a blue **?** and tips his umbrella aside while he is distracted (also while he swats). The aim reticle turns **red** and a prompt says "Umbrella blocks it! Distract the Warden..." when your poop would hit the umbrella. The poop aim assist now pulls toward vendors and the Warden's body (never the umbrella). The to-do tip for the Warden explains all this.
- **Picking things up:** props within ~1.4 m of the beak or body are grabbed whichever way you face, the reach counts to the edge of the prop (small bottles were the problem), range 2.2 m and cone 120°. A **white ring** now shows which prop `E` will grab. Spray range 7 → 9 m so vendors do not need you right next to them.

## After playtest 2 (fixes)
Feedback: spray readable now; arms race clear but wants a notice/announcement; still hard to poop on the Warden (he only swats when the gull is low and near, the net above stops the gull flying high enough, the umbrella comes back up too fast); bottles fine.
- **Announcements:** each escalation shows a big banner ("CITY ALERT: Nets going up!" with a one-line tip), with an alarm; each learned deterrent ("The gulls learned: spray bottles") and each counter-measure the city brings in because of it ("The city adapts: ...") too. The heat meter now also says what comes next ("Next at heat 40: scare lines").
- **Net:** only catches gulls **coming down** into it (diving or falling); you can climb out through it. It also hangs higher (4 m, lines at 3.2 m) so there is more room underneath.
- **Warden window:** a noise now holds him for 5 s (was 2.5 s) and his umbrella stays down for 1.5 s **after** he recovers (so about 6.5 s to poop him). Poop aim assist radius 3 → 4 m. The umbrella is also still down while he swats and sulks. (`deter.wardenDistract`, `deter.umbrellaDelay`, `poop.assistRadius`.)
- Debug handle `window.__lokki` also exposes `noise()` and `heat()` for testing.

- (Later, in M7 playtesting) The net hangs at 5 m and dives need only 1.6 m clearance, so you can still dive from under it.

## Notes
- The Warden's hit/dodge, the vendor spray and the line/net hits are covered by unit tests and a browser test that escalates the city and checks the loop stays alive. I could not feel the balance myself.
- Escalation targets the **market stalls** only (that is where the deterrents are); tourists keep behaving as in M5.
- Vendors spray only gulls lower than 4.5 m inside the cone; flying high is safe. The Warden only swats low gulls (< 2.8 m).
- Not in M6: spikes, the fake owl kite and the gull-hunting drone (backlog / later), real Hawk-eye skill (M8).

## Exit criteria (for the user)
- [x] The ramp feels fair and funny, not frustrating: you can see what is coming and why you got hit
- [x] You can dodge a spray (watch the vendor aim) and a Warden swat
- [x] The arms race is understandable: heat meter, deployed list, "learned" toasts
- [x] You can poop on the Warden (distract him with a noise first, so the umbrella goes down)
- [x] fps still fine with everything out
