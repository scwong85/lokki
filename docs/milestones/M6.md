# M6 — Heat and deterrents

## Goal
The city fights back in an arms race, and the difficulty ramp feels fair and funny, not frustrating.

## Built
- **Heat** (`src/gameplay/heat.ts`, pure, tested): 0-100, rises with mischief (a tourist spotting you +4, snatch +25, poop on a tourist +15, breaking something +8, tipping a bin +8, a clatter +2), falls only while **nobody can see you** (after a short delay). Four tier thresholds (20 / 40 / 60 / 80) in the tuning panel. A small meter at the top of the screen shows the heat, the tier marks, what the city has deployed and what the gulls have learned.
- **The arms race, in order** (`src/gameplay/defense.ts`):
  1. **Vendors with spray bottles** (tier 1): three vendors behind the south row of stalls. They wake up when the city is on alert (or you come close), turn to you, **aim, then fire**: the windup (0.6 s) is your chance to dodge. A hit stuns you, drains stamina, pushes you back and makes you drop what you carry. A poop on a vendor puts them out of action for a few seconds.
  2. **Scare lines** (tier 2): thin lines strung over the stalls (the real Helsinki measure). Flying into one tangles you (stun, stamina, dropped item). They are put up visibly over a couple of seconds and are harmless until finished.
  3. **Nets** (tier 3): a net over the stalls catches gulls coming down from above, but the aisles are open at the sides: gulls can fly in **under** it.
  4. **The Gull Warden** (tier 4, invented character): hi-vis vest, cap, umbrella. Patrols the square; when he sees you he alerts, hunts you, and **swats** you with the umbrella when you are close and low (windup 0.5 s, dodgeable). **While his umbrella is up it blocks a poop from above** ("Plink!"); it goes down when he is **distracted by a noise**, mid-swat, or sulking. A poop on him humiliates him (he sulks for 5 s, the heat drops by 20).
- **The city learns / the gulls learn** (`src/gameplay/deterrents.ts`, pure, tested): each deterrent counts your *successful bypasses* (dodged sprays, clean passes through the lines, flights under the nets, dodged swats). It gets weaker as the gulls learn it, and once learned (4 / 8 / 3 / 5 bypasses) it barely works and **the city brings in the next deterrent immediately**, whatever the heat. Toasts say what happened ("The gulls have learned to beat the nets!"). Deterrents stay up until you press R: the city does not forget, even when the heat cools down.
- **Hawk-eye hint** (`H`): briefly lights up lines and nets in orange (4 s, 8 s cooldown, shown in the meter). The lines also glint now and then. The real Hawk-eye skill comes in M8.
- New extras objective: **Poop on the Gull Warden**. New placeholder sounds: hiss, whistle, twang, alarm.
- **Performance:** every prop and person is now one merged mesh for its static parts (vertex colours): the worst view (market, everyone out, all deterrents up) is ~120 draw calls instead of ~200.
- All numbers are in the tuning panel (`heat.*`, `deter.*`).

## Notes
- The Warden's hit/dodge, the vendor spray and the line/net hits are covered by unit tests and a browser test that escalates the city and checks the loop stays alive. I could not feel the balance myself.
- Escalation targets the **market stalls** only (that is where the deterrents are); tourists keep behaving as in M5.
- Vendors spray only gulls lower than 4.5 m inside the cone; flying high is safe. The Warden only swats low gulls (< 2.8 m).
- Not in M6: spikes, the fake owl kite and the gull-hunting drone (backlog / later), real Hawk-eye skill (M8).

## Exit criteria (for the user)
- [ ] The ramp feels fair and funny, not frustrating: you can see what is coming and why you got hit
- [ ] You can dodge a spray (watch the vendor aim) and a Warden swat
- [ ] The arms race is understandable: heat meter, deployed list, "learned" toasts
- [ ] You can poop on the Warden (distract him with a noise first, so the umbrella goes down)
- [ ] fps still fine with everything out
