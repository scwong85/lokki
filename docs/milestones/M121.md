# M121 — Resume a stage, the "show me" flyover, and the playtest report

Picked from the M120 list of improvements (1, 2 and 7).

- **Resume a stage:** a collection stage saves its run (steps done, things on the roof, courses served) after every row and course, in the browser (`lokki-resume-v1`, one run at a time, kept 14 days). Loading the stage again offers **Continue** ("You left off with 2 things on the roof") or **Start over**. Continuing puts the things back on the roof, the guest gulls back, and replays the course beats (the ferry crowd, the Market Guard). R (restart) and clearing the stage forget the run. The clock starts fresh, so a resumed run does not get the par-time star unfairly. Not for an online guest or in co-op. (`src/gameplay/resume.ts`)
- **"Show me" flyover:** when a course opens, the camera sweeps over each of the new course's places (2.5 s each, `tuning.flyover`) with a "???" marker on the spot; the gull waits where it was; any key (Space, Enter, Esc, E, F, Q, Shift) skips it. `perSpot` 0 turns it off. (`src/gameplay/flyover.ts`)
- **Playtest report:** Options > *Copy playtest report*, and the same button on the results card, copy one block of text: the stage, time played, rows done, time per row, the longest stretch without finding or serving a row (and where), dive near-misses (and how many the body blocked), setbacks (Guard, spray, fly-backs), help used (compass, hints asked, flyovers skipped), and the options. Paste it into the chat after a playtest. (`src/gameplay/report.ts`)

Tests: `tests/unit/resumeFlyoverReport.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/resume.spec.ts` (two rows, the flyover, the report, reload and Continue, the ferry crowd back, play on, R forgets).

## Playtest checklist
- [ ] Close the tab mid-stage, open it again: is Continue offered, and is everything where you left it?
- [ ] Is the flyover helpful, or too long? (`flyover.perSpot`)
- [ ] After a playtest, paste the report into the chat.
