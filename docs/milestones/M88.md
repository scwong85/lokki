# M88 — Map sabotage: a hint for the last step, and a follow-up that always happens

Playtest: "map sabotage does not have hint, and sometimes cannot trigger follow up after dropping map to the water".

## Fixes
- **Hint** on the last step ("Watch them head for the wrong ferry"): it said nothing; now it says to wait and keep clear.
- **The victim is always found.** `mapInSea` fires once, so if the game did not know whose map it was (it left the hand by a tug, a bump or a slam, not a recorded snatch) nobody was sent and the step could never finish. Now the fallback is whoever held the item first.
- **Nobody gets stuck on the way.** A person sent somewhere (the wrong ferry, a bell, a lure) who stops getting closer for 6 s while within 14 m now counts as arrived, so a wall or stall at the quay cannot block `wrongFerry`. New tuning: `npc.stuckSeconds`, `npc.stuckRange`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/victim.test.ts`.
