# M105 — The sea under the paving, and the campaign joke-first

## 1. Water fix
People and the gull on the open paving south of the market tents (where the trial puzzles are) looked as if they stood in the sea from a low camera. The land was there (all land sample points are covered by the land mesh; hiding the sea showed the paving underneath), but the sea plane won the depth test. The sea is now a few centimetres lower and pushed back in depth (`polygonOffset`), and drawn first. Checked from the same low camera: paving where it should be, the sea beyond the quay edge with its foam line.

## 2. The campaign stages, joke-first
From `docs/design/hooky-objectives-research.md` (c), row 9.
- **Every campaign chain names its victim** (`src/data/levels/victims.ts`, applied to Acts 1 and 2): the punchline photo (M104) now frames them on the stages too. The Warden and the pigeons are left without one.
- **No chain ends on a silent grab any more:** 15 chains ended on a step nobody reacted to (a cone taken, a bun taken, a photo). Each final step now has a line and a reaction from the victim and the people around them (anger, laugh, facepalm). Examples: *"The peeker: "The BUSH took my ice cream!""*, *"He bought it to feel better. Now he feels worse."*, *"Three photos, three gulls. The album is ruined."*, *"The band follows the gull. The conductor follows the band."*
- **Reactions can centre on a role** (`witness.role`): the victim, wherever they are, and everybody near them react.
- **The quality rule is stricter:** the player's own actions (snatch, peck, pose) no longer count as "the world reacting" (`REACTION_EVENT`). The three chains that relied on them got real reactions: the selfie tourist on her ice cream, the queue on the alarm and on the sprinkler, the guards saluting a very small captain.
- New test `tests/unit/stageJokes.test.ts`: every stage chain's victims are in its cast, and its last step gets a reaction you can see.
