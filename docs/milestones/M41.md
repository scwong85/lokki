# M41 — Graphics G6: polaroids, stamp and a grouped level list

Branch `m41-ui-polish`. Plan: `docs/design/graphics-refresh.md` (G6) and `docs/design/fun-todos.md` (V2, first part).

## Built
- **Polaroids** (`src/ui/polaroids.ts`): every finished to-do asks for a photo; it is taken from the game canvas right after the next frame, framed in white with the to-do text as a hand-written caption, and pops up bottom right for a moment. One per second at most; the album keeps the newest 12 and resets with each level.
- **Level-complete card**: a red **CLEARED** stamp slams down, and three polaroids (first, middle, last of the run) drop onto the card. Click one to save it as a jpeg (works on phones too).
- **Level list**: grouped by district (Kauppatori, Senate Square, Esplanadi, Free play) with a "3/10 cleared" count per district.
- Pure helpers (`limitAlbum`, `fitCaption`, `pickShowcase`) are unit-tested; a browser test checks a real picture is captured and the list has district headings.

## Not done
The illustrated map with a gull flying between levels, an album screen outside the level card, native phone Share sheet, polaroids at Havoc chain peaks, title screen with the live city. The level-complete card was not screenshotted (only the list and the pop-up were).
