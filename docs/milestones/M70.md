## Built
- **2.1 Steps** (Senaatintori, morning), opened by the end of Act 1. Chains with joke cards, reactions and no padding: *Leipäjuusto Downhill* (take the family's cheese, roll it into the hot-dog cart: the vendor yells, the queue laughs), *Cold Fish* (take a fried fish, put it on the walker's route, he slips, take his bun), *Hot-dog Hazard* (splat the vendor, he storms off to wash and is oblivious to you, take a hot dog from the empty cart). Co-op (required): Relay race and a two-pass Stairway relay.
- **2.2 Say Pigeon!** introduces **Pose** (the emote keys 1 to 4: wave, laugh, point, facepalm; a pose counts in a photo for 3.5 s). Chains: *Class of 2026* (pose in three photos, each a different pose), *Cap toss* (take a graduate's cap, he chases you, fly through the next photo), *Camera shy* (splat a photographer, the class scatters). Co-op (required): Double bomb and Duo pose.
- **Photo events:** the click now says which gulls are in the picture (`selfieClick.who`); a pose in the picture fires `pose:any`, `pose:<kind>` and `pose:new` (different from the last photo's), and `coop:pose` for two posers. A polaroid captions it.
- **Gate:** `pose` is introduced at 2.2 and `disguise` moves to 2.4 (the captain's cap), as in the Act 2 plan.
- The playtest card keys are now 7, 8, 9 (1 to 4 are poses and emotes).
- Decisions taken from the plan (the user did not answer them; reversible): five stages in the plan's order, skills only from 2.3, Pose as a gated verb built on the existing emotes.
