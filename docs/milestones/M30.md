# M30 — Roads (W3 start) and playtest fixes

Branch `m30-roads-fixes`. From the M28/M29 playtest: "Thermal Express cannot clear (cannot land on the Uspenski dome)", "the bike is going in reverse", "still no roads". (Roofs, sky, shopfronts and turning were fine.)

## Fixes
- **Thermal Express (level 14)**: the real cause was that the level's dome position was about 20 m away from the actual Uspenski onion, so the landing zone never covered the dome and the roof there is only 18 m high (the bun must land above 20 m). The dome is now at the real position (`DOME` in `act2.ts`), and a unit test checks it against the building footprint so it cannot drift again. On top of that, the central onion now has a wide gold **collar** (a `perch` ledge about 2.5 m wide, at about 27.6 m) round its neck to land on; the old top ledges were only about half a metre wide.
- **Bike going backwards**: the cyclist model's front is its -z side but it was turned by an extra half turn. Fixed, with a unit test.

## Roads
- Street areas are now drawn as roads (`src/world/roads.ts`): a **pavement** along both edges, a **kerb line**, **dark asphalt** inside, and a **dashed centre line** on straight wide streets. Squares and quays (names ending in "tori" or containing "laituri") keep their cobbles, and areas wider than 34 m stay paved. About three quarters of the real street areas turn into roads; corners that will not inset cleanly keep the plain street colour.
- One extra mesh (about +1 draw call). Market view on high: 166 calls and 243k triangles (was 159 and 241k); low: 113 and 171k.
- The street dressing (lamps, benches, vans) is unchanged.
