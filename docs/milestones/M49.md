# M49 — Honk, Cannonball and the sound of the city

Branch `m49-honk-splash` (stacked on `m48-mischief`). Study: `docs/design/mischief-mechanics.md` (rounds 2 and 3).

## Fixed: environment sounds ("I cannot hear the tram or the church bell")
The church bell and the tram bell were both a tiny 880 Hz chime at one fixed volume, and the tram rumble, the sea and the city had no sound at all. Now:
- **Church bell**: a real low strike with inharmonic partials that ring on for seconds; heard across the whole square (never below 40 % volume), louder near the tower.
- **Tram bell**: ding-ding, fading with distance up to 160 m (it rang only within 70 m before, as the same blip).
- **Ferry horn**: two low notes when the finale starts.
- **Ambient layers**: a tram rumble (louder when it is moving and near), the sea (when you are near water), and a city murmur (when people are around, quieter up high). New option-style knob `audio.ambient` (F3 panel).
- Distance maths is `src/core/spatial.ts` (tested). `audio.log` keeps the last sounds for tests.

## Built: Honk (verb) and Cannonball (verb)
- **Honk**: a quick tap of T (hold is still the taunt, which now starts after 0.25 s of holding). Calm people within `honk.range` (14 m) jump; **someone with their back to you drops what they hold**, someone who was looking only flinches; sleepers wake; pigeons fly. Cooldown 1.5 s; works on touch (the Taunt button) and gamepad (R3). Pure `TapHold` and `honkAt` are unit tested.
  - Level **M3. Honk!** (level list, "Mischief (new)"): honk at two tourists who face the sea, take what falls, get away.
- **Cannonball**: a splashdown faster than `cannon.minImpact` (9 m/s) is a cannonball: a big splash and a wave that soaks everyone within 3 to 8 m, by speed (`cannon.*`); they drop what they hold and run.
  - Level **M4. Splash Landing**: dive into the harbour beside the pier, soak three at once, take a dropped ice cream, get away.
- Tests: 9 new unit tests (honk, tap/hold, distance gain) and three browser tests (a honk chain, a real hold-Shift dive that soaks the pier, the sounds).

## Tug (designed, not built)
See section 12 of the study: items say whether they can be snatched from the air, tugged on the ground, or both; a tug can be interrupted (a vendor's spray makes you lose it).

## Not done
Honk and Cannonball are not yet in co-op for the guest (the host's gull honks; a guest's honk plays locally only), dogs do not bark at a honk, and the stall-chain levels are not converted to the new toys yet.
