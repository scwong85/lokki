# M14 — Online co-op

## Goal
Two players on two devices play the M13 levels (and free play) together. No account, no game server.

## How to play it
1. **Host**: press **N** (or Options → "Play with a friend online…", or the title screen button) → **Host a game**. After a moment an **invite code** appears. Send it to your friend (any chat).
2. **Guest**: press **N** → **Join a game**, paste the invite, press **Make my answer code**, send the answer code back.
3. **Host**: paste the answer into the second box → **Connect**. The game starts on both screens within a few seconds.
- Works on the same PC (two browser windows) for testing, over the internet, and on phones (the touch controls are unchanged).
- **The host picks the levels** (L, level-complete card, R to restart); the guest's game follows automatically, so nobody has to rejoin. Progress and stars are saved on the host's side only; skills and gull looks stay with each player's own save.
- **Free play works too.** The host's vendors, scare lines, nets, the Warden and heat are shown on the guest's screen, and a spray or swat on the guest's gull is felt on the guest's side.
- Leave with N → **Leave the game**. If either side drops, the other is told and carries on alone.

## How it works
- **WebRTC peer to peer**, one unordered, no-retransmit data channel (`src/net/peer.ts`). Codes are the connection description compressed and base64url-encoded, `LK1.…` (`src/net/codec.ts`, about 600 characters).
- **Host-authoritative** (`src/net/session.ts`, `src/main.ts`): the host runs the real game (props, people, quests, defence, heat) with the guest as a second gull. The guest is a thin client:
  - It **flies its own gull locally** (no lag) and sends its state plus recent button presses about 60 times a second. The host trusts the flight state and decides grabs, snatches, poop hits and everything else.
  - The host sends a **snapshot about 20 times a second** (gulls, props, people, quests, heat and the city's response), plus **events** (toasts, sounds, banners, the level card, the host gull's droppings, hits on the guest). The guest eases everything towards the latest news (`src/net/remote.ts`).
  - **Button presses are repeated** for half a second and counted once (`TapSender` / `TapReceiver`), the hello is repeated until welcomed, and the level number rides on every snapshot, so dropped packets never strand a player.
- **Levels switch in place** (`loadLevel` in `main.ts`): props, people, quests, triggers and defence are rebuilt without reloading the page, so the connection survives a level change. (This also makes level changes instant in single player.)
- **Co-op twists** (`LevelDef.coopQuests`): each of levels 1–4 gets one extra hidden `???` to-do that only exists with two players and needs both gulls: *Say cheese, together* (both in the selfie), *Splat squad* (both poop on someone within 5 s), *Double trouble* (both snatch within 5 s), *Pass the parcel* (one drops or tosses, the other grabs it). They count for the second star, never for clearing. `CoopCombo` (pure, tested) detects the pairs.

## Room codes and relay (optional, free)
- **Copy-paste codes** always work with no server. For a **short room code** (`K7QX2M`), deploy the tiny room service in `server/signal` (a Cloudflare Worker, free plan); the lobby then offers *Host a game* / *Join with a room code* and keeps copy-paste as a fallback.
- A **relay (TURN)** for the networks that cannot connect directly: either automatic through the same service (Cloudflare Realtime TURN) or pasted by hand from any free provider, in N → Settings.
- Step by step: [`docs/online-setup.md`](../online-setup.md). The only external requests are those the player turns on: the public STUN server (Google's, to find the network address), and the room service and relay if configured. Single player makes none.
- The service code (`server/signal/src/handler.ts`) is unit-tested and also runs in the browser test through the real lobby.

## Limits fixed after the first version
- **Guest skills:** (a guest also borrows the host's skills for the session, without saving them) the guest's own skills work (crow call, flock call, pigeon disguise, hawk-eye): the guest gates them with its own save and cooldowns, the host performs the effect. The disguise is shown on both screens and the vendors and Warden respect it.
- **Carrying weight:** the guest's flight feels the weight of what the host says is in its beak.
- **No freezing:** an open menu no longer pauses a connected game (you just stop steering), so the other player never sees the world stop.
- **Guest progress:** the host announces each clear, and the guest records the stars and earns the level's skill in its own save.

## Still true
- Voice or chat: use your own call.
- Not yet tried on two real networks (needs you and a friend); the automated tests run two browser pages on one machine, with and without the room service.

## Playtest checklist (needs a friend, or two windows)
- [ ] Two windows on one PC: host, join, fly; each sees the other gull.
- [ ] Grab, toss and poop from the guest work (the item moves in the guest's beak; poop splats).
- [ ] Host switches levels (L): the guest follows without doing anything.
- [ ] Level 1: both stand in front of the selfie phone at the click: the hidden to-do ticks.
- [ ] Free play: raise heat; the guest sees vendors, lines, nets and the Warden, and gets sprayed or swatted.
- [ ] Over the internet with a friend (PC–PC and phone–PC); note the lag and whether the connection ever fails.
- [ ] Guest uses a skill (crow call, flock call, disguise, hawk-eye): the effect happens near the guest's gull.
- [ ] Host opens a menu: the guest's world keeps running.
- [ ] After a level clear, the guest's own level list shows the stars.
- [ ] (If you deploy the room service) a room code works; a friend on a strict network connects with the relay.
- [ ] Close one side: the other is told and keeps playing.
