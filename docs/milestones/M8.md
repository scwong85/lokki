# M8 — Skills and saving

## Goal
Finishing a quest earns a skill, progress survives a reload, and a cleared level can be replayed with extra objectives or as a speedrun.

## Built
- **Seven skills, one per main quest** (`src/data/skills.ts`, plain data). Passive ones are multipliers (`src/core/skillMods.ts`) read by flight, interaction and the defence code; active ones have keys and cooldowns.

| Skill | Earned by | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Strong wings | Ice-cream snatch | bigger flap, slower sink, more stamina |
| Mimic (V) | Salmiakki swap | crow call: people turn toward a spot ahead of you (noise) |
| Hawk-eye (H) | Cap heist | lines and nets stay visible 3x as long, cooldown shorter |
| Fat bird | Cinnamon bun ring | carry up to 5 kg (chairs) |
| Flock call (G) | Map sabotage | decoy gulls fly to a spot ahead and make noise |
| Disguise (B) | Blind-date wrecker | pigeon colours: vendors and the Warden ignore you for 10 s |
| Quick bowels | Lost teddy | poop cooldown halved |

- **Saving** (`src/core/save.ts`): localStorage, versioned, sanitised on load; blocked or throwing storage just means no save. Stores skills, won quests, replay unlock, extras toggle, best times, clears, mute. Debug panel button "Reset saved progress".
- **Skills panel (K)** and an ability bar with cooldown fills; an announce banner when a skill is earned.
- **Replay variants:** clearing the finale unlocks **X** (hidden "bonus" quest with six harder objectives) and **Z** (speedrun: the clock starts on your first move; best time saved). R starts a normal run; skills and best times are kept across restarts.
- New feel parameters in `tuning.skills` (cooldowns, ranges, durations).
- Tests: `tests/unit/skills-save.test.ts` (skills, mods, save/sanitize, quest flags and restore, abilities, run timer, flock), `tests/smoke/skills.spec.ts` (unlock, reload persistence, finale -> X/Z).

## Notes and decisions
- Disguise only fools vendors and the Warden, not tourists (they have no deterrent role).
- H is shared: before Hawk-eye is earned it is the short city-lines hint from M6; the skill upgrades it.
- R overwrites the saved "quests won" with an empty list, but skills stay.

## Exit criteria
- [ ] Each quest win gives its skill and the effect is noticeable
- [ ] Reloading keeps skills and finished quests
- [ ] V / G / B abilities work and show cooldowns
- [ ] Finale clear unlocks X and Z; speedrun clock and best time work
- [ ] No lost progress or errors with storage blocked
