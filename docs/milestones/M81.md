# M81 — A busier square (strollers and roaming)

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §3. First slice of routines, kept safe for the puzzles.

## Built
- **Strollers:** every campaign stage gets background walkers (`tuning.crowd.strollers`, default 6; `LevelDef.strollers` overrides). Harmless tourists (role `stroller`, no items) placed on standing room 6–30 m around the start, seeded by the level id (co-op players get the same crowd), never on top of the cast, and strolling up to `tuning.crowd.wander` metres. Playground levels have none unless asked.
- **Roaming:** `NpcSpawn.roam` lets a "stationary" person stroll within a leash of their spot and wait 5–11 s between walks. Used for the picnickers (Esplanadi, Dog-and-honk) and the Senate worker. Puzzle people (selfie spots, queues, photographers, stall vendors, the conductor) stay put on purpose.
- Anyone disturbed goes through the usual reactions and then strolls again.
- New tuning group `crowd` (count, wander, spawn ring).
