# M97 — Two more trial puzzles with long chains

You asked for more trial stages with long and complex to-dos. Both are in the level list under **Puzzles (new)**, next to P1. Each has six steps, readable people (name tags, hats), scenery you can recognise, the hint ladder (wish, riddle, marker, answer; J asks), and several ways into the steps.

## P2. The Grand Opening
A new ice-cream kiosk is about to open. Steps: a crowd waiting at the ribbon → the Mayor losing his big scissors → the ribbon cut (the scissors on it, **but only once the crowd has been there**) → the Vendor's first cone (he is cheering after the cut) → the cone to the Reporter → get away.
New: a trigger that needs another trigger first (`needs`), the scissors prop, a kiosk, ribbon posts that show two limp halves when it is cut, a reaction that makes the Vendor delighted and oblivious for 22 s after the cut and the Mayor angry.

## P3. Fire Drill
A fish stall with a golden fish, a Guard on his round, a Fishmonger, and a queue. Steps: no customers at the stall → the Guard off his round → the Fishmonger looking the other way → the golden fish → the fish at the green MUSTER sign → get away.
The alarm sends the queue and the Guard to the assembly point, which empties the stall for a while; the sprinkler soaks the Fishmonger; a puddle trips the Guard; a bun trail leads hungry people off; a honk shoos. The Guard comes back, the crowd drifts home: the timing is yours to find. New: a green assembly sign with a patch on the ground; the fish stall is the buffet table with a cloth.

## Designer's notes: the ways (not shown to the player)
- P2 crowd: jukebox (people come to dance), buns on the ground (people come to eat), both. Scissors: a honk from behind (he drops them), the jukebox (he dances), a taunt (he follows and walks away with them), all then taken unseen. Cut: scissors dropped on the ribbon with a crowd there; without it nothing happens. Cone: take it in the window after the cut.
- P3 stall: the alarm, a honk, a bun trail. Guard: puddle, taunt, the alarm, a clatter. Fishmonger: the sprinkler, a bottle, the alarm (his eyes). The fish: only when none of the three can see you; whoever does gives chase, and if they catch you the fish goes back.
