# M5 — First NPC and quest — Decision gate 2

## Goal
The core loop (scout → dive → snatch → escape) is fun on its own.

## Built
- **Tourist NPC** (`src/gameplay/npc.ts`, pure logic, unit-tested; reactions documented as data in `src/data/npcs.ts`). Six tourists on the market square, four holding an ice cream. Capsule-ish low-poly people with sun hats and jackets in different colours, a procedural walk, eat and panic pose, and a floating "!" when they have spotted you (`src/world/npcModel.ts`, pose rules in `src/gameplay/npcPose.ts`).
  - **Senses:** sight (range, cone, line of sight blocked by walls/roofs/canopies; much shorter while eating; **blind spot straight overhead**) and hearing (clatter, crash, tipped bins, splats).
  - **States:** idle / eat / walk (wander within their area, dry land only) → notice → **shield** → panic / chase / recover.
  - **Shielding:** once a tourist has spotted you they face you and swing the ice cream to the far side of their body. The body is solid, so you cannot beak through it. Their hand follows you faster than you can walk around them.
  - **Distraction:** a loud noise turns the body towards it and **the hand stays where it was**, so the far side opens up for a moment. Knock over a bin, drop a bottle, splat something, then go around.
  - **Snatch:** press `E` when the ice cream is in reach, or **dive at it** (dives snatch automatically). The tourist panics, chases you for a few seconds, then sulks. Pooping on a tourist makes them panic.
  - Tourists are solid for the gull (it is pushed out sideways) and for walls, counters and each other.
- **Quest log** (`src/gameplay/quest.ts`, definitions in `src/data/quests.ts`, pure and unit-tested):
  - **Ice-cream snatch** (timed, 90 s): the clock starts the moment the first tourist spots you. Objectives: snatch an ice cream from a tourist, then get away with it (carry it 30 m away from every tourist for 3 s). Win/lose with sounds and toasts; `R` retries.
  - **Little extras** (untimed): poop on a tourist, tip over a bin, smash a bottle.
- **To-do list UI** (paper-note style, left side) with ticked items and the timer.
- **Poop aim assist** (answer to the M4 feedback "poop is hard to aim"): a tourist within about 3 m of where the poop would land pulls its drift toward them; the reticle shows the assisted landing spot. `poop.assistStrength` = 0 turns it off.
- New placeholder sounds: gasp, yelp, win jingle, fail buzz (synthesised).
- All NPC and quest numbers are in the tuning panel (`npc.*`, `quest.*`). NPC randomness uses a seeded RNG.

## How to play the quest (tips)
- Sneak: eating tourists see less, and nobody sees you straight overhead. A high dive from above is the quiet way in.
- If you are seen, use a distraction (noisy props) and come in from the hand side.
- After the snatch: fly away fast and far; stay out of their reach (30 m from every tourist for 3 s while carrying it).

## Notes
- Only one tourist behaviour set exists. Vendors and the Warden come in M6.
- Tourists do not yet react to being dived at or bumped; props thrown at them do not count as hits.
- NPCs are CPU-light (6 of them), draw calls rose to ~70-80 with models.
- `?greybox` has no tourists or quests.
