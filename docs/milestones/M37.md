# M37 — World enrichment W6: landmark kit

Branch `m37-landmarks`. Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W6).

## Built
- **Cathedral portico**: six white columns, a stepped pediment, and the grand staircase's top step is a perch.
- **Empire style** (`STYLE_WALLS`/`STYLE_ROOFS` in `city.ts`): cream walls, mint roofs, pilasters every ~4.4 m and a pediment on the longest wall; applied to the Government Palace and the University via `data/landmarks.json` restyle entries. Uspenski got its brick-red walls and green roof.
- **Havis Amanda fountain**: splash puffs ring the statue when you are within 80 m.

## Not done
Chimney smoke, weather, grass tufts, bunting, static crowds, far ships, hanging signs. G4 post look, G5 actor art, G6 UI are still open (`docs/design/graphics-refresh.md`).
