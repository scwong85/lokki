# M50 — Handling and comfort, and playtest fixes

Branch `m50-handling`.

## Playtest fixes
- **"Get away didn't work, there is no bar to fill."** Two causes. (1) Items that were *dropped* by a person (a slip, a honk, a soak, the owl) were not counted as stolen, so the escape step never started in Wet Floor, Honk! and Splash Landing. Carrying off something a person dropped now counts. (2) The escape step only showed numbers; it now shows a **bar that fills** (red while you are too close to people, green when you are getting away) with the numbers beside it.
- **The wave sound is softer** (sea ambience about half as loud).
- **Cannonball had no splash sound**: a new synthesised **splash** (a heavy whoosh, a wet slap and bubbles), louder for a cannonball, also used for ordinary splashdowns.

## Handling and comfort (answer to "can the gull handle better, with less motion sickness?")
Research (summarised below) points to the same few levers; all are in **Options** and saved.
- **Comfort preset**: *Standard*, *Gentle* (about half the camera movement, a light vignette) or *Calm* (no camera roll so the horizon stays level, no dive FOV kick, almost no shake, no slow motion, softer look and steering, a stronger vignette). It scales the dive FOV kick, camera roll, screen shake, look-ahead and slow motion.
- **Comfort vignette**: the screen edges darken in fast turns and dives (Gentle and Calm), the standard anti-nausea trick.
- **Smooth steering** (on by default): a keyboard press ramps up over a fraction of a second (70 ms standard, 140 ms calm) instead of jerking the gull round.
- **Camera follows the gull** (Off, Gentle by default, Strong): when you are not looking around with the mouse for 1.2 s and are moving, the camera drifts round behind the gull's heading, so A and D steer like turns and you do not lose the gull.
- **Field of view** (60 to 100), **camera distance** (4 to 12), **invert vertical look**, and mouse-look smoothing in Gentle and Calm.
- Pure logic is in `src/core/comfort.ts` with 9 unit tests.

### Sources
[Xbox Accessibility Guideline 117](https://learn.microsoft.com/da-dk/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117) (camera and FOV changes without player input, FOV sliders), [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/?p=3281), and community comfort guides ([one](https://macmyths.com/how-to-reduce-motion-sickness-from-fast-movement-in-games/), [two](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-fix-camera-fov-causing-motion-sickness)): reduce shake, head bob and camera movement, a wider FOV, a vignette in fast motion, a stable horizon, smoothing, and third-person views with the body in sight. Several of those pages are generic; I used only advice that several agree on.

## More ideas, not built
- **"Where to?" key**: snap the camera behind the gull towards the current objective.
- **Auto-level glide** when there is no input, and an optional **speed cap** while comfort is Calm.
- A "free flight assist" that holds altitude when you let go of the keys.
- Gamepad: a dead-zone and response-curve slider.

## Playtest questions
1. Try Comfort: Gentle and Calm. Which is nicer, and does anything feel mushy?
2. Is "camera follows the gull" (Gentle) helpful, or does it fight you? (Off in Options.)
3. Is smooth steering comfortable, or too slow to react? (Turn it off in Options.)
