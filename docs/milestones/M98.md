# M98 — The Grand Opening: the crowd could not be drawn to the ribbon

Playtest: "ribbons cannot be cut; how to attract people to the ribbon? scissor is floating midair".

## What was wrong
- **The jukebox drew the crowd to the jukebox**, not to the ribbon: it stood 10 m from the ribbon, so the five people dancing round it were outside the "crowd at the ribbon" zone, and the ribbon (which only counts with a crowd, by design) never cut. Nothing told you that dropping the scissors there without a crowd does nothing, either.
- The scissors that "float": the Mayor takes his scissors back when they lie within about two metres of him (a real behaviour: he reclaims what is his) and holds them out at hand height; with the pinned-in-the-air case I could not reproduce, I added a safety net below.

## Fixes
- The **jukebox now stands right by the ribbon**, so the dancers gather where the crowd should be. A crowd is **four or more** guests within 7.5 m of the ribbon (was five within 6). Crumbs near the ribbon work too (people who eat stay where they ate); the hint says both.
- **Scissors on the ribbon with no crowd yet** now say so once: "Cutting a ribbon with nobody watching? Where is the audience?" (new trigger options `unless` and `always`).
- **Safety net for props:** a prop nobody holds that is still pinned in the air (a hold that was never released) is released and falls. Test: `tests/unit/propLimbo.test.ts`.
- Test: the real jukebox peck by the ribbon draws the crowd and ticks the step (`tests/smoke/puzzles2.spec.ts`).
