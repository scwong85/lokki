# M91 — Crowd tools: shoo, crumbs, clear the square

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §4 (food trails, repulsors, the "empty the square" objective).

## Built
- **Shoo (repulsor):** a honk now sends free people (strollers, passengers, partygoers, bystanders) about ten metres away from the gull, on the first walkable side. The stationary cast stays put.
- **Crumbs (food attractor):** a level can list `crumbs: ['bun']`. Untagged food of those kinds lying on the ground draws the nearest free person within 10 m: they walk over, eat it (delighted, hearts), and stay around where they ate. Events `crumbs:N` count the eaten pieces. Tagged items (puzzle buns) are never eaten.
- **Triggers:** `npcsInZone` accepts `role: '*'` (anyone), and a new `npcsClear` fires once a zone that held at least `min` people has `max` or fewer.
- **Two Playground levels** to try them: **M17. Shoo!** (honk the crowd off the stall, take the cone) and **M18. Breadcrumbs** (lay a bun trail, let three be eaten, gather four people at the bench).
- Tuning: `crowd.crumbRange`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/crowdTools.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/crowd-tools.spec.ts`.
- Test fix found on the way: the Wet Floor browser test waited too briefly for the inspector (he walks half the time, and the software renderer runs the sim slowly); not a game bug (it failed the same on main).

## Not done yet
- The new tools are only in two showcase levels; no campaign stage uses them yet. Candidate: a crowd step in a stage of Act 2, and a co-op crowd objective.
- Spectacle attractors (a bell or a fountain surge calling a crowd).
