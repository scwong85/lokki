# M1 — Flight feel (graybox) — Decision gate 1

## Goal
Moving around is already fun. Flight must feel good before anything else is built.

## Built
- **Ground:** walk (WASD, camera-relative) and **hop** (Space). Hop + flap = take-off.
- **Flap/climb** (Space, hold to repeat): costs stamina; stamina regenerates slowly while gliding, fast on the ground, fastest when perched.
- **Glide:** speed follows heading at cruise speed with a steady sink; WASD steers (camera-relative), **S = flare/brake** (slower, and near a surface a gentler sink for precise landings).
- **Committed dive** (hold Shift): needs a little clearance, can't be cancelled before `dive.minTime`, steering is heavily reduced; releasing it turns some speed into a swoop up.
- **Landing:** soft / rough / hard by impact speed (rough and hard stun briefly), skid on the ground; **bonk** when hitting walls too fast.
- **Perch detection:** landing on chimneys, ledges and lamp posts shows "Perched".
- **Wind:** slowly drifting direction + gusts push a flying gull (HUD arrow, camera-relative).
- **Camera:** positional lag, velocity look-ahead, FOV kick on dive only, small damped roll in turns.
- **Greybox course** (`src/world/greybox.ts`): courtyard, 4 rooftops, ledges/chimneys/lamp posts, an orange-stall "market", water to the east, 10 ice-cream targets to hit by diving (they respawn).
- **HUD:** stamina bar, mode/speed/height, wind, target counter, toasts. **R** resets the gull.
- Every feel number is in `src/core/tuning.ts` (F3 panel, "Copy tuning as JSON").
- Readability aids (small, graybox-only): two wing planks and a blob shadow on the surface below. The real model/animation is still M2.

## Notes / deviations
- Collision is simple AABB-vs-sphere in `src/world/collision.ts`, not Rapier. Rapier arrives when M3 needs static colliders from city data and M4 needs dynamic props; the course/collision API is small enough to swap.
- Flight logic is pure (`src/gull/flight.ts`) with unit tests in `tests/unit/flight.test.ts`.

## Exit / evaluation (for the user)
- [ ] 5 minutes of aimless play is fun
- [ ] A target can be hit by diving
- [ ] Landing on a ledge feels fair
- [ ] No motion sickness
- If not fun → redesign flight before anything else (decision gate 1).
