# M82 — Attractors: the crowd comes to the music

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §4. Decision: a heavy boom box, needing Fat bird to carry, pecked otherwise.

## Built
- **Music attractor** in `NpcManager` (`playMusic`, `moveMusic`, `musicStep`): while it plays, every *free* person in range who is not busy (strollers, wanderers, roaming bystanders; not the stationary puzzle cast, not people buying, tugged or in a conga) walks to a loose ring around it (`gatherSlot`), dances when close, and keeps dancing while it plays. The count feeds the existing `dancers:N` quest events. When the source moves, the crowd follows.
- **Boom box** prop (4.5 kg): without Fat bird, E pecks it and it plays for 30 s; with Fat bird, E lifts it, and it plays while carried, so the crowd follows the gull around (the "carry a jukebox" idea). Fires `pecked:boombox`.
- The existing **jukebox** now also draws free people over, besides making those nearby dance.
- New Playground level **M16. Block Party**: start the music, five people dancing, take a dancer's ice cream, escape.
- Tuning group `boombox` (seconds, range, gather distance, call interval).

## Not done yet
- Crumb trails and other attractors (food, spectacle), repulsors (a honk pushes people away), the "gather N people at a spot" and "empty the square" objectives, and rebuilding a campaign stage around attractors.

## Exit criteria
- Peck the box, wait, a crowd gathers and dances; with Fat bird carry it and they follow; puzzle people stay at their posts.
