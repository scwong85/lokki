# M114 — Stage 1.1 First Light, version two: Breakfast on the Roof

**Goal:** the first stage on the picnic formula (`docs/design/act1-v2.md`, `docs/design/picnic-v2.md`). Same verbs as before (fly, dive-snatch, carry, drop, land, walk, escape; nothing else is unlocked), but a ten-minute shape: four rows in two courses of two, owners 55 to 110 m from the market hall roof, each row a chain.

| Course | Row | Owner and place | The chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early birds | Something cold | Selfie Tourist, east quay | wait for her 3-2-1, dive on the 1, outrun her, carry home |
| Early birds | A sandwich | Dockhand, west quay | bun from the far bench, dropped on his spotless quay, he scolds it, take his sandwich |
| Second helping | Coffee | the Captain, the ferry berth | the ferry horn sends him to the gangway; take the thermos |
| Second helping | A sun hat | the Sleeper, far north-east | he nods off every half minute (💤); take the hat; he wakes and chases |

After the four: the breakfast gong (a peck); every victim looks up. Side order: Map sabotage. Co-op: Handover, Two birds. Reward: Strong wings.

**Built:** `src/data/levels/firstLight2.ts` (`FIRST_LIGHT_V2`, stage `a1s1`); the old First Light is the Playground level `first-light-v1`; the Dockhand's lure trigger is `always` (no step waits for it); puzzle-quality test accepts a collection stage with fewer chains. Tests: `tests/unit/firstLight2.test.ts` (data, land, spread, courses), `tests/smoke/firstlight2.spec.ts` (the world: lure, courses, ferry, gong); a real dive on the Sleeper's hat was replayed by hand. Time per row appears on the results card as in 1.2.
