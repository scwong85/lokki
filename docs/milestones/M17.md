# M17 — Market Square Act 1, part 2: levels 5–8

## Goal
The second half of Act 1 from `docs/design/levels-kauppatori.md`: four levels, about 5 minutes each, each with a headline puzzle, a hidden `???` to-do, a co-op twist and a skill reward. Unlock order: 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8.

| # | Level (`id`) | Teaches | Headline puzzle | Other to-dos | Secret `???` | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **The City Strikes Back** (`city-strikes`) | the city's response: spraying vendors, scare lines, heat | **"Spray Tan"**: the angry vendor sprays at gulls. Stand just behind the rich tourist (top hat), wait for the red fan, dodge sideways: the spray soaks *him*, he drops his lunch and runs. Grab it | Slip through the scare lines five times · the cinnamon bun ring (a bun past three vendors) | Push the city to heat 60 | **Hawk-eye** |
| 6 | **Owl Be Back** (`owl-be-back`) | nets, the plastic owl | **"Borrowed Authority"**: a plastic owl scares everyone near it. Carry it to the bread granny: she runs, dropping her bread. Take it | Fly in under the net three times | Scare three people with the owl in your beak | **Fat bird** |
| 7 | **The Warden** (`the-warden`) | the nemesis, his umbrella and his rounds | **"Lunch Break"**: about 18 s in, the Warden goes for lunch (a banner says so): he sits on his bench with the umbrella down and his sandwich beside him. Poop him | Steal his sandwich | Take the sandwich while he is still eating (he looks up only for a close gull) | **Disguise** |
| 8 | **Harbour Day** (`harbour-day`) | floating, swimming, the ferry | **"Message in a Bottle"**: grab the child's bottle and drop it in the harbour: a current carries it to the ferry (about 40 s before the horn). Timed | Map sabotage (the existing quest) | Paddle in the harbour for eight seconds | **Flock call** |

Each level also has a two-player hidden to-do in online co-op (like levels 1–4).

## Built
- **Spray soaks people:** a vendor's spray now soaks everyone in its cone (`CityDefense` spray event with aim → `NpcManager.soakCone`): they drop what they hold and run. New `soaked` event, `soaked:<role>` quest events.
- **The plastic owl** (new prop): frightens calm people within `owl.scareRange` of it (carried or lying), who drop what they hold; vendors within range freeze for a few seconds (`CityDefense.scareVendors`). Events `owlScare:<role>`, `owlScare:carried`, `owlScare:vendor`.
- **The Warden's lunch:** `wardenLunch` in a level (start, duration, spot, optional repeat). New `lunch` mode: he walks to the spot, sits with the umbrella down, and looks up only for a gull within 6 m. The level announces it and fires `wardenLunch`.
- **Levels set up their city:** `deploy` (put specific deterrents up without raising the heat), `startHeat`, `drift` (a current for tagged message bottles, `water.driftSpeed`).
- **New to-do kinds:** `swim` trigger (seconds paddling), `heat60`, `sandwichUnseen`, a timed quest on a message bottle (`quest.bottleTime`); NPC spawn options `holdTag` and `hat: 'top'`.
- Title screen hint now says Space: jump (hold: fly) and Tab: lock on.
- Tests: `tests/unit/act1-part2.test.ts` (soak, owl, lunch, swim, level data), `tests/smoke/act1b.spec.ts` (each level in the browser: its situation starts, the key event chain completes, the bottle drifts to the ferry).
