# M68 — Accomplishment beats

Branch: `m67-puzzle-pass` (built together with M67). Plan: `docs/design/puzzle-quality.md`, section 5.

## Built
- **The recap.** When a chain is won, a short slow-motion beat on the punchline, then a banner that retells what you did and what the world did, step by step ("You splatted his route → he slipped → and his lunch was yours"). Each step has a past-tense `recap` caption in data (it falls back to the step text).
- **The playtest card.** After a chain, a small card asks "how was that? 1 stuck · 2 got it · 3 funny" for nine seconds. The answers are kept in the browser and exported with F3 > "Copy playtest notes" as JSON (per objective counts and every entry), so playtests give data on the puzzle questions.
- Tests: the recap text and the playtest log are covered in `tests/unit/puzzle.test.ts`.

## Not here
