# M83 — Experiments: chain reactions, badges, nudges

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §5 (items 2, 3 and 6).

## Built
- **Chain-reaction recogniser** (`src/gameplay/chainReaction.ts`): three different reactions (knock-downs, soaked, yuck, a tipped chair, a spread fright, a dance, a snatch…) within 8 s trigger "Chain reaction! ×N": a toast, a chime, a polaroid and a second of slow motion (single player). Repeating the same thing does not count; it cools down for 15 s. Fed from the one place every game event passes through, so it works in every level.
- **Badges**: Chain reaction (3), Rube Goldberg (5), Party starter (5 dancing), Strike! (4 knocked down), Wildfire (a fright spread to 5). Earned badges are listed on the level-complete card the first time and kept in the save (`badges`).
- **Idle nudge**: after 75 s without any event the game suggests an idea (the hint of the current step, else a nudge to experiment); again every 60 s. Not in the sandbox, Havoc Hour or after clearing. All numbers in `tuning.chain` and `tuning.nudge`.

## Not done (left in the plan)
- Timetables (a ferry crowd, rain, gusts), people remembering and gossiping, optional photo hunts, hidden-goal trails, three answers per prop kind.

## Exit criteria
- A visible reward when you make things happen in a row; nothing nags a player who is busy.
