# M35 — World enrichment, rest of W4: flags, steam, petals, cloud shadows

Branch `m35-world-w4b`. Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W4).

## Built
- **Flags**: Finnish flags on tall poles along the market quay, at Senate Square and at the Esplanadi end. Instanced (one call), and they flutter in the vertex shader.
- **Steam** rises above the market stalls when you are within 70 m (the pooled particles from M25, no extra calls).
- **Blossom** drifts down around you in the Esplanadi park.
- **Cloud shadows**: slow soft patches of shade drift over the ground and the roofs on the wind (a scrolling noise texture sampled in the ground and building shaders). Subtle by default.
- Tuning (`motion` group): `cloudShadow`, `cloudScale`, `cloudSpeed`, `flagFlutter`, `steam`, `petals` (0 turns each off).

## Not done
Chimney smoke, weather presets (rain, snow outside winter levels), ambient sound zones, grass tufts and flower beds, bunting.
