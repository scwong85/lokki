# M90 — A crowd objective in the campaign (stage 1.4)

Plan: `docs/design/living-world.md` §4: group objectives that need the crowd to be brought together.

## Built
- **Stage 1.4 Jukebox Hero is a crowd step now.** The four dancers are no longer nailed to their posts: six free "partygoers" (holding the cones) drift about the square, and the step is "Get five people dancing" (`dancers:5`). Peck the jukebox and everyone who is not busy walks over to dance (M82), then the cone step works as before (dancers don't guard anything).
- **A boom box** lies in the stage as a second way to start the music: peck it, or lift it with Fat bird and walk it to the crowd.
- The Playground "Jukebox Hero" level is unchanged (four dancers, `dancers:4`).
- Tests: `tests/unit/crowdStage.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/crowd-stage.spec.ts` (real jukebox, the step ticks).
