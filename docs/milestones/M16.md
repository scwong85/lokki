# M16 — Controls and flow polish

## Goal
Make aiming, turning, walking and the "unseen" to-do easier, and let the host pick the level (from your M14/M15 playtests).

## Done
- **Host picks the level:** the co-op lobby has a **Level to host** list (unlocked levels). The game starts that level when your friend joins, with its two-player to-do; the guest follows. While playing, the lobby has a **Change level** button for the host.
- **Target lock (Tab):** press Tab to lock onto the best target in view (people, vendors, the Warden, the brass sign); Tab again picks the next one, then lock off. A pulsing ring and diamond mark it. A **locked dive** bends toward the target all the way (you still choose when to dive), and a **locked dropping** is pulled onto it from up to 45 m (the ring shows where it lands). The lock lets go by itself if the target is gone or too far. Controller: **LB**. Phone: the **⌖** button (next to the eye). In co-op the guest's lock steers their droppings too. Tuning: `lock.*`.
- **Turning in the air** is quicker (3.8 rad/s instead of 2.4, and tighter again when flying slowly: `flight.airTurnRate`, `flight.airTurnSlow`).
- **Jump:** on the ground **tap Space to jump** (about 0.9 m, you keep walking-style control and land softly), **hold Space to take off**, and press Space again in a jump to spread your wings. A new jump pose. Tuning: `walk.jumpSpeed`, `walk.jumpSpeedFactor`, `walk.takeoffHold`.
- **Unseen snatch is fair:** seeing you is no longer the same as noticing you. A calm person builds *suspicion* while you are in sight (1.4 s; half that when you dive) that fades when you are not, so a quick dive-snatch counts as "has not noticed you" (`npc.noticeBuild`, `npc.suspicionFade`). Everyone else is a bit slower to spot you too, so guarded ice creams are a little easier as well.
- **Swimming legs:** checked in a screenshot on this build: legs are tucked and hidden while floating (the M15 fix; the live site had the old build when you tested).
