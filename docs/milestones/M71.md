# M71 — Act 2 continues: 2.3 Ding Dong and 2.4 Turf War

Branch: `m71-act2-bells-turf`. Plan: `docs/design/act2.md`.

## Built
- **2.3 Ding Dong** (noon; the bell rings every 28 s, six seconds of ringing, and the tram runs on its line). Chains with joke cards: *Bell Heist* (wait for the bell, take one sandwich, then the other before they look down), *Lunch Express* (a bottle on the track stops the tram, the driver climbs out and is oblivious to you for a while, take his lunch), *Bell Splat* (splat grandma while everyone looks up at the tower, take the bun she drops). Co-op (required): Chime and dash and Bell bombers. Reward: **Flock call**.
- **2.4 Turf War** (the Pigeon Gang owns the statue). Chains: *Forgive and forget* (win the pigeons over with a bun, then scatter them with salmiakki), *Hard Hat, Soft Heart* (wind up the foreman, lure him to the florist, he buys flowers, take them), *Captain Gull* (peck the captain's cap: nobody sees a gull; take a cone from three guards). Co-op (required): Pigeon bombers and Dressing room. Reward: **Disguise**.
- New quest event `bellPoop` (a splat while the bell rings); the quest reaction list gained the bell and tram events.
- The captain's cap puzzle is moved onto Senate Square (its stand east of the statue).
- Tests: stages 2.3 and 2.4 in `tests/unit/stages.test.ts`, the joke card test covers them, `tests/smoke/stage-act2.spec.ts`.
