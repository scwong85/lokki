# M119 — Easier dive snatch, and women who look like women

From the playtest: dive snatches fail too often, and every woman (Granny included) looks like a man.

## Dive snatch
- A diving beak takes a held item from **2.0 m** of the hand (was 1.2 m, the reach on foot): `tuning.npc.diveSnatchReach`.
- In a dive only the core of the body blocks the beak (`tuning.npc.diveBodyBlock`, 0.55 of the body radius); on foot the whole body still blocks, so distractions still matter on the ground.
- The dive's aim assist bends harder toward a hand on the line: `tuning.dive.assistStrength` 0.6 -> 0.8, `assistConeDeg` 20 -> 28.
- A steep dive at a hand within the new reach snatches instead of bowling the person over (the skittle check follows the reach).
- Tests: `tests/unit/npc-quest.test.ts` (reach and block in a dive).

## Women
- `src/gameplay/look.ts`: who looks like a woman. The roles the levels call "she" (Granny, the Baker, the Selfie Tourist, the bride, the blogger, the librarian, the florist, the west Berry Seller, ...), a seeded half-and-half mix for the crowd (tourists, queues, passengers, strollers), and a spawn can say so (`look: 'f' | 'm'`, set where a level's text needs it: the watchful tourist, the sweets tourist, the blind date, the map tourist).
- The person model gets long hair falling down the back (or a grey bun for older people) and a flared skirt in the trouser colour. Screenshot: `M119-women.png`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/look.test.ts`.
