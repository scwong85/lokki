# M2 — The gull

## Goal
Reads as a seagull; animation sells the flight.

## Built
- **Procedural low-poly gull** (`src/gull/model.ts`): chunky body + tail, head with eyes and a two-part beak (lower beak opens), grey two-segment wings with black tips (shoulder + elbow), two legs with feet. All colours from `palette.ts`.
- **Pose logic is pure** (`src/gull/pose.ts`, unit-tested): game state in, joint angles out.
  - ground: wings folded, walk cycle (legs swing, head bob, body bob) scaled by speed
  - glide: wings spread with a small flutter, legs tucked; legs drop when close to a surface (landing)
  - flap: stroke from wings-up through down and back, timed to `flight.flapInterval`
  - dive: wings swept back, legs tucked, head down
  - turning: head tilts into the turn; body already pitches/rolls with flight
  - beak: opens on dive start (screech), hard landing and bonk; stunned = dizzy head wobble, beak open, wings drooped
- **Blob shadow** (from M1) kept and tuned.
- **Audio manager** (`src/core/audio.ts`): lazy AudioContext on first key/click; sounds: flap, hop, screech (dive start), thud (landings), bonk, chime (target hit), plus a continuous air-rush that follows speed. **M** mutes. Volumes in the tuning panel (`audio.*`).

## Notes / deviations
- Plan says "placeholder CC0 sounds". The sounds are **synthesised with WebAudio** instead of downloaded files: no licences/credits, offline, tweakable. Easy to swap for CC0 samples later behind the same `play(name)` API (M9 audio pass).
- The gull is a bit bigger than a real gull (wingspan ~2.5 m) for readability from the third-person camera; collision radius unchanged.
- `window.__lokki` exposes the gull for debugging/screenshots.

## Exit criteria (for the user)
- [ ] It reads as a seagull
- [ ] Animation sells the flight (flap, glide, dive, landing, walking)
- [ ] Sounds are okay as placeholders (nothing grating or too loud)
