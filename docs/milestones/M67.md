# M67 — Act 1 puzzle pass

Branch: `m67-puzzle-pass` (stacked on `m66-puzzle-research`). Plan: `docs/design/puzzle-quality.md`.

## Built
- **Joke cards in data.** `QuestDef.joke` (the joke, the one insight, the reaction) on every Act 1 chain, and a test (`tests/unit/puzzle.test.ts`) that every stage chain has a card, at least two reaction steps, at least two steps, and does not end on "get away with it" unless somebody is chasing you.
- **Reactions: the world answers.** `LevelDef.reactions` (`src/data/reactions.ts`): when a quest event happens, people change mood, walk somewhere, look elsewhere or become oblivious to the gull for a while, an audience emotes, a line is shown, a sound plays, the Warden sets off after the gull, or another event is emitted. Run through one hook in the quest manager, once per level run.
- **The padding is gone.** The "get away with it" tail came off every chain except Breakfast and Map and Cone, where a chaser is the joke.
- **Rewrites.** 1.1 *Breakfast* (snatch the ice cream, she chases, lose her; the crowd laughs), 1.1 *Bun voyage* (take the bun, put it down on the dockhand's spotless quay, he walks over to scold it and is oblivious to you for a while, take his lunch), 1.5 *Nemesis* (the Warden's lunch, his sandwich, he hunts you, you dodge his swing, his umbrella is down, you poop him). Tug of War now has a different reaction for each of its three people; the Clean Freak, Bottle Service, Honk!, Spray Tan, Peekaboo, Jukebox Hero, Rush Hour and Strike! have audiences and recaps.
- New quest events: `robbed` (any victim of a snatch starts chasing you) and `wardenHunt` (the Warden spots you).

## Notes
- The dockhand's walk stops if he notices the gull; the `oblivious` reaction makes him miss it for 14 seconds so the plan can play out.
- Souvenir (the map quest) is now a chain with its own card; it still ends when the tourist trots to the wrong ferry.
