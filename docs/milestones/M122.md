# M122 — Land here: land on any spot without a dive, and stay there

From the playtest: landing on a special spot meant diving at it, and then sliding off the platform. Research and the options: `docs/design/landing.md`. Decisions: every spot; glide up to it; a dive still crashes, the land button lands softly.

- **Land here (hold S):** when a gull in the air has a flat spot ahead and below it (any rooftop, ledge, perch top or the ground, never the sea), a ring shows on it. Holding S (the stick pulled back, LT on a pad) flies the gull onto it along a braking arc: down to a dip under the spot's top, **up** over the edge (a hawk's approach), then a short settle; it arrives with no speed and stands there. Flap, a dive (Shift) or letting go of S cancel it; the wind is cancelled while it flies the arc. The ring is white while a spot is available and green while the arc is being flown. Spots come from a scan every 0.15 s (22 m ahead, 35° either side, never steeper than 0.75, a flat 1.2 m round the middle; a perch top may be tiny). If the way under is blocked (a roof's wall), the dip gets shallower or goes; if the arc stalls against something for a second it ends. (`src/gull/landing.ts`, `src/render/landRing.ts`)
- **Stick the landing:** a landing from the arc, or a glide onto a perch top, stops dead (no skid). A dive keeps the crash: the hard landing, the stun and the skid, as before.
- **Ledge guard:** for 0.8 s after any landing, and always while standing on a perch top, walking stops at an edge unless the stick pushes against it for 0.3 s (a hop is never held back). Tapping toward the edge never takes you over.
- **Taught:** the first two times a ring is in range a tip says "Hold S to land on the ring" (on touch: "Pull the stick back to land on the ring"); the Controls screen and the pad's LT row say what it does.
- **Tuning:** `tuning.landing` has every number (range, cone, slope, flat, approach speed, brake, sink, dip depth and distance, the end of the glide-up, ledge guard time, push time and drop).

Tests: `tests/unit/landHere.test.ts` (finds roofs and not the sea or what lies behind or above; holding S stops on the roof from different heights, speeds and distances, with no speed at touchdown; the same glide without S arrives fast; the dip exists in the open and shrinks against a wall; flap, dive and release cancel and a dive still crashes; perches stick; the ledge guard stops at the edge, a push steps off, a tap does not), `tests/smoke/landing.spec.ts` (the ring and the landing on the market hall roof in the browser).

## Playtest checklist
- [ ] Does the ring show where you expect, and not too often?
- [ ] Hold S from far out: does the arc look like a bird landing, and does it stop on the spot every time?

- [ ] Is the ledge guard right (not sticky when you want to walk off a roof)?
- [ ] Dives at perches still crash: wanted?
