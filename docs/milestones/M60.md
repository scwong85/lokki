# M60 — Stage framework and the first stage

Branch: `m60-stage-framework`. Plan: `docs/design/arcs.md`.

## Built
- **Stage data.** `LevelDef` gains `arc`, `stage`, `intro` and `coopRequired`. `src/data/arcs.ts`: the four arcs, `stage(arc, n, def)` (fills the id `a1s3`, the gate position, and `requires` = the stage before; the first stage of an act follows the finale of the act before), `stagesOf`, `playgroundOf`.
- **Gate from position.** A stage's mechanics are those of its own arc and stage and the stages before it (M59); nothing to configure.
- **Co-op objectives are required.** With two gulls the `coopRequired` quests are added to the main to-dos, so the stage is cleared only when they are done too; solo play skips them.
- **Level list.** The in-game list and the title screen list the campaign first, grouped by act, then the **Playground** (the toys, the older levels, free play). Old levels keep their ids, saves and stars.
- **Stage intro.** When a stage starts, a banner shows its intro text and one banner per mechanic it introduces (`New: Tug` and so on).
- **Touch.** The Poop, Toss and Taunt buttons are hidden when those verbs are locked.
- New profiles start in stage 1.1.
- **Stage 1.1 First Light** (act 1, stage 1; reward Strong wings): Breakfast (snatch an ice cream, land on a roof, get away), Souvenir (the existing Map sabotage chain), Neighbours (land by the bench, pick up the bun, drop it on the ferry); co-op: Handover and Two birds. Only flying, snatching and carrying are unlocked.
