# M99 — Solid furniture, and a last step that can be done

Playtest: "No collision detection for music box and table; carry it cannot be completed for Fire Drill."

## Fixes
- **Solid furniture:** the buffet table, the kiosk counter, and the jukebox, alarm and sprinkler boxes are now solid for the gull and for people (`World.furniture`, set per level). Their tops are perches: the gull can land on the table to take the pie.
- **The impossible "Get away with it":** in P2 and P3 the last step asked you to carry the loot away from everyone, right after a step that has you put it down (the cone at the reporter, the fish at the assembly point), so there was nothing left to carry. Those last steps are gone: the levels end on the delivery. A new test checks every level for this mistake (`tests/unit/escapeRule.test.ts`).
