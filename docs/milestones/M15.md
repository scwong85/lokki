# M15 — Polish pass from playtest feedback

## Goal
Fix what you noticed while playtesting M10, M11 and M12.

## Done
- **Windows:** every building has its own window rhythm (`windowPlan` in `src/world/facade.ts`): a bay width from 3.4 to 7.3 m, one of six styles (classic, tall arched, wide ribbon, small pair, shuttered, banded), accent floors (often the top one), and blank walls (never the longest). One facade atlas, one material, so no extra draw calls; the facade detail is draw-only (the physics mesh keeps plain walls).
- **Street life:** pedestrians and cyclists wander the streets (`src/gameplay/streetLife.ts`, `src/world/streetLife.ts`; tuning `street.*`; half as many on the low preset; only the ones near you are moved and drawn). Street furniture along the street edges: lamp posts, planters, signs, café tables with chairs, parked delivery vans, plus more bikes. All cosmetic: no senses, no effect on quests.
- **Swimming:** the legs are tucked and hidden while the gull floats.
- **Floating items:** bob higher and rock with the waves (`water.bobHeight`, `water.bobSpeed`, `water.rock`).
- **Running tourists:** the run clip keeps its own arms (`people.runArms`).
- **Static ice cream removed:** the floating pink ice-cream dive targets with light pillars from the M1 test are gone from the city (only the ice creams people hold remain as dive targets).
- **Menus:** Esc opens the options (or closes what is open); the options have **Skills & gull looks** and **Controls…**; **Controls** shows keyboard, controller (built from the real mapping) and touch.
- **Controller in menus:** D-pad or left stick moves a highlight, A presses, B goes back, on every panel (title, options, skills, controls, level list, lobby, credits).
- **Touch:** faster drag-to-look (default 2.6, **Touch look speed** slider), a new default button layout in a thumb arc, **Left-handed buttons**, and **Arrange the touch buttons** (drag each button; remembered; Reset).
