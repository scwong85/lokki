# M111 — Picnic engine: courses, the waiting ritual, the thrown-only bell, the Market Guard, time per row

Design: `docs/design/picnic-v2.md` (sections 4, 5, 8 and 9). This is the engine half; M112 builds the content (the spread-out map, the six chained rows, the pigeon race, the raid). Nothing here changes the campaign stage 1.2 yet.

## Built

### Courses (rows open in order)
- A row can have a `course` (1-based); a collection can name its courses and say what opens each (`QuestDef.collect.courses`).
- **A course opens when every row of the courses before it is done.** A row in a closed course:
  - does not count when its event happens, and is not "wanted" by the world triggers;
  - has no map marker, and cannot be found (it stays `???` even with the gull on top of it).
- **The note:** a heading per course, a finished course ticked, and a closed course as one line: `🔒 Main course  opens when the starters are served`.
- **When the last row of a course is delivered** the game says "Starters served!", cheers, and fires the quest event `courseServed:<n>`. Level data hangs reactions on it: the next course's people arrive, the Guard starts his rounds.
- Pure helpers in `src/gameplay/collection.ts` (`courseOpen`, `courseDone`, `rowLocked`, ...); a new quest note type `course`.

### A ritual that waits for customers
- `Ritual.needs = { count, r }`: the ritual only runs while at least `count` other people (not of the person's own role) stand within `r` of where they stand. Its clock stops when they leave and starts again when they come back, so the countdown never begins without them.
- The Waiter uses it: he flourishes only when strollers sit at his table (drop a bun for them: the crumbs system brings them).

### A bell that only a thrown thing rings
- `switch.hitOnly: 'thrown'`: a splat does not set it off (it lands on the awning); something thrown (Q) or dropped onto it does. It needs the toss verb, not poop. `switchHitAllowed` and `hitOnlyReachable` in `mechanics.ts`.

### The Market Guard
- **`NpcSpawn.confiscates`** (a list of quest tags): if he is not busy, not looking at a noise, and he **sees a gull carrying one of those items** (within `tuning.guard.whistleRange`, in his sight cone and line of sight), he blows his whistle.
  - Everybody within 16 m looks round.
  - The item goes home: into its owner's hand if it was theirs, else back where it started (`NpcManager.returnLoot`).
  - The gull is shoved away and dazed for a moment.
  - Event `whistle:<role>` for reactions. A cooldown keeps him from whistling every frame.
- **`NpcSpawn.investigates`:** he walks to a few metres short of a noise he hears (a honk), then goes back to his rounds. Event `arrived:noise:<role>`. A guard who is looking at a noise cannot see you, which is how a honk gets you past him.
- **Late arrivals:** a reaction can `cast` people (`Reaction.cast`). They appear when the reaction plays and are removed on a restart. The Guard comes this way, and so will the ferry crowd.
- Tuning group `guard` (range, cooldown, stop distance, shove, stun).

### Time per row
- `RowTimes` (`src/gameplay/rowTimes.ts`, pure) records when each row of a collection was delivered and how long after the one before.
- The **results card** shows it: `Time per row (in the order you did them)`, then each row with the time and "+ since the last", and course headings with the time each was served. `__lokki.rowTimes` exposes it for tests.
- Row finding (`RowScout`) and the time record now also reset on a restart.

### The Picnic lab (Playground, series "Picnic lab (engine)")
`P4. The Picnic lab (engine test)`: three rows in two courses, built from the stage's roof, stalls and bell.
- **Starters:** the Waiter's tablecloth (customers needed) and the Baker's loaf (the bell is thrown-only).
- **Main course:** the Market Guard's coffee. The Guard is cast when the starters are served.

It is a place to try the pieces before M112 uses them, and it is what the tests drive.

## Tests
- `tests/unit/picnicEngine.test.ts` (20 tests):
  - **Courses:** locked rows do not count or show markers, the course note fires exactly once, rows are found only when their course is open.
  - **The waiting ritual:** never starts without customers, one customer is not enough, a colleague of his own role does not count, the clock stops and resumes, and a ritual without `needs` is unchanged.
  - **The Guard:**
    - whistles once per cooldown, with the right item and gull;
    - does not whistle for the wrong item, an empty beak, a gull too far, behind him, disguised, behind a wall, or while he is looking at a noise;
    - walks to a noise and back to his rounds, and one who does not investigate stays put;
    - loot goes back to its owner's hand or its start;
    - late arrivals appear and vanish on reset.
  - **Time per row:** the lines and clock format.
  - **The switch rules.**
  - **The lab level:** items and triggers, the Guard is cast and not there before, the rounds are on land.
- `tests/smoke/picnic-lab.spec.ts` plays the lab in the browser:
  - the note shows two courses and the second is locked;
  - the thermos on the roof early does nothing;
  - the Waiter does not start without customers, then does with two, and the cloth goes up and is delivered;
  - a splat by the bell does nothing, a dropped bottle rings it and the Baker comes;
  - "Starters served!" and the Guard arrives, and the new row shows `???` until you fly near it;
  - a honk sends the Guard to the noise;
  - he whistles at a gull carrying his thermos and it goes back to his table;
  - delivering it clears the level, and the card lists the time per row.
- 840 unit tests pass, lint and type check pass.

## Known issues
- **Online co-op:** a guest does not see the "course served" toast, and does not see people cast in late (the host runs them). The quest state still syncs.
- **Guard cone:** he only sees what is in front of him (the normal sight cone), so the sneaking routes in the design rely on that.
- **The lab's numbers** (whistle range 14 m, cooldown 9 s, shove 7 m/s) are first guesses; they are all in the tuning panel under `guard`.
