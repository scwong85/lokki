# M20 — Act 3, part 2: levels 19–22

## Goal
The last four levels of the Market Square (design: `docs/design/levels-kauppatori.md`, Act 3). Unlock order 18 → 19 → 20 → 21 → 22.

| # | Level (`id`) | Headline puzzle | Other to-dos | Secret `???` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | **The Rival** (`the-rival`) | **"Snack Duel"**: Kalle, a rival gull, flies to the nearest free snack, carries it to his perch (east quay) and eats it. First to three snacks wins; if he gets three first, the level restarts. Dive near him while he carries and he drops it | Make Kalle drop a snack | Land on Kalle's perch |
| 20 | **Night Market** (`night-market`) | **"Midnight Snack"**: it is dusk and the vendors sleep. They wake at a loud noise nearby (a clatter, a splat) or a gull landing right beside them, and doze off again when it is quiet. Take one thing from each of three stalls | Poop on a vendor | Wake a vendor up |
| 21 | **The Drone** (`the-drone`) | **"Battery Low"**: a drone follows you and dives at you (the red light aims first: fly sideways to dodge). Its battery runs out in about 50 s and it lands: steal the battery | Dodge its dive three times | Dive-bomb the drone (drains the battery) |
| 22 | **The Great Kauppatori Heist** (`grand-finale`) | The city at full alert (vendors, lines, nets, the Warden). Land on the ferry cabin roof to ring the horn, then collapse the chair stack, swipe the pastry and poop on the Warden before time runs out. The ending: the city makes you the market mascot | Poop on three people | Dodge two of the Warden's swats |

Each level also has a two-player hidden to-do in online co-op, and a skill reward. After level 22 the sandbox (free play) is still there.

## Built
- **Kalle** (`src/gameplay/kalle.ts`, `src/world/kalle.ts`, tuning `kalle.*`): a rival gull AI (perch, seek, carry, flee), carrying real props, scoring, dropping them when a gull dives near. Shown to a co-op guest.
- **Drone** (`src/gameplay/drone.ts`, tuning `drone.*`): hunts the nearest gull, aims then dives (hit or dodge), a dive at it bonks it and drains the battery, lands when empty and leaves a **battery** prop. New hit kind `drone`.
- **Sleeping vendors** (`sleepingVendors`, tuning `deter.wake*`, `deter.sleepAfter`): asleep until a loud noise or a gull beside them wakes them; they doze off again when it is quiet.
- **Night** (`LevelDef.night`, tuning `render.nightLight`): a dusk sky, fog and dimmer lights.
- **Level endings** (`endingNote`): the finale's closing line on the level-complete card; the finale quest is reused without the seven-quest requirement.
- Tests: `tests/unit/kalle.test.ts`, `drone.test.ts`, `act3b.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/act3b.spec.ts` (Kalle takes a snack, the vendors sleep and wake, the drone lands and leaves its battery, the finale is open).

## Playtest checklist
- [ ] L19: Kalle takes snacks; you can beat him to three; diving near him makes him drop it; losing restarts the level.
- [ ] L20: it is dusk; vendors sleep; the three stall items can be taken quietly; a racket wakes someone.
- [ ] L21: the drone chases and dives; you can dodge (the red light, then sideways); it lands after about 50 s; you take its battery.
- [ ] L22: ring the ferry horn, wreck the market in time; the ending line appears on the level card.
- [ ] Each level about five minutes (the finale a little more).

## Known limits
- Not played by hand. The drone does not react to nets or fountains (the design's extras), Kalle cannot be framed with a vendor's spray, and the night has no lanterns that go out.
