# M34 — World enrichment, rest of W2: ground floors, awnings, balconies, turrets

Branch `m34-buildings`. Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W2).

## Built (all merged into the chunk meshes: no new draw calls)
- **Ground-floor variety**: the facade atlas has four ground rows now (shop, door, arched gateway, café front with a striped blind) plus the six window styles (10 rows). Each ground wall of four bays or more is split into runs (`groundRuns`): shop windows with a door, gateway or café front near the middle on about 7 walls in 10. Walls are drawn render-only with one solid wall for physics, so collisions are unchanged.
- **Awnings** over about 4 in 10 shop runs on the decorated streets (not the plain-city mix): five pastel colours, sloping out from the wall with a short valance. Visual only.
- **Balconies** on long walls of tall blocks in the Senate, Esplanadi and Katajanokka zones: a slab and rails, one or two bays, one or two floors. Visual only.
- **Corner turrets** on tall blocks in those zones: a round drum with a stepped cone roof and a gold tip, solid (the tip is a perch) so a gull lands on what it sees.
- Lit windows now also light the door fanlights and café windows at night.
