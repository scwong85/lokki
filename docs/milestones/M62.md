# M62 — Stages 1.3 Strings Attached and 1.4 Mood Swings

Branch: `m62-strings-moods`. Plan: `docs/design/arcs.md`, Act 1 stages 3 and 4.

## Built
- **1.3 Strings Attached** (reward Hawk-eye): the city strikes back (heat, spray vendors, scare lines); unlocks tug, hiding and BOO, the alarm and sprinkler buttons. Objectives: Tug of War (soup, balloon, bun), Spray Tan (the vendor soaks the rich tourist, take his lunch, get away), Peekaboo (hide in the bush, BOO, take a cone); the Big Red Button is a hidden bonus. Co-op (required): **Team tug** and Rescue.
- **1.4 Mood Swings** (reward Mimic): unlocks taunt and the lure, slip, the jukebox and bell. Objectives: Map and Cone, Wet Floor, Jukebox Hero. Co-op (required): Decoy and thief, and **Duet**.
- **Team tug (new mechanic).** A second gull can join a tug already in progress on the same person's hand; each of them then faces only a share of the pull, and winning with two pulling fires `coop:tug`. The person stays "tugged" until the last gull lets go.
- **Duet (new).** Both gulls taunting at once for 3 seconds fires `coop:duet` (`coop.duetSeconds`).
- Tests: stages 1.3 and 1.4 in `tests/unit/stages.test.ts`, team tug in `tests/unit/tug.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/stage34.spec.ts`.
