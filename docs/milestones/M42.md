# M42 — The victory run

Branch `m42-outro` (stacked on `m41-ui-polish`). Also adds the plan `docs/design/chain-levels.md`.

## Built
- **After a level is cleared** (single player), the camera takes over: the gull comes down to a clear spot and **runs straight at the camera, laughing** (beak open, a "ha" bubble, a wiggle, a screech now and then) while the camera sits in front of it and **backs away at the same speed**, drifting a little side to side. Black bars slide in, the HUD hides, confetti on touchdown. The level card (stamp and polaroids) follows after about seven seconds. Any of Space, Enter, Esc, E, F or Q skips it.
- **Landing spot** (`src/gameplay/outro.ts`, pure and tested): looks for the longest stretch of flat, dry ground, with dry ground either side, first from where the gull is and then from rings around it (over water, a roof edge or a wall it moves away). The run is shortened to the room there is.
- Skipped in online co-op and in browser tests (so the card shows at once); `?outro` turns it on in a test.
- Everything is in `tuning.outro` (enabled, landSeconds, runSeconds, holdSeconds, camDistance, camHeight, camSway).
- Test browsers also skip the polaroid photos and the card animations (software rendering stalled for seconds on the first photo); `?polaroid` turns the photos on.
