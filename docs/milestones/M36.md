# M36 — World enrichment W5 (start): a busier city

Branch `m36-busy-city`. Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W5).

## Built
- **Ambient birds** (`src/world/birds.ts`): small white birds (16 on high, 8 on low) circling lazy loops over the harbour, the parks and the squares; one instanced mesh, the wings flap in the vertex shader. Purely cosmetic, they never get in your way.
- **The tram is part of the city**: the Senate Square tram now runs on its line in every level (not just S4), stopping at the stop and ringing its bell when you are near. In S4 it is still the level tram (a bottle on the track stops it). Its roof is solid, and online guests see it too.

## Budget
Market view on high: 173 calls and 256k triangles (was 168 and 252k); low: 120 and 180k.

## Not done
Static crowds, ambient ships far out, cars (still not recommended without lane data).
