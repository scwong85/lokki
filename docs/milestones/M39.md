# M39 — Graphics G5 (part 1): actor art

Branch `m39-actor-art` (stacked on `m38-post-look`). Plan: `docs/design/graphics-refresh.md` (G5).

## Built (visual only, no logic changes; all in `src/world/senateFx.ts`)
- **Tram**: green body with a cream waist stripe, a row of window panes on both sides, two door panels per side, a glass cab with headlights and a cream nose, a roof pantograph, bogies. Collision and the solid roof are unchanged.
- **Pigeons**: rounder bodies, shimmering green necks, orange eyes, beaks, tails and wings; heads bob while they peck, and wings beat while they scatter.
- **Sunbathers**: striped towels, swimsuits, arms, legs and feet, hair colours and skin tones, a straw hat over the face while asleep and a book; woken ones sit up and put on sunglasses.
- **Dog**: floppy ears, eyes, nose, red collar, pale chest; legs swing when it runs and the tail wags (faster when running).
