# M120 — Playability: never lost, never guessing why

From the playtests: lost in a big stage (1.3), dive snatches failing without knowing why, blind waits for the ferry.

- **Row compass:** after `tuning.hints.compassAfter` (60 s) with no row found and none served, a "??? Something here" marker points at the nearest open row not found yet (`RowCompass`, `collection.ts`). Found rows still get their marker from the hint ladder.
- **Near-miss feedback:** a dive that came within `tuning.npc.nearMissRange` (4 m) of a held item and came away empty says why: "Missed by 0.6 m: aim at the hand…" or "Their body was in the way…" (`DiveReview`, `diveReview.ts`).
- **The waits line:** a pill under the top of the screen counts down to the next ferry (stages whose to-dos react to it) and the Warden's lunch (`waits.ts`, `Timetable.nextIn`, `Defense.lunchIn`).
- **Course "next" line:** when a course is served, the toast says where the next course's rows are (`courses[].where`, set for 1.1 to 1.5).
- **Relaxed play (Options):** people see 30 % less far, snatches reach 40 % further, heat rises 40 % slower (`tuning.assist`, `core/assist.ts`). Off by default.
- **Fly back (P):** after the Guard takes your loot back or a vendor sprays you, P (for 8 s) flies the gull back over the row you were working on (`tuning.hints.backOfferSeconds`). Listed on the Controls screen.
- **Bug fixed on the way:** the first level of a session had **no timetable** (no ferries, showers or gusts) until a restart or a level switch: the boot path never built it. The ferry now runs from the start.

Tests: `tests/unit/playability.test.ts`, `tests/smoke/playability.spec.ts`.
