# M31 — Roads, part 2: tram line, zebra crossings, manholes

Branch `m31-roads-b`. Continues M30 (W3 of `docs/design/world-enrichment.md`).

## Built
- **Tram line** on Senate Square (`TRAM_LINE` and `planTrack` in `src/world/roads.ts`): a dark bed, sleepers and two steel rails (1.44 m apart). It is there whether or not a tram level is playing; the S4 tram level now runs on it (`TRAM` in `senate2.ts` takes its position from `TRAM_LINE`).
- **Zebra crossings** on long straight streets (at least 44 m), 8 m in from each end, as white bars side by side across the asphalt.
- **Manholes**: a few dark covers on the asphalt of each street (up to four, kept a metre off the kerb), seeded so they are the same every time.
- Road markings sit a hair above the asphalt (`dy` in `flatLayer`) so they no longer fight with it for depth. Still one road mesh.
