# M29 — World enrichment W2: roofs, district colours, courtyards, lit windows

Branch `m29-world-w2`. Plan: `docs/design/world-enrichment.md` (W2).

## Built
- **District palettes** (`src/world/districts.ts`): wall and roof colours now follow where a building stands. Senate Square: pale yellow and cream walls with copper-green and grey roofs (Empire). Esplanadi: cream, stone and pale grey. Katajanokka (around Uspenski): ochre, salmon, grey-green and brick over dark roofs (Jugend). Market front: pale blues, yellows and white. The rest of the city keeps the old mix. Zones are hand-drawn boxes (`ZONES`); colours are from general knowledge, please check against photos.
- **Mansard roofs** (`src/world/roofs.ts`): 7 in 10 big blocks (8 m or taller, 110 m² or more) get a sloped skirt 2.2 m wide all the way round, courtyards included, and a flat top 1.3 m higher. The flat top is a solid landing slab, so a gull lands where it sees the roof; the sloping skirt keeps the wall's height (visual only). Roof clutter stands on the new top. `offsetRing` and `planMansard` are pure and tested; any footprint that will not offset cleanly keeps the old flat roof with a parapet.
- **Courtyards**: inner yards get gravel or lawn instead of street cobbles showing through.
- **Lit windows**: an emissive twin of the facade atlas, with a per-window hash so only some windows glow, driven by the sky preset (`windowGlow`: 0 at noon, 0.15 morning, 0.35 golden hour, 0.8 dusk, 1 night). The night levels now look designed.

## Budget
No new draw calls: everything is merged into the chunk meshes. Triangles are unchanged within 1 percent (market 241k high, 168k low). See `tests/smoke/perf.spec.ts`.
