# M112 — Stage 1.2 The Gull Picnic, version two (the content)

Plan: `docs/design/picnic-v2.md`. Engine: M111. The playtest of the first version (M107) said about 5 minutes; this is the redesign, and **the campaign's stage 1.2 is now this one**. The first version stays playable in the Playground ("The Gull Picnic (the first version)").

## The stage
One picnic on the roof of the market hall, in **three courses of two rows**. A course opens when the one before it is served; a served course gets a cheer, and the world moves on.

**Where everything is** (all on land, 55–113 m from the roof):

| Row | Owner | Where | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tablecloth | the Waiter | terrace by the Havis Amanda statue, west | 113 m |
| Rye bread | the Baker | north lane | 55 m |
| Herring | the Fisherman | east quay, float in the water | 56 m |
| Coffee | the Market Guard | his table on the south quay | 25 m |
| Berries | the two Berry Sellers | far east | 111 m |
| Vendace | the Nervous Diner | north-west kiosk | 66 m |

The sum of the round trips from the roof is more than **three times** the first version's (a test checks).

### Starters
- **Tablecloth.**
  - He only does his flourish when two guests sit at his table. Cinnamon buns lie by the clock, 100 m away: drop them by his table and the strollers come to eat them and sit down.
  - Then he counts 3, 2, 1 and tosses the cloth up: catch it in the air (E) and carry it home.
  - If you grab the cloth too early he bows to an empty table.
- **Rye bread.**
  - The bell is under an awning, so a splat only lands on the awning. Throw (Q) or drop something onto it.
  - The only bottles are by the statue, 100 m from the bell, so there is a carry.
  - She hurries over ("Coming!"), finds nobody, and the loaf on her stall is yours.

*Serving the starters:* the ferry horn sounds, a crowd of six comes ashore, and the **Market Guard** arrives and starts his rounds.

### Main course
- **Herring.**
  - Poop into the water by his float: a bite! He reels in and pulls up an old boot.
  - Carry the boot out over the water and drop it by the float: a **monster fish**, and he reels for 12 seconds with his back turned. Take the herring from the bucket behind him.
  - A second splat brings up a second boot (the secret).
- **Coffee.**
  - The Guard walks a loop round the south quay and sees what is in front of him. A honk draws him over to look (and while he looks he cannot see you).
  - Snatch the thermos from his table. If he sees you carry any picnic item he whistles and it goes straight back.
  - Fly home behind the hall and the tents, which block his sight.

*Serving the main course:* the lunch rush (eight more people) fills the square.

### Dessert
- **Berries.**
  - Walk into the blind spot between the sellers, who stand back to back.
  - When they find the punnet gone they blame each other ("YOU ate them!" / "I did NOT!") and **their shouting draws the Guard** (the new `noise` reaction). Slip out the other way.
- **Vendace.**
  - Honk from behind the Diner: he drops the cone.
  - The **pigeons race you for it**: take it within 4 seconds or they eat it and he buys another.
  - If you get it they **chase you to the roof**, and where you land with them the raid begins.

### The raid, and the gong
- The pigeons sit on the picnic and mob a gull that comes near. A honk or a cinnamon bun settles them.
- **If they are ignored for a minute they fly off with the last item delivered and drop it 38 m away.** The row un-ticks, and one trip brings it back.
- When everything is served and the raid is over, ring the dinner gong. The guest gulls tuck in, and every victim, wherever they are in the square, looks up and shouts at once ("MY CLOTH!" "MY LOAF!" ... "Caw.").

**Side orders** (second star): the Clean Freak, Honk! and Bottle Service, moved to the corners the rows leave empty. **Secrets** (two): Granny's good scarf as a napkin; the second boot. **Co-op:** Pass it on; Two seats at the table.

## Engine pieces added (beyond M111)
- **`PigeonRace` and `Raid`** (`src/gameplay/pigeonRace.ts`, pure): the race (racing, chasing, eaten), the arrival on the roof, the raid clock. The flock is drawn with the pigeon models.
- **`QuestLog.undo`** (a done step back to open; a won quest active again) and **`TriggerSystem.unfire`**, so a delivered row can be taken back and delivered again.
- **`Reaction.noise`:** a reaction can make a noise people hear (the Berry Sellers' argument).
- **`row.tag`:** the quest tag of the item a row takes, so the raid finds the last one.
- **`awning` decor**, and `LevelDef.pigeonRace`, `LevelDef.picnic.takeTo`.
- **Tuning:** `picnic.raidTakeAfter` (60 s) and a `pigeonRace` group; both read live.
- **Level list:** the first version of the picnic is `gull-picnic-v1` in the Playground.

## Known issues
- **Online co-op:** a guest does not see the course toasts, the late arrivals, the flock or the guest gulls (the host runs them). The quest state syncs.
- **Hand-tuned numbers** (whistle range, race 4 s, chase 40 s, raid 60 s) are first guesses and all live in the tuning panel (`guard`, `pigeonRace`, `picnic`).
- **Time:** my estimate for an expert run is about 7.5 minutes, and for a first play 10–11 (picnic-v2.md section 8). **Last time I was off by three, so the results card now lists the time per row**: please send it back.
- **A bun dropped on the terrace table** (not beside it) cannot be reached by the strollers.
