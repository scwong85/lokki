# M53 — Contagion, Slam and Peck (three more mischief toys)

Branch: `m53-contagion-slam-peck`. Design background: `docs/design/mischief-mechanics.md`.

## What was built
- **Contagion.** A fright (panic, startle, slip, soak) jumps to calm neighbours within 3.4 m after 0.35 s, up to four people deep. The first two rings drop what they hold and run; the outer rings only jump. Fires quest events `spread:N`. Tuning: `contagion.*`.
- **Slam + knock-down chain.** A dive at 14 m/s or faster bowls over whoever it passes (they slide the way they were hit and drop what they hold); the gull keeps 55% of its speed. A fallen person knocks over neighbours within 1.5 m (up to three generations). Events `slammed`, `knockdown:N`. Tuning: `slam.*`.
- **Peck + jukebox.** `LevelDef.switches`: E next to a switch (nothing carried) pecks it. The jukebox plays a tune and calm people within 20 m dance for 9 s, delighted and with their snacks open. Events `pecked:<id>`, `dancers:N`. Tuning: `dance.*`.
- **Levels** (series "Mischief (new)", no unlock needed): M6 Rush Hour, M7 Strike!, M8 Jukebox Hero, each a chain of splat/slam/peck, spread/knock/dance, take the cone, escape.
- Note: the vendor's spray cone also soaks the person being tugged, so they drop the bun (intended, emergent).
