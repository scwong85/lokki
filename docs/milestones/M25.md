# M25 — Co-op synergy core (F1) + juice pass (G1)

Branch `m25-coop-juice`. Plans: `docs/design/fun-todos.md` (F1), `docs/design/graphics-refresh.md` (G1).

## Co-op teamwork (two different jobs at the same time)
- **Taunt: decoy and thief** (hold **T**): the taunting gull squawks and wiggles, and everyone within 16 m watches it instead of the partner. A snatch under cover of the partner's taunt fires `coop:decoy`.
- **Mid-air pass**: toss (Q) or drop an item; if the partner is flying close with an empty beak it jumps into their beak (`coop:pass`; three in a row `coop:volley`).
- **Spotter and bomber**: a gull with no lock of its own gets the partner's lock (Tab) as a bomb sight; a splat on the marked person fires `coop:spotted`.
- **Rescue**: in co-op, a gull hit by spray, a line or a net is stuck for 6 s, unless the partner flies right up and pecks it free (`coop:rescue`).
- **Emotes** (**1 2 3 4**): wave, laugh, point, facepalm, shown over the gull on both screens.
- Tuning group `coop`. Logic in `src/gameplay/coopPlay.ts`.
- **Five Act 1 co-op to-dos now use these** instead of "both do it within five seconds": L2 Laser guided (spotter x2), L3 Good cop, bad gull (decoy), L4 Pass the parcel (three mid-air passes), L5 Wet wingman (rescue), L7 In his face (decoy).

## Online sync
- The **tram, Pigeon Gang, sunbathers, dogs and sprinklers** now reach the guest in the snapshot (`Snapshot.x`), and the guest rides the tram roof locally. Taunting and being tangled travel with each gull.
- New host-to-guest events: particles (`fx`) and emotes.

## Juice
- **Gull**: squash on landing, stretch on take-off and on a snatch, a happy wiggle when a to-do is ticked.
- **Emotes over heads** when people's mood changes (`!` noticed, anger when guarding or chasing, sweat when panicking, heart, facepalm), over woken sunbathers and barking dogs. Works on the guest too.
- **Particles** (one instanced draw call): feathers, crumbs, dust, stars, splashes, hearts, confetti.
- **Slow motion** for a moment on a snatch (single player only), a pop on the to-do note's "done!".
- Tuning group `juice` (`puffs` 0 turns particles off).

## Not in this milestone

- Two-gull carry, stacking and tug of war (F2); the full to-do pass for all levels (F3, F5).
- From G1: head tracking and blinking, dive wing trails, camera zoom on to-do completion.
- The debug second gull (`?coop`) does not get the co-op to-dos; they appear in online co-op.
