# M113 — The level list: old levels behind one button

**Goal:** the level selector stays short as the ten-minute stages arrive. The title screen and the in-game level select (L) list the campaign stages and free play; every older level (the Playground: the pre-arc Market Square levels, the toys, the trials, the Senate Square and Esplanadi levels, the old picnic) sits behind one **Playground** button, with a way back.

**Built**
- `levelsOf(all, 'main' | 'playground')`, `inPlayground(l)` and `playgroundButton(all)` in `src/ui/menus.ts`; the title list and `LevelSelect` render one view at a time and open the Playground view when the current level is in it.
- The Playground button shows how many of its levels are cleared.
- Tests: `tests/unit/stages.test.ts` (split and buttons), `tests/smoke/level-list.spec.ts` (title list: main view under 14 rows, playground over 30, back works).
