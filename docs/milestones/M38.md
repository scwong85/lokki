# M38 — Graphics G4: the post look

Branch `m38-post-look`. Plan: `docs/design/graphics-refresh.md` (G4).

## Built
- `src/render/post.ts`: the scene renders to a half-float, 4x multisampled target with a depth texture; one full-screen pass adds an **ink outline** (depth jumps relative to distance, fades into the far haze), a soft **vignette**, a small **saturation** boost and an optional **tilt-shift** blur, then tone mapping and sRGB.
- High preset only, plus an Options checkbox "Ink outline look (high graphics)" (saved). Low preset (phones) is untouched.
- Everything lives in `tuning.post` (outline, width, threshold, vignette, saturation, tilt, enabled).
- Draw-call counter still counts the scene plus the one post pass (Senate view: 85 vs 84 calls).

## Not done
Bloom (the cost on integrated GPUs is unmeasured; lamps and windows already glow from emissive colours), per-sky colour grading LUTs. Tilt-shift is off by default (`post.tilt` = 0).

## Measure on your hardware
Open F3, watch the frame time with the option on and off. If the outline costs too much, tell me and I will fall back to half resolution.
