# M12 — Level framework, two-gull-ready

## Goal
Turn the single Market Square level into a framework for many short levels, built from the start for two gulls and for online play (M14). No networking yet.

## Built
### Levels
- **Levels as data** (`src/data/levels/`): a level lists its to-dos (existing quests plus its own), secret `???` to-dos, world triggers, which tourists take part, whether the city is on guard (heat, vendors, lines, nets, the Warden), a par time and a skill reward. `src/gameplay/level.ts` (pure, unit-tested) builds a level from it, decides when it's cleared and awards stars.
- **Free play** (`sandbox`) is today's full game: all quests, the finale, extras and speedrun, skills per quest as before.
- **A preview level, "1. Snack Attack (preview)"**: snatch an ice cream and get away, land on top of the Havis Amanda statue, plus a secret to-do; a calm city (no heat meter, no deterrents). It proves the loop; M13 replaces it with the real levels.
- **Stars** per level: ★ cleared · ★ every to-do including the secret ones · ★ under the par time. Secret to-dos found after clearing still add their star.
- **Skills unlock by clearing a level** (the level's reward; the preview gives Strong wings). Free play keeps per-quest skills.
- **Per-level saves**: best stars, best time, number of clears, and the last level played.
- **Level select** on the title screen: Play continues the current level; the list shows every level with its stars; picking another level reloads into it (`?level=<id>` also works in the address).
- Secret to-dos show as **"???"** on the to-do note until done; the level-complete card shows the stars and the reward.

### Two gulls and online-ready state
- **Gamepad support** on the M11 input layer: left stick move, right stick look, LT brake, A/RB flap, RT dive, X grab, Y toss, B poop, D-pad abilities (left Caw, up Flock, right Pigeon, down Hawk-eye), Back skills, Start options. A plugged-in pad never fights keys or touch.
- **One beak per gull**: grabbing, carrying, tossing and pooping per gull; the physics world steps once for everyone. Events say which gull did what.
- **Who people react to**: tourists and vendors watch the nearest gull they can perceive; the Warden goes after the gull carrying stolen food within range, else the nearest; lines and nets catch any gull and track near misses per gull; hits name the gull they hit.
- **Debug second gull**: add `?coop` to the address. A second gull (juvenile look) joins, driven by the first gamepad, with its own beak and view direction. The camera stays on player 1 (split screen is not part of the plan; online co-op is M14).
- **Snapshots** (`src/net/snapshot.ts`): the whole game as compact data with stable ids (gulls, what each carries, props, people, heat, quests). A full market is under 8 KB, fine at 20 per second.

### Tests
`tests/unit/levels.test.ts` (level data, setup, clearing, stars, records, saves), `tests/unit/coop.test.ts` (two beaks, gamepad mapping and idle pads, who people watch, snapshots), `tests/smoke/levels.spec.ts` (load a level from the address, clear it, stars, reward, saved record, the title's level select), `tests/smoke/coop.spec.ts` (`?coop`: the second gull grabs and flaps on its own; the snapshot describes both).

## Deviations and notes
- **Switching levels reloads the page** (simple and robust); switching in place can come later if it feels slow.
- The level select is a styled list on the title card; the illustrated "postcard" can come with M13's real levels.
- **Free play keeps per-quest skills** so your existing progress still works; the real levels (M13) give skills per level.
- With two gulls, the **world triggers** that check where "the gull" is (ringing the ferry horn, landing on the statue) still only look at player 1; the second gull's physical bumping into props is also not simulated yet. Both are for M14.

## How to try it
- Title screen (or `?title`): pick **1. Snack Attack (preview)** or **Free play**.
- `?level=snack-stub` jumps straight into the preview level.
- `?coop` with a gamepad plugged in: a second gull.

## Exit criteria
- [ ] Free play plays exactly as before
- [ ] The preview level loads from data, can be won, gives stars and its skill, and the stars stay after a reload
- [ ] A second gull on a gamepad can grab, poop and complete to-dos alongside player 1 (`?coop`)
- [ ] Gamepad works for player 1 without `?coop`

## Playtest notes
**Signed off by the user (merged to main).**
- The controller works well, but menus (options, title, panels) can't be navigated with it.
- The controller needs a way to show its button mapping (for example, which buttons trigger the skills).
- Landing on top of the Havis Amanda statue is very difficult with the current controls.
- The preview level, the secret to-do and saving all work.
- `?coop` works: the controller drives the brown gull.
- Not tested on a phone this time.

Follow-ups: landing assists in M13 (levels need forgiving perches); controller menu navigation and a controls/mapping screen in M15.
