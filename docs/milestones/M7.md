# M7 — Market Square quest pack

## Goal
Each quest is distinct; a full level takes about 15-20 minutes.

## Built
Six quests built from the systems of M4-M6, a locked finale, and a level-complete screen. All quest content is data (`src/data/quests.ts`); the world triggers that feed them are data too (`src/data/triggers.ts`).

| Quest | What you do | Mechanic it leans on |
|---|---|---|
| **Ice-cream snatch** (timed 60 s) | snatch an ice cream, get away | tourist senses, shielding, distraction, escape distance |
| **Salmiakki swap** | steal the lilac tourist's sweets, then leave her a bag of salmiakki (black bags on the candy-stall counters) so she picks it up and eats it, and watch her face | tourists that *want* items: they pick up and eat sweets lying near them, then react ("yuck") |
| **Cap heist** (timed 45 s from the moment you take it) | take the student cap off the Havis Amanda statue (land on it, it is a perch) and drop it on the ferry's upper cabin roof | carrying + a delivery zone; the city notices (heat +25) |
| **Cinnamon bun ring** | carry a bun past the three vendors of the south row, in order | spray dodging; starting it puts the city on alert (vendors on, heat 20) |
| **Map sabotage** | steal the pink tourist's map, drop it in the harbour; she walks to the wrong ferry | snatch + the sea as a delivery zone + a forced destination for the victim |
| **Grand finale: market mayhem** (timed 120 s) | **locked until the seven quests above are done.** Land on the ferry's cabin roof to ring the horn; then collapse the chair stack (3 chairs), swipe the pastry from a stall counter, and poop on the Warden | everything: the horn puts the city at maximum alert (heat 80: vendors, lines, nets, the Warden) |
| Little extras (untimed, from the start) | poop on a tourist, tip a bin, smash a bottle, poop on the Warden | emergent gags |

- **Quest log upgrades** (`src/gameplay/quest.ts`, pure, unit-tested): locked quests that unlock when their prerequisites are won (with a "New quest" banner), per-quest timers, quests that start on a game event, ordered objectives, and a failed timed quest **resets itself after 3 s** so you can retry without pressing R.
- **World triggers** (`src/gameplay/triggers.ts`, pure, unit-tested): "a loose prop with this tag is in this zone" (ferry roof), "a map floats in the sea", "the gull stands on the funnel", "carry a bun through these points in order". They only fire for events a quest currently wants, so progress is never wasted.
- **Map markers:** a pulsing ring and light pillar point at the next objective (statue, ferry roof, the three vendors, the funnel, the pastry...).
- **To-do list** redesigned to stay short: active quests show their objectives and a tip, quests not started show a one-line teaser, finished ones are struck through, the finale shows a lock.

(Blind-date wrecker and Lost teddy were added after the first playtest, see below.)
- **Level-complete screen** with your stats (time, snatches, poop, smashed, tipped, times caught, dodges, peak heat). Enter keeps playing, R starts over.
- New props (bag of sweets, bag of salmiakki, tourist map) and a more general tourist brain (any item in hand, items they want, "yuck", a forced destination).
- Tests: quest pack rules, triggers, stats, markers; browser tests play the cap heist and the finale unlock/horn/level-complete.

## After playtest 1 (fixes and additions)
Feedback: quests distinct; not obvious that you have to dive to steal the map and sweets; finale unlocked but could not find how to swipe the pastry; level took a bit less than 5 min; hard to land on the ship and on the statue.
- **Stealing from guarded tourists is explained and easier:** the to-do tips for every steal now say what to do (dive from straight above, or make a noise and grab with E from the open side); the first time a tourist guards something a banner says the same; and **diving now curves toward a tourist's held item** (dive aim assist; the aim ring turns pink when one is on your line).
- **The pastry is marked:** map markers now show for **every** open objective, so the finale shows the chair stack, the pastry (follow the yellow pillar to the south-row counter) and the Warden at once.
- **Easier landings:** the cap lies on the statue's **wide plinth** (3.8 m perch) instead of the figure's head; anything dropped **anywhere on the ferry** (deck or roofs) counts as delivered; landing on **either cabin roof** rings the horn (was the 2.6 m funnel top).
- **Two more quests to lengthen the level (the plan's 5-6 quests plus finale):**
  - **Blind-date wrecker:** a couple waits at a clock on the square; he holds a bouquet. Swipe the bouquet, poop on one of them, watch them storm off in opposite directions.
  - **Lost teddy** (good-bird quest): a small child on the quay lost a teddy. It is in the Esplanadi park ~330 m west; find it, carry it back, drop it near the child and they hug it. (This one sends you across the map.)
  The finale now needs all **seven** quests.
- Tourists now notice a wanted item from 2 m away.
- **Playtest 2 follow-up (could not find the teddy or the couple; the flower man was under the clock, making a dive hard):**
  - **Waypoint labels:** every quest marker now has a name and a distance on screen ("Teddy bear · 325 m", "Blind date · 25 m", "Student cap · 131 m"); when the goal is off screen the label sits at the screen edge with an arrow pointing at it. The light pillars are taller and brighter.
  - **The teddy** is nearly twice as big and has a **red balloon** on a string, so it shows from far away. It is in the Esplanadi park, ~330 m west of the square.
  - **The couple** stands at the **clock on the quay, west side of the square** (about 25 m west of the start), and now 3.4 m either side of the pole instead of 1.4 m. The clock face is smaller and higher, so a dive onto the man never hits the clock. A test guards this.
- The to-do list hides one-line teasers on short windows.

- **Playtest 3: "the net makes diving impossible, even for the static ice cream".** True: the net covers the stalls and the square, so every dive from above crossed it, and under it (3 m of space) you could not start a dive because that needed 3 m of clearance. Fixed: the net hangs at **5 m** (was 4 m) and **dives now need only 1.6 m of clearance** (`dive.minHeight`, was 3 m), so you can fly in under the net and dive from there. Dives from above the net are still caught (that is the net's job), and the lines (3.2 m) and vendors still threaten a low dive, but it is possible; a test checks that a dive can start and finish under a fresh net without touching it.

## After playtest 3 (polish)
Feedback: flying under the net is still very difficult; a key item dropped by a swat/spray can be lost; owners should take items back; the bun ring is tedious (only walking, always sprayed); the sandbox breaks immersion; the teddy is too far for such an empty city.
- **Net:** now hangs at **8 m** on tall poles (was 5 m): lots of room to fly and dive under it. It still catches dives from above.
- **Lost items:** anything knocked out of your beak by a spray, swat or net gets a ring, a light pillar and a waypoint ("Dropped cinnamon bun · 57 m") until you pick it up again.
- **Owners take things back:** a stolen item that lands within about 2 m of its owner (while they are calm) is picked up again and guarded again ("They took it back!"). The sea is safe for the map, of course.
- **Bun ring:** the three checkpoints are wide (5 m radius) and reach up to 6 m, and vendors only spray below 4.5 m, so you can **fly past above the spray** instead of walking the gauntlet (or dodge the red fans). It now shows a start marker on the bun counters.
- **No sandbox:** the test area is gone. Buns lie on the middle-row counters, salmiakki bags on the candy-stall counters (north row), fish, hats and bottles on counters, street bins dot the square, a café chair stack stands at the west edge (finale), with a few crates beside it.
- **Teddy** is now behind the market stalls (about 70 m from the child, ~20 m north of the north row), not in the park.
- Fixed a flaky browser test (the bottle was dropped over stall canopies; it now drops over open water).

## Notes / deviations
- (superseded: blind-date wrecker and the good-bird quest are now built) Not built from §3.8: **Blind-date wrecker** (needs a pair of NPCs and a clock landmark) and the **good-bird quest**. The plan asked for 5-6 quests plus a finale, so five quests + finale + extras are in; the two others are good candidates for a later content update. The "Nemesis bombing run" is covered by the finale and the extras (poop on the Warden).
- The finale's "free the pastry" became "swipe the pastry from the stall counter" (it sits next to a vendor).
- Quest timers start on the first relevant event (spotted / cap taken / horn rung), so scouting is free.
- The level time (15-20 min) depends on how you play; the five quests plus finale are meant to be played in any order.
- Debug handle `window.__lokki` also exposes `notes`, `triggers` and `stats` for testing.

## Exit criteria (for the user)
- [ ] Each of the six quests feels distinct (different mechanic or twist)
- [ ] The goals and next steps are clear (to-do tips, markers, banners)
- [ ] The finale unlocks, starts on the ferry funnel, and the level-complete screen shows
- [ ] A full level takes roughly 15-20 minutes
- [ ] Nothing gets stuck (e.g. an item you need ends up unreachable): R resets everything
