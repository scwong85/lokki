# The Gull Picnic, version two (plan for review)

Status: **plan, nothing built.** Written after the M107 playtest. Read it, change what you want, and I build it.

## 1. What the playtest said

You played stage 1.2 (M107): **about 5 minutes, not 10**. Time per part, by my own estimate:

| | v1 plan | v1 as built (my estimate) | measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per row | 90–180 s ("find it, work it out, do it") | 35 s | |
| Whole stage | 12–16 min first play | about 3.5 min for someone who knows the tricks | **about 5 min** |

So my estimate was about 1.4 times too low, and the plan was about 3 times too high. The plan's "find it" and "work it out" time never happened, for four reasons:
1. **No travel.** All five places were within 40 m of the roof, so a trip took 3 to 4 seconds.
2. **One trick per row.** Once you read the nudge, a row was one move and one carry.
3. **All rows open at once.** You could wait for the ferry while doing the other five, so the waits cost nothing.
4. **Nothing was at stake.** No one chased you and a mistake cost seconds.

(You also knew the tricks from the plan I wrote, so a new player is probably somewhat slower. I won't count on that.)

## 2. The four changes

| Lever | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | everything within 40 m of the roof | owners 55–110 m from the roof and 60–150 m from each other: 5 to 12 s of flight each way, plus the legs between |
| Steps per row | 1 | 2–3 (make it happen, grab it, get it home) |
| Pacing | six rows open at once | **three courses of two rows**, each opening only when the last is served |
| Stakes | none | a **Market Guard** who confiscates loot he sees you carrying; pigeons that chase the cone; a raid that costs one trip |

The key idea is that a stage takes ten minutes because the player **travels, plans a route and gets caught**, not because there are more rows.

## 3. The map (all within the Kauppatori district)

- **Walking is slow, flying is fast:** the gull cruises at about 10–12 m/s, so 110 m is about 10 seconds.

## 4. The three courses

Each course closes when both its rows are on the blanket: two guest gulls land, a bell rings, and the next course's cast switches on. The note shows later courses as `🔒 Main course` and says what unlocks them.

### Course 1: Starters (calm morning, about 3 minutes)

**Row 1, A tablecloth: the Waiter (café terrace, west, 110 m)**
- ① **Make him serve.** He only does his flourish when guests sit at his terrace. Drop a cinnamon bun by the empty table (a bun lies by the clock): two strollers wander over to eat it and sit down.
- ② **Catch the cloth.** He counts 3, 2, 1 and tosses it up with a flourish; snatch it in the air (E).
- ③ **Carry it home** (110 m).
- *Reaction:* he bows to an empty table; the two new customers laugh: "…Monsieur?"
- *Uses:* crumbs, the flourish ritual, the air snatch.

**Row 2, Rye bread: the Baker (north lane, 55 m)**
- ① **Ring the bell without being there.** The bell hangs under the awning, so a splat only hits the awning. Throw (Q) something at it from the side. The nearest thing to throw is a bottle on the statue plinth, back by the café.
- ② **She hurries to the door,** finds nobody ("Hello? ...Who rang?").
- ③ **Take the loaf** from the back window and carry it home.
- *Reaction:* she counts her loaves and finds one missing, then looks up at the roof.
- *Uses:* toss, hearing, a watcher.

*(Route puzzle: the bottle is at the café, on the way from row 1 to row 2, so a good player combines the trips.)*

### Course 2: Main course (the ferry brings the crowd, about 4 minutes)

The ferry horn at the end of course 1 brings a crowd ashore, and the **Market Guard** starts his rounds (see section 5).

**Row 3, Fish: the Fisherman (east quay, 60 m)**
- ① **Fake a bite.** Poop into the water by his float. He reels in with his back turned and pulls up an **old boot** (it lands on the quay).
- ② **Use the boot.** Carry it over the water and drop it by the float: a big splash. He thinks it is a monster fish and reels in with his back fully turned for 10 seconds.
- ③ **Take the herring** from his bucket and carry it home.
- *Reaction:* "It's a big one!" ... a boot. Then: "And the small ones are gone too."
- *Uses:* poop as a signal, the boot as a tool.

**Row 4, Coffee: the Market Guard (his kiosk, south, 25 m)**
- ① **Draw him away.** Honk on the far side of the quay (hearing): he walks over to see.
- ② **Snatch the thermos** from his kiosk.
- ③ **Get it home unseen.** If he sees you carrying it he blows his whistle and the thermos goes back. Fly the route behind the tents and buildings, which block his sight, and drop it on the roof.
- *Reaction:* he comes back to an empty kiosk and a long, slow whistle.
- *Uses:* honk, sight cones and line of sight.

### Course 3: Dessert (lunch rush, about 3 minutes)

More strollers fill the square, and the pigeons are out.

**Row 5, Something sweet: the two Berry Sellers (far east, 100 m)**
- ① **Walk in the blind spot** between them (they stand back to back; the red rings show what they see).
- ② **Take a punnet.** They blame each other ("YOU ate them!" / "I did NOT!") and their shouting draws the Guard over.
- ③ **Slip out** while he walks toward the noise, then carry it home.
- *Uses:* sight cones, hearing, using the Guard's route against him.

**Row 6, The main course: the Nervous Diner (north-west kiosk, 70 m)**
- ① **Honk behind him:** he jumps and drops the vendace.
- ② **Beat the pigeons to it.** They race you for the cone: grab it in about 4 seconds, or they eat it and he buys another (try again).
- ③ **Carry it home.** The pigeons follow you to the roof and sit on the picnic: the raid.
- *Reaction:* "...Why is it always me."
- *Uses:* honk, a short race.

**After the six rows:** see off the raid (a honk, or a cinnamon bun) and ring the dinner gong on the roof. Every victim walks over and shouts up at the roof, as in M107.

## 5. The Market Guard (new person)

- **Why him:** heat, vendors and spray bottles are what stage 1.3 teaches (the ladder in `docs/design/arcs.md`), so I do **not** switch them on here. The Guard uses only what 1.2 teaches: sight and hearing.
- **Rounds:** he walks a loop between his kiosk, the quay and the tents, with a visible red sight ring.
- **Whistle:** if he sees you with a picnic item in your beak, he blows the whistle, everyone nearby turns to look, and the item goes back to where it was taken from. You are shoved and stunned for a moment. Only items on their way to the roof count: once on the blanket, an item is safe.
- **Noise:** a honk or a loud argument (the Berry Sellers) makes him walk to the spot and look, then go back to his rounds.
- **Not in course 1,** so the first two rows teach the new route-planning idea in peace.

## 6. Stakes, in order of cost

| Thing | Costs you |
|---|---|
| The Guard sees you carrying | the item goes back: one trip, about 30–60 s |
| The pigeons win the race for the cone | the Diner buys another: a retry, about 20 s |
| The raid is ignored for 60 s | the pigeons carry the **last delivered item** 40 m away: one trip, about 30 s |
| A watcher catches you | stunned and shoved: seconds |

Nothing is lost for good, and nothing gives a "failed" screen.

## 7. Side orders, secrets and co-op

- **Side orders** (the old Fish Market chains, one line each, for the second star): the Clean Freak, Honk! and Bottle Service. They move to the corners of the map the rows leave empty, so they give a reason to roam.
- **Secrets** (two): Granny's good scarf as a napkin, and the boot served at the picnic (a second boot: one from the first bite, one that stays).
- **Co-op** (required with two gulls): *Pass it on* (mid-air relay) and *Two seats at the table*, as in v1. A natural third: **decoy the Guard**, where one gull honks at him from the quay while the other carries a loaded item past his kiosk.

## 8. How long it should take

Seconds at expert pace, by leg (flight 10–12 m/s, from section 4):

| Row | Legs | Expert |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Tablecloth | fly 11 s, fetch a bun and wait for guests 30 s, wait for the flourish up to 20 s, catch 5 s, fly home 11 s | 75 s |
| 2 Bread | bottle 10 s, fly 6 s, throw 5 s, she walks over 10 s, loaf 5 s, fly home 6 s | 55 s |
| 3 Fish | fly 6 s, poop 5 s, boot 10 s, carry 8 s, splash and reel 10 s, herring 5 s, fly home 6 s | 60 s |
| 4 Coffee | honk 15 s, snatch 5 s, hidden route 40 s, drop 5 s | 75 s |
| 5 Berries | fly 10 s, walk the blind spot 15 s, snatch 5 s, slip out 25 s, fly home 10 s | 65 s |
| 6 Vendace | fly 8 s, honk 10 s, race 5 s, pigeons chase 15 s, drop 5 s | 50 s |
| Between courses, raid, gong | | 70 s |
| **Total, expert** | | **about 7.5 min** |

Multiplying by the 1.4 I measured from v1 gives **about 10 to 11 minutes for the main list**, and about 3 more minutes if you do the side orders. A first-time player, who has to find the places and the tricks, should be slower still.

**This is an estimate and I was wrong by 3 times last time.** So the build includes a **time per row on the results card** (and the run log), so the next playtest says exactly where the minutes go.

## 10. What it means for the other 19 stages

The table in section 2 replaces "more rows" as the way to make a stage long. For every stage: spread the places, write rows as 2–3-step chains, open them in courses, and put at least one thing in the way. I'd also **apply it to 1.1 First Light** (at 3 minutes it is the shortest stage, and the first one a new player meets) once the picnic works.
