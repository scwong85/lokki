# Level design brainstorm: Katajanokka & the harbour

Status: **brainstorm / proposal**, nothing built. Same rules as the other districts (`levels-kauppatori.md`): about 5 minutes per level, a headline puzzle, 2–4 to-dos, a hidden `???` to-do, precision "low" or "medium" only.

## 1. The place

Katajanokka is the island neighbourhood just east of the Market Square, across a short bridge; Uspenski Cathedral stands on its hill. It is **already inside our city map data** (we restyled Uspenski in M10). What makes it different:

- **The cruise and ferry terminals**: huge ships at the quay, passengers with luggage, a check-in hall, a baggage belt, security scanners.
- **Harbour cranes** and stacks of containers.
- **A big Ferris wheel** on the waterfront, and a **floating sea pool** with saunas.
- **Fishermen** on the piers, **sailing ships** visiting, and in winter, **icebreakers** and a frozen harbour.
- Strong **sea wind**, waves and ship wakes.

Theme of the district: **"All aboard!"** The market is about stealing, Senate Square about making a scene, Espa about stealth. Katajanokka is about **moving things and the sea**: ride, drift, swim and stow away.

## 2. New mechanics (location-specific)

| Mechanic | How it works | Why it's fun |
|---|---|---|
| **Moving platforms, big and slow** | The Ferris wheel (rotating cabins), the baggage belt (a conveyor), crane hooks (lifting and swinging on a schedule), docked ships' decks | Rides and timing that are slow and readable, never twitchy |
| **Ship horns** | Departing ships blow their horns on a timetable shown on the terminal board. Everyone nearby covers their ears and looks at the ship | A harbour version of the Senate Square bells, with a timetable |
| **Waves and wakes** | When a ship moves, its wake pushes floating things (and a swimming gull) toward the quay or out to sea | Floating items become a delivery system; ties into M10's floating |
| **Fishing lines** | Fishermen's lines can be grabbed: a **tug-of-war** with a clear tension bar (hold, let go when it flashes, hold again) | A physical, funny minigame with low precision |
| **Sea wind** | Gusts come in clear waves, shown by flags, ripples and flying napkins, with calm pauses in between | Flight that is harder but readable; shelter behind ships and containers |
| **Security** | The terminal scanner shows what's inside bags (a funny X-ray view); guards check anything "suspicious" | A puzzle tool: find the right bag, sneak the wrong thing through |

## 3. New skills

| Skill | Earned in | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Plunge** | K2 | From the water, duck under for a few seconds: swim unseen, grab things that are sinking or floating just under the surface, pop up somewhere else |
| **Gust Rider** | K6 | Spread your wings in a gust or the updraft along a ship's hull to hover and rise without flapping (and without stamina) |

## 5. Gags for hidden to-dos and chaos

- Push a **beach ball** into the sea pool: water polo breaks out.
- Ride the **baggage belt** wearing a tourist's sun hat: passengers wave at you.
- Drop a **fish** into a passing **kayak**: the kayaker paddles in circles.
- Land on a **mooring post** next to a fisherman and copy his pose exactly.
- Blow the **foghorn** in perfect sunshine: everyone puts on their raincoats.
- Swap the **"Tallinn"** and **"Stockholm"** signs at the terminal: two queues swap places.

## 6. Precision check

| Puzzle | Rating | How it stays forgiving |
|---|---|---|
| Boot Swap | Low–medium | The tug-of-war uses a big tension bar with slow cues; losing just resets the line |
| Lifeguard Duty | Low–medium | Plunge lasts a few seconds with a breath meter; the ladder is a big zone |
| Date at the Top | Medium | The wheel turns slowly; holding onto a cabin is automatic once you land on it |
| Lost Luggage | Low | The belt is slow; tags snap onto bags |
| Special Delivery | Low–medium | The pick-up spot is marked and the crane waits a few seconds |
| Buffet Raid | Medium | The horn timetable is shown on a board; the horn lasts several seconds |
| Flag Day | Low–medium | Rigging perches are big; the flag line has a big grab zone |
| Against the Wind | Medium | Gusts come in clear waves with calm pauses; shelters are obvious |
| The Icebreaker | Low–medium | Cracks are drawn on the ice; the Warden follows you slowly |
