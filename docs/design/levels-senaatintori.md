# Level design brainstorm: Senate Square & the Cathedral (Senaatintori)

Status: **brainstorm / proposal**, nothing built. Follows the rules in `levels-kauppatori.md` (about 5 minutes per level, a headline puzzle, 2–4 to-dos, a hidden `???` to-do, precision rated "low" or "medium" only).

## 1. The place

Senate Square is about 300 m north of the Market Square and **already inside our city map data**, so it needs landmarks and props, not a new data pipeline. What makes it different from the market:

- **The Cathedral and its 47 steps**: a big white stairway where people sit, take photos and graduate. Steps are a slope for rolling things and a grandstand for crowds.
- **The statue of Alexander II** in the middle, a famous pigeon hangout.
- **Trams** running along the edges (Aleksanterinkatu), with bells and roofs you can ride.
- **The Cathedral bells**, heard across the whole square.
- **Big events**: graduation photos in spring, summer concerts on a stage, and the **Christmas market** in December (this is where Helsinki really holds it).
- Less food than the market, more **people doing something important** (photos, ceremonies, concerts) that a gull can ruin.

Theme of the district: **"Make a scene."** The market was about stealing; Senate Square is about **performance, timing and crowds**.

## 2. New mechanics (location-specific)

| Mechanic | How it works | Why it's fun |
|---|---|---|
| **The Steps** | Round things roll and bounce down the stairway along a few lanes (guide rails); people sitting on the steps react as things roll past | Physics comedy with low precision: you only choose the lane at the top |
| **Photo moments** | Photographers and tourists frame shots with a visible countdown ("3, 2, 1, click"). Whatever is in the frame at the click ends up in the photo, shown as a little polaroid pop-up | A natural, telegraphed timing window (medium precision); the polaroid is the joke |
| **Trams** | Trams loop the square on a timetable, ring their bell, stop at the stop. The roof is a moving platform; things dropped on the track get "delivered" to the next stop | A moving perch and a delivery system with a clear schedule |
| **Bells** | The Cathedral bells ring every few (game) minutes. While they ring, **everyone looks up at the tower**: a square-wide distraction window. A clock shows when they'll ring next | A planning puzzle: line up your heist with the bells |
| **The Pigeon Gang** | A pigeon faction lives on the statue. They swarm loose food (including *yours*), chase lone gulls off the statue, and can be **won over** | A rival group that can become allies |
| **Crowds** | Big events pack the square. Crowds move as groups, do "the wave", follow a leader, block paths | Herding and chain reactions at scale |

## 3. New skills

| Skill | Earned in | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Photobomb** (pose key/button) | S2 | Strike a pose: if you're in a camera frame at the click, the photo is ruined in a specific way (wings out, beak open, "the dab"). Some puzzles need a particular pose |
| **Pigeon Diplomacy** | S5 | Feed the Pigeon Gang once and they become a small squad: point at a spot and they swarm it (local cousin of Flock call, but on the ground and good at stealing crumbs, bad at flying high) |

Disguise from the market has a twist here: as a pigeon you can **join the Pigeon Gang** without them noticing.

## 4. Levels

Each level is about 5 minutes. The first few teach the new mechanics; the rest remix them with everything from the market.

## 5. Gags for hidden to-dos and chaos

- Roll a **beach ball** down the 47 steps into a tour group: an instant game of volleyball.
- Land on the **emperor's head** and stay still: tourists photograph "the emperor's new hat".
- Drop a **bun on the tram track**: the next tram delivers it to a confused man at the stop.
- **Ring the tram bell** while the graduation photographer counts down: everyone looks the wrong way.
- Swap the **"Quiet please"** sign at the library with the concert's **"Make some noise!"** sign.
- Make the **Pigeon Gang** do a formation over the square that spells… well, a gull shape.
- Get **confetti** from the wedding cannon onto the Warden right as he's being photographed.

## 6. Precision check (from the control budget)

| Puzzle | Rating | How it stays forgiving |
|---|---|---|
| Cheese Run | Low | You only pick a lane at the top; the cheese follows rails; instant reset if it misses |
| Class of 2026 | Medium | Long, spoken countdowns; the frame is drawn on the ground; the cap arc is slow and the catch radius generous |
| Bell Heist | Medium | A 6 s window, a visible clock, and an extra window if you ring the side bell |
| Lunch Express | Low–medium | The tram waits at the obstacle for several seconds; the roof is wide; falling off just means waiting for the next tram |
| Statue Makeover | Low | Big snap zones on the statue; pigeons can do the fiddly drops |
| Jumbotron | Medium | The camera pans slowly and the frame is shown on screen |
| Shhh! | Low–medium | A noise meter shows how loud you are; walking is always quiet |
