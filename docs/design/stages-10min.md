# Ten-minute stages: the campaign rebuilt (20 stages, 4 acts)

Status: **plan.** Stage **1.2 The Gull Picnic** (section 6) is built in M107 (`docs/milestones/M107.md`); the other nineteen wait for its playtest.

**Built differently from section 6 (M107), cheaper or clearer in play:**
- The market hall is a new pavilion (`hall` decor) on the open paving south of the tents, not the real Old Market Hall (that is 250 m away).
- The Captain's coffee: he leaves it by the bollard when the ferry comes in (the ferry itself does not move).
- The Diner's cone just drops (it does not fly in an arc).
- Granny's scarf: she dozes (a ritual), no sneeze.
- The second secret is the Fisherman's boot (the radio is too heavy before Fat bird).
- The pigeon raid never steals: it mobs you until a honk or a bun, then the pigeons stay as polite guests (open question 3: the gentle version).
- Bottle Service keeps its own ending (the tourist's ice cream).

**Playtest result (2026-11-21): the picnic took about 5 minutes, not 10.** Why, and the redesign (spread out, two-to-three-step rows, three courses, a Market Guard): `docs/design/picnic-v2.md`. For all 20 stages: a stage gets long from distance, chained rows, courses and something in the way, not from more rows.

**Open questions, decided for now (change after the playtest):** the headline clears the stage, and side orders and secrets are the second star (2); the raid is gentle (3); all rows are open at once (4).

Playtest note behind it: *"Each stage is too short."* The goal now is at least 10 minutes per stage. That means a richer world: more people, more things to pick up and interact with, and to-dos you discover on the spot.

A stage today is about **8 to 12 steps**. Each step takes 15 to 40 seconds once you know it, so a stage lasts 3 to 6 minutes. Only 1 of the 10 stages has a hidden to-do. Most stages have 0 to 5 extra items, and every one of those items is the answer to a step.

**Why it feels short:**
1. **Too few to-dos.** There are three chains, all visible from the start, so the list never surprises you.
2. **Nothing to find.** Every item and person in the stage belongs to a to-do, so there is nothing to explore. The world is a set, not a place.
3. **One small area.** Each stage plays in a patch about 40 m across, so the rest of the square is scenery.
4. **No midpoint.** Nothing changes halfway: no second list, no new event, no complication.

Acts 3 and 4 are not built as stages yet. Esplanadi has the 3 Playground levels from M24, and Katajanokka has design notes only.

### 1.4 World systems (what people and the city can do)
- **Senses and moods:** sight cone, hearing, moods with emotes, gossip (people warn each other), wary people.
- **Watchers:** someone who minds an item notices when it goes missing (the double take) and recognises it in somebody else's hands (neighbours: they march over and confront).

## 2. What a ten-minute stage needs

| Ingredient | Today | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline goal** (one funny outcome, shown at the top of the note) | none | **1**, with 5 to 6 discovered rows |
| **Rows to discover** (`???` that half-reveals near the place, then becomes a wish) | 0 to 1 | **5 to 6** inside the headline |
| **Side gags** (short visible victim to-dos, 2 to 3 steps each) | 3 | **2 to 3** |
| **Secret to-dos** | 0 to 1 | **2** |
| **Co-op objectives** | 2 | 2 (unchanged) |
| **Midpoint event** (the list grows, or the world fights back) | none | **1**, after about half the rows |
| **Finale beat** (every victim sees what happened) | none | **1** |
| **Areas used** | 1 (about 40 m) | **4 to 5 spots** across the district, each with its own cast |
| **People with something going on** | 4 to 15 | **15 to 20** (plus strollers) |
| **Things you can pick up** | 0 to 5 | **20 to 30**, and fewer than half of them are answers |

### Rules (from the M100 research, kept)
1. **Every row has a victim, a trait and a reaction.** The punchline is a person, not an item in a zone.
2. **Each row in a stage is solved a different way.** Two rows in the same stage never use the same trick.
3. **The world explains itself.** The half title tells you what is wanted, and the place shows the trait. The hint ladder covers the rest: nudge, then hint, then marker.
4. **Long comes from content, not from waiting.** No window opens less than every 30 s, travel between spots is under 20 s, and a mistake costs at most 30 s to retry.
5. **Any order.** All rows are open from the start. The midpoint event depends on how many rows are done, not which ones.
6. **Extra items are not filler.** Each pickable thing does something funny when used wrong. Put it in the picnic and the guests comment; drop it on someone and they react.

### Time budget (how we check "at least 10 minutes")
Per row: find it (30 to 60 s) + work out the trick (30 to 90 s) + do it and deliver it (30 to 45 s) = **1.5 to 3 minutes**.
- Six rows: 9 to 18 minutes.
- Side gags: about 1 minute each.
- Midpoint and finale: 1 to 2 minutes.
- **Expected first play: 12 to 16 minutes.** Par time (third star): about 9 minutes, for someone who already knows the stage.

## 5. The to-do note for a collection stage (layout)

The note today shows chains. A collection stage adds a **headline card** with rows.

```
THE GULL PICNIC                        2 / 6
Throw the flock a picnic on the Market Hall roof.

  ✓ A tablecloth ........ (the Waiter)
  ✓ Bread ............... (the Baker)
  ?  ??? …something fishy
  ?  ???
  ?  ???
  ?  ???

  ─ then: ring the dinner gong (locked)

SIDE ORDERS
  ☐ The Clean Freak: make him wash his sign three times
  ☐ Honk!: make the card reader drop her coffee

??? ··· 2 secrets
```

- A row stays `???` until you come near its place. Then it shows a **half title** (`??? …something fishy`), which is the existing `reveal`.
- When you start the trick (the first step happens), the row becomes the full wish: *"Get the fisherman's catch"*.
- When the item lands on the blanket, the row is ticked with the victim's name, and that victim's reaction plays down below.
- Hint ladder per row, as in the trials: a nudge about the trait, then a hint about the trick, then a marker.

## 6. The sample stage in full: **1.2 The Gull Picnic**

**One line:** It is a lovely morning, and the gull decides to throw the flock a picnic on the roof of the Old Market Hall. Everything on the menu belongs to somebody down below.

**Mechanics:**
- Available already: fly, dive-snatch, land, walk, carry, escape, rituals.
- New here: **poop, toss, honk**, and **sight and hearing** (the guard ring).
- Nothing else works yet (the M59 gate). Every row uses at least one of the new verbs or a world rule from 1.1, and no two rows use the same trick.

**The places (five spots, each under 20 s of flight from the roof):**
1. **The Market Hall roof:** the picnic spot (the blanket zone, a big landing slab).
2. **The café terrace** by the hall door: the waiter, the bakery window, the bell over the door.
3. **The quay:** the fisherman, his bucket, the ferry berth.
4. **The fruit stalls** under the orange tents: two berry sellers, back to back.
5. **The vendace stand** (fried vendace, the Market Square classic): the fryer, a picnic table with a nervous diner.

**Cast:** 18 people with something going on: the waiter, the baker, the fisherman, the ferry captain, 2 berry sellers, the vendace cook, the nervous diner, the sign polisher, the card reader, Granny with the pigeons, 3 queuing tourists, 2 photo tourists, a dockhand with a radio, and a child. Plus about 6 strollers, the Pigeon Gang (8 pigeons) and 5 guest gulls who arrive for the finale.

**Pickable things:** about 26.
- The 6 answers and 2 secret answers.
- 18 "wrong" things, each with a reaction: napkins, paper cups, a newspaper, a boot (from the fisherman's line), flowers, a ball, a sign, 3 bottles, a map, sunglasses, a scarf, a toy, 2 salmiakki bags, a crate (too heavy) and a chair (too heavy).

### 6.1 Headline: *Throw the flock a picnic on the Market Hall roof* (6 rows)

Rows can be done in any order. Anything you drop on the blanket gets a comment from the gull side of the roof:
- **Something wrong:** a guest gull peers at it and shakes its head. *"A boot. Lovely."*
- **A row's item:** the row ticks, and the gulls on the rooftops around the square start to gather.

#### Row 1: **A tablecloth** (the Waiter): ritual window plus air snatch
- **Near:** `??? …something to sit on` (shown when you come near the café terrace).
- **The trait:** Every 30 s the waiter shakes out a chequered tablecloth with a flourish (a ritual, 3-2-1). For 2 seconds it is **up in the air** and he is looking at the crowd, not the cloth.
- **The trick:** Dive-snatch the cloth while it is in the air. If you try while it is on the table he is watching, it is too heavy to grab from the ground, and he shoos you.
- **Reaction:** He lays the "cloth" down with a bow on an empty table, looks at his empty hands, then up: *"…Monsieur?"* The diners laugh.
- **Ladder:**
  - Nudge: "He does love a flourish."
  - Hint: "Take it while it is in the air."
  - Marker on the table at the next countdown.
- **Carry:** Light, so it can be flown up directly. It **is** the blanket: it lands spread out on the roof slab, and the other rows are dropped on it.

#### Row 2: **Bread** (the Baker): toss at the bell
- **Near:** `??? …something fresh` (shown when you come near the bakery side of the hall).
- **The trait:** The baker's rye loaves cool on a rack in the **back window**. She watches it, and she sees a gull landing on the sill. But whenever the **bell over the shop door** rings, she hurries to the front: *"Coming!"*
- **The trick:** Toss something (a bottle, a cup, a pebble) at the door bell. A thrown thing that lands on a switch sets it off (the M101 `hit:` event). She hurries to the front, and you take a loaf from the back window.
  - Wrong ways: land on the sill while she watches and she flaps a towel at you (a shoo). Honking makes her look straight at you.
- **Reaction:** She finds nobody at the door (*"Hello? …Who rang?"*), comes back, sees the gap in the rack, then a double take: *"I counted those!"*
- **Ladder:**
  - Nudge: "She is never far from her loaves, unless somebody is at the door."
  - Hint: "Ring the door bell without being there. Throw something."
  - Marker on the bell.

#### Row 3: **Something fishy** (the Fisherman): poop as a fake bite
- **Near:** `??? …something fishy` (shown on the quay).
- **The trait:** The fisherman watches his float like a hawk, and his bucket of herring sits behind him. **Any splash near his float** makes him think he has a bite: he leaps up, turns to the sea and reels in wildly for 6 seconds.
- **The trick:** Poop into the water by the float (wind drifts the poop, and the zone is big). While he reels in, take a herring from the bucket.
  - Twist on the second try: he reels in **a boot** (now a pickable thing; put it in the picnic for the guest comment).
- **Reaction:** *"It's a big one!"* … a boot, or nothing. He turns round to an empty bucket. *"…and the small ones are gone too."* The tourists on the quay laugh.
- **Ladder:**
  - Nudge: "Anything that splashes near that float gets his full attention."
  - Hint: "Make a splash by the float. From the air."
  - Marker on the float.
- **Carry:** The herring is heavy-ish, so you fly slower and lower. (Fat bird is the reward that fixes this later.)

#### Row 4: **Coffee** (the Ferry Captain): the timetable
- **Near:** `??? …something to wash it down` (shown at the ferry berth).
- **The trait:** The ferry comes in on the timetable (every 2 minutes). While it is berthed (40 s), the captain leans on the railing with his **thermos** and watches the passengers board. When the horn blows, he sets the thermos on the railing to steer, and the ferry leaves with it.
- **The trick:** Wait for the horn. The thermos is on the railing of a **moving ferry**, so take it while the ferry pulls out (an air snatch from a moving deck), or land on the roof and walk to it.
  - Missed it? The ferry comes back in 2 minutes. The **captain's ritual** is the horn countdown (3-2-1, *BWAAAP*).
- **Reaction:** Halfway across the harbour he reaches for his coffee. Then comes a horn blast of pure rage, and the passengers on deck point back at the roof.
- **Ladder:**
  - Nudge: "The captain needs both hands when the horn goes."
  - Hint: "Take it as the ferry leaves."
  - Marker on the thermos.
- **New item:** thermos (heavy-ish).

#### Row 5: **Something sweet** (the Berry Sellers): sight cones and blame
- **Near:** `??? …something sweet` (shown under the orange tents).
- **The trait:** Two berry sellers stand **back to back** at a double stall and see everything in front of them, with the guard rings showing. The only blind spot is **under the counter, between them**. They also bicker constantly.
- **The trick:** Land behind the stall, walk in under the counter (the sight rings show where you are safe) and take a punnet of strawberries from the middle. Honking gives you away.
  - A flying snatch works too, but whoever sees you shoos you, and the punnet goes back.
- **Reaction (neighbours):** Each seller thinks the other ate them. *"YOU ate them!"* / *"I did NOT!"* They argue loudly, and the queue watches. If they see the punnet on the roof later, they both point up: *"…oh."*
- **Ladder:**
  - Nudge: "They watch the front. Who watches the middle?"
  - Hint: "Walk in under the counter, between them."
  - Marker on the gap.

#### Row 6: **The main course** (the Nervous Diner): honk makes him jump
- **Near:** `??? …the main course` (shown at the vendace stand).
- **The trait:** The vendace cook hands paper cones of fried vendace to a queue. One **nervous diner** sits at the picnic table, hunched over his cone, and jumps at any loud noise. Tourists in the queue watch the fryer, not the table.
- **The trick:** Land close behind him (out of his sight) and **honk**. He leaps up, the cone flies up in an arc, and you catch it mid-air (an air snatch), or pick it up off the table before the pigeons do.
  - If you are too slow, the Pigeon Gang gets it. A second cone is on the way in 30 s, because he buys another (the comfort food station).
- **Reaction:** He lands back on the bench, looks at his empty hands and at the pigeons. *"…Why is it always me."* The cook pats his shoulder and hands him another. (The second cone is a free extra; put it in the picnic for a bonus comment.)
- **Ladder:**
  - Nudge: "He nearly drops it every time a pigeon coos."
  - Hint: "Get close behind him and make a loud noise."
  - Marker behind the bench.

### 6.2 Midpoint: **Uninvited guests** (after 3 rows)
Once three items are on the blanket, **the Pigeon Gang notices**. Four pigeons fly up to the roof and start pecking at the food.
- **New visible to-do:** *"Get the pigeons off the picnic."*
  - Honk on the roof scatters them. It's a gull-versus-pigeon gag: they scold you and leave.
  - Or drop a bun crust on Granny's bench, and the whole gang goes back down to her (crumbs).
- **If you ignore them:** every 40 s they carry one item off the roof and drop it in the square. The row un-ticks, and the item lies where they dropped it.
- **Reaction:** Granny, below, as they come back to her: *"There you are, my darlings."*

### 6.3 Finale: **Ring the dinner gong** (after all 6 rows)
- **The to-do appears:** *"Ring the dinner gong."* An old ship's bell hangs on the roof's flagpole. Peck it, or toss something at it.
- **What happens:**
  - The five guest gulls land around the blanket, and the gull takes the head of the table.
  - Down below, **every victim looks up** (the neighbours system, at roof range) and recognises their property.
  - The waiter, the baker, the fisherman, the two berry sellers and the diner gather under the roof, pointing and shouting all at once. The ferry, coming back, blasts its horn.
- **Punchline photo:** The polaroid is framed on the crowd of victims looking up. Caption: *"Table for six."*
- The stage is cleared, and the reward is **Fat bird**.

### 6.4 Side orders (visible from the start; count for the second star, not for clearing)
- **The Clean Freak** (the Sign Polisher; reuses *Bombs Away*):
  1. Poop his freshly polished sign. He scrubs, annoyed.
  2. Again. He storms off to the fountain for water.
  3. A third time, as he comes back. He gives up and sits on the sign, which earns a facepalm photo.
  This teaches poop with a forgiving target.
- **Honk!** (the Card Reader; reuses *Honk!*):
  1. Land behind the tourist reading postcards on the bench.
  2. Honk. She drops her coffee, and the cup clatters.
  3. Her shriek makes the queue at the vendace stand turn round (sight and hearing: the queue looks at the noise, not the fryer). Their rings show it.
  This teaches honk, and it is a second way into Row 6.
- **Bottle Service** (the Guard; reuses *Nosy Parkers*):
  1. Toss a bottle into the big bin by the hall. It clatters.
  2. The market guard walks over to look.
  3. While he is there, take the flag from his post and put it on the picnic. The guest gulls salute.
  This teaches toss and hearing.

### 6.5 Secret to-dos (2)
- `??? …a picnic needs music` (the Dockhand):
  1. The dockhand on the quay has a **radio** on a bollard. He takes it everywhere and turns it up when the ferry comes.
  2. Snatch it while he helps passengers with the ropes, which happens when the ferry berths and he turns to the bollard.
  3. Drop it on the blanket. It plays, and the guest gulls bob along.
  4. The dockhand hears his song coming from the **roof**: *"…that's my song."*
- `??? …the finest napkin` (Granny):
  1. Granny on the bench has a lace handkerchief she waves at the pigeons.
  2. Make her sneeze: she sneezes when something lands in her flowers. Drop or toss anything into her flower basket.
  3. The handkerchief blows away (wind), and you catch it.
  4. Put it on the blanket. She sees it flutter on the roof: *"…Is that my good lace?"*

### 6.6 Co-op (required with two gulls)
