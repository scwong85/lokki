# Arcs and stages: the new campaign shape

Status: **plan, nothing built.** Written from the planning note: four locations, five stages each, about five minutes per stage, several objectives per stage, each objective a chain of events, co-op objectives for every stage, mechanics and skills that unlock once and stay. Start with Act 1.

## 1. The shape

- **4 arcs** (acts), one per location: **Act 1 Kauppatori** (Market Square), **Act 2 Senaatintori** (Senate Square), **Act 3 Esplanadi**, **Act 4 Katajanokka** (harbour). (Assumption: the four locations we have designed; the Railway station and Suomenlinna stay backlog.)
- **5 stages per arc = 20 stages.** The last stage of each arc is the arc finale.
- **A stage is about 5 minutes** of to-dos: **three solo objectives**, each a **chain of 3 to 5 events** (the C1 to C3 chain format: the note shows done steps, the current one, `?` for the rest). A chain step is something a person or the city does *because of* the previous step (chain-levels.md).
- **Co-op objectives:** each stage also has **two co-op objectives** (visible only with two gulls, or listed as "needs a partner" on the note). They need both gulls (a handover, a pincer, a decoy and a thief). Solo play never needs them; they only add stars and a co-op badge.
- **Stars per stage:** clear (all three solo objectives) · hidden `???` found · par time. Co-op objectives give a separate co-op star.
- **Level order is fixed inside an arc** (stage n+1 opens when stage n is cleared); the arc map shows all five.

## 2. Unlock rules (mechanics and skills)

> Once a mechanic is introduced it is **available in every later stage** and may be used in puzzles. A mechanic that has **not been introduced yet is locked**: its control does nothing, it is not in the tips, no puzzle needs it.

- A **mechanic** = a verb or interaction the player learns (poop, tug, honk, taunt, hide, slam...). Each has an id, the stage that introduces it, a one-line tip, and the control that is gated (see section 6).
- A **skill** = a power-up (Strong wings, Hawk-eye...). Each stage may award one when cleared; it is **kept forever** (it already is: per-level rewards in the save).
- The **stage that introduces a mechanic is built around it**: a safe first use (no heat, no failure), then a twist, then a combined use (section 4 of the physics doc: the "teach, twist, combine" rule).
- **Unlock state comes from the save** (the highest stage cleared), so a new profile starts with only flying and snatching. Free-play "Playground" (section 7) can unlock everything for testing.
- The **environment is introduced the same way**: each stage opens with a short intro beat that shows one new place or actor (a marker flyover or a first-use tip), and keeps it from then on.

## 3. Act 1 in detail: Kauppatori, "The Market Square"

Theme: from one hungry gull at breakfast to the Warden's nemesis. Every stage uses the same square (so the player learns it), at a different time of day, with a different part of the cast switched on.

Notation: objective **A/B/C** = a chain; steps are numbered; "(co-op)" are partner objectives. Existing levels we can recycle are named in *italics*.

### Stage 1: First Light (teaches flying and snatching; time of day: dawn)
New environment beat: the quay, the ice cream cart, the ferry, Havis Amanda (just as a landmark, no landing).
1. **A. Breakfast** (recycles *Snack Attack*): 1 fly to the cart (marker) · 2 dive-snatch an ice cream from a tourist who is not looking · 3 carry it away until the escape bar fills.
2. **B. Souvenir** (recycles *Map sabotage*): 1 snatch the tourist's map (he turns angry, first emote) · 2 carry it over the water and drop it (big zone) · 3 the tourist wanders to the wrong ferry · 4 the ferry horn blows.
3. **C. Neighbours:** 1 land on the quay (big zone) · 2 walk to the bench and pick up the bun (E) · 3 carry it to the ferry roof zone and drop it (the other gulls cheer) · 4 a hidden `???`: swap the ice cream for the bun in a tourist's hand (the swap).
- **Co-op A (Handover):** one gull snatches the ice cream and passes it mid-air to the other, who lands in the delivery zone. **Co-op B (Two birds):** both snatch from two tourists within 3 seconds.

### Stage 2: Fish Market (poop, toss, honk, distraction; morning)
New environment: the market hall, fish stalls, bins, bottles, flags showing the wind.
1. **A. Clean Freak** (*Bombs Away*): 1 poop the polisher's sign (he scrubs, annoyed) · 2 again, so he storms to the fountain to wash · 3 while he is away take the bun from his stall.
2. **B. Bottle Service** (*Nosy Parkers*): 1 toss a bottle into the bin (a clatter) · 2 the guard looks at the noise · 3 take the herring from the stall he watched · 4 toss it onto the ferry deck (big zone).
3. **C. Honk!** (*Honk!*): 1 land behind the card-reading tourist · 2 tap T, she drops her coffee · 3 take it and drop it in the sea (the bird "fills a bin" gag).
- **Co-op A (Spotter and bomber):** one gull stands in front of the food blogger so she looks up, the other poops her dish within 5 seconds. **Co-op B (Relay):** pass a herring along three tourists' stalls by hand-over, never touching the ground.

### Stage 3: Strings Attached (heat and deterrents, tug, hide, switches; midday)
New environment: scare lines over stalls, the vendor with the spray, bushes by the hall, an alarm box on the wall.
1. **A. Under the Lines:** 1 find the scare lines (faint shimmer; Hawk-eye is the reward) · 2 fly under or around them · 3 tug the soup from the soup man (hold E on the ground) · 4 get it away with heat below the second step.
2. **B. Spray Tan** (*The City Strikes Back*): 1 stand near the rich tourist so the vendor sprays · 2 he is soaked, drops his sun hat · 3 take it and hang it on the statue (big zone).
3. **C. Peekaboo and the Button:** 1 hide in the bush (stay still) · 2 BOO the pastry man (T) · 3 take the pastry · 4 peck the alarm box (E) so the vendors run to it, then raid the empty stall.
- **Co-op A (Team tug):** both gulls hold E on the same big pretzel: their pulls add up. **Co-op B (Rescue):** one gull is tangled in a scare line, the other pecks the line free.

### Stage 4: Mood Swings (moods and the lure, stations, slip, dance; afternoon)
New environment: the café terrace, the ice cream stall as a "comfort food" station, the jukebox, the puddle patch.
1. **A. Map and Cone** (*Map and Cone*): 1 snatch the map (angry) · 2 lure the angry tourist to the stall with hold T · 3 he buys a cone · 4 snatch the cone.
2. **B. Wet Floor** (*Wet Floor*): 1 splat the pavement on the inspector's route · 2 he slips · 3 take his lunch.
3. **C. Jukebox Hero or Pied Piper:** 1 peck the jukebox · 2 four people dance · 3 take a dancer's ice cream · 4 `???` lead the conga to the ferry gate (*Pied Piper*).
- **Co-op A (Decoy and thief):** one gull taunts an angry chaser round the fountain while the other takes the cone. **Co-op B (Duet):** both taunt: the conga line doubles and reaches the gate.

### Stage 5: The Warden (contagion, slam, domino; dusk; arc finale)
New environment: the Warden's kiosk, the owl on the pole, the long queue.
1. **A. Rush Hour** (*Rush Hour*): 1 splat the first person in the queue · 2 the panic spreads to five · 3 take a dropped cone.
2. **B. Strike!** (*Strike!* or *Domino Rally*): 1 dive fast through the line (slam) · 2 knock down four · 3 take a cone, or push the first domino tile and let the row fall into the queue.
3. **C. Nemesis:** 1 take the Warden's sandwich at his lunch (umbrella down) · 2 he chases you (lure with T) to the fountain · 3 he steps on a puddle or into the domino row and falls · 4 poop on him (umbrella down) · 5 the camera finale.
- **Co-op A (Pincer):** one gull lures the Warden under the fountain while the other bombs from above. **Co-op B (Chain reaction):** two panics started at the two ends of the queue meet in the middle.
- Clearing it opens **Act 2** and awards **Quick bowels**.

## 6. How gating works in the code

- `MECHANICS` is a list: `{ id: 'tug', name: 'Tug', stage: { arc: 1, index: 3 }, tip: '...' }`.
- `Progress.unlocked(id)`: true if the stage that introduces it is cleared (or it is the current stage: it unlocks when the stage starts, so the stage can teach it).
- Gates are single checks at the verb: `honk()`, `tugTarget()`, `hiddenNow()`, `peck()`, taunt input, slam and knock-down, domino and contagion are **systems**, so they are gated by simply not being used in earlier stages (their triggers do not exist there), except the player-driven verbs above.
- Locked verbs fail silently except for a soft "not yet" cue the first time (so a player who mashes T in stage 1 is not confused).
- The tutorial/tips system (`tutorial.ts`) already shows tips by event; each mechanic's tip plays on its first use.
