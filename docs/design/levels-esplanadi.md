# Level design brainstorm: Esplanadi park (Espa)

Status: **brainstorm / proposal**, nothing built. Same rules as the other districts (`levels-kauppatori.md`): about 5 minutes per level, a headline puzzle, 2–4 to-dos, a hidden `???` to-do, precision "low" or "medium" only.

## 1. The place

Esplanadi is the long, tree-lined park that runs west from the Market Square (the Havis Amanda statue stands at its east end). It is **already inside our city map data**. What makes it different:

- **Two rows of big lime trees** along the paths, with lawns in between.
- **Picnics and sunbathers** on the lawns, **dogs** with their owners, **kids** with kites, balloons and frisbees.
- **Buskers** along the paths, and the **Espa stage** (a summer bandstand) with daily concerts.
- **Café terraces**, including the old glass pavilion restaurant at the east end, an **ice-cream kiosk** with a long queue, and the poet's statue in the middle.
- At the west end, a big **department store** (parody name, e.g. "Stockgull's") with display windows, a revolving door and a delivery bay.

Theme of the district: **"Lazy summer, sneaky gull."** The market is about stealing and Senate Square about making a scene. Espa is about **stealth and chain reactions in a relaxed crowd**: people half-asleep in the sun, dogs and sprinklers that set things off, and music you can time things to.

## 2. New mechanics (location-specific)

| Mechanic | How it works | Why it's fun |
|---|---|---|
| **Your shadow** | Sunbathers lie with their eyes closed. They wake up if **your shadow** crosses their face (the gull's blob shadow, which already exists, gets a real job). Fly high (smaller, fainter shadow) or come in from the sunny side | A stealth rule players can see and understand at once |
| **Dogs** | Park dogs chase gulls on the ground (fast) but not in the air. They **fetch** anything thrown, running straight through whatever is in the way (picnics, queues, buskers' hats) and drop it at their owner's feet | A living chain-reaction tool; "throw a stick" becomes a puzzle verb |
| **Sprinklers** | Lawn sprinklers switch on section by section on a timer (or when you peck the valve). People flee the wet lawn and leave their stuff | Clearing an area on a schedule, or on purpose |
| **Treetops** | The lime-tree canopies are **hiding perches**: nobody sees you there and heat drops faster. The two rows form "treetop highways" along the park | A stealth route above everyone's heads |
| **The beat** | The Espa stage plays music; dancers, buskers and some characters move **on the beat** (a visual pulse on the stage shows it). Some windows of opportunity open on the beat | Timing that's telegraphed and musical, never a split second |
| **Queues** | The ice-cream kiosk queue: each person follows the one in front. Move the first person and the whole line follows | Herding a crowd with one nudge |
| **Kalle** | The rival gull from the Senate Square finale lives here and competes for the same snacks | A rival who can steal your loot, or be tricked |

## 3. New skills

| Skill | Earned in | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloud Glide** | E1 | While gliding high, your shadow fades almost to nothing for a few seconds (cooldown): sneak over sunbathers and guards |
| **Air Snatch** | E7 | Catch things **in mid-air** with a bigger grab radius while diving: frisbees, tossed bread, falling ice-cream scoops. Lets you intercept things dogs, kids or Kalle are chasing |

## 5. Gags for hidden to-dos and chaos

- Drop a **lime blossom** on a sleeping sunbather's nose: they sneeze and sit up.
- Get the **dog** to fetch the **Warden's hat**, then the Warden chases the dog around the statue.
- Peck the **sprinkler valve** during the dance class: they keep dancing, now in the rain.
- Put a **coin** on the ground in front of the queue: everyone looks down at once.
- Land on the **guitar busker's head** while he plays: the crowd thinks it's part of the act and tips him.
- Steal a **balloon** and float up with it for a moment (the gull is surprisingly light).

## 6. Precision check

| Puzzle | Rating | How it stays forgiving |
|---|---|---|
| Strawberry Raid | Low–medium | The shadow is visible on the ground; faces are marked when you're close; a woken sunbather lies down again |
| Fetch! | Low | Throws use aim assist toward the marked target; the dog runs a straight, visible line |
| Clear the Lawn | Low | Sprinklers follow a visible timer; the valve box is big |
| Café Commando | Medium | Trees are big perch zones; guards look away on a clear rhythm |
| Underdog | Low–medium | The amp cable is a big grab zone; the beat is shown on the stage |
| Dance Floor | Medium | Spins happen on every fourth beat with a light flash one beat before |
| Intercept | Medium | Air Snatch's bigger radius, slow-falling objects |
| Line Leader | Low | Any noise or coin in the marked spot works |
| Window Shopping | Medium | The revolving door turns slowly and has wide gaps; getting stuck just pushes you out |
