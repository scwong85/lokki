# Act 1, version two: all five stages on the picnic formula

Status: **plan; 1.2 is built (M112), 1.1 is M114, 1.3 to 1.5 follow as M115 to M117, one milestone each.** Formula: `docs/design/picnic-v2.md` section 2 (spread out, 2 to 3 step chains, courses, something in the way, time per row on the results card). The old stages move to the Playground (M113 made the level list short).

## The formula, in one table
| Lever | Rule |
|---|---|
| Distance | owners 55 to 115 m from home base; at least 35 m between rows |
| Steps | every row is a chain: make it happen, take it, get it home |
| Pacing | rows open in two or three courses; a served course changes the world (a crowd, a ferry, a guard) |
| Stakes | something in the way: a chase, a guard, pigeons; a mistake costs one trip (30 to 60 s), never a fail screen |
| Tricks | within a stage, no two rows use the same trick; across the act, each stage teaches its own verbs (the gate ladder) |
| Report | time per row on the results card |

## 1.1 First Light: **Breakfast on the Roof** (collection, light; M114)
Verbs: fly, dive-snatch, carry, drop, land, walk, escape. Nothing else works yet (gate 1.1). Home base is the market hall roof, which the player meets again in 1.2. Four rows in two courses of two. About 8 to 10 minutes for a new player.
| Course | Row | Owner and place | The chain | Trick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early birds | Something cold | the Selfie Tourist, east quay (55 m) | wait for her 3-2-1 and dive on the 1; she chases; lose her; carry the cone home | a blind window (ritual) |
| Early birds | A sandwich | the Dockhand, west quay (75 m) | take the bun from the bench far west, drop it on his spotless quay, he marches over to scold it, take his sandwich, carry home | a lure (a mess) |
| Second helping | Coffee | the Captain, the ferry berth | the ferry horn sends him to the gangway; his thermos stays by the bollard; take it | the timetable |
| Second helping | A sun hat | the Dozing Tourist, far north-east bench (110 m) | he nods off every half minute (the 💤); take the hat from his head while he dozes; carry home | a second window, but long and passive: you wait, not time a dive |
After the four rows: ring the breakfast gong on the roof (a peck). The victims look up. Reward: Strong wings (as before).
Side orders (the old chains, one line each, spread to the corners): Map and Cone is not here (the taunt is 1.4's); the **map into the sea** and the old **bun voyage** stay in the Playground. One secret: a seagull-shaped weathervane... (decided when built).
Co-op (required with two gulls): *Pass it on* and *Two birds*, as before.

## 1.3 Market Day Heist (heist; M115)
Layers of defence around the soup man's cauldron: scare lines (strings), a spray vendor, heat, the tug, hide and BOO, the peck buttons. Rows = the layers (cut the strings by tugging, draw the vendor away with a button, hide from the guard, take the ladle). New: heat, tug, hide, peck buttons.
