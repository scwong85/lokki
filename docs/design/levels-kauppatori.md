# Level design brainstorm: Kauppatori (Market Square)

Status: **brainstorm / proposal**. Nothing here is built. Pick, cut and reorder freely; once agreed, it becomes milestones in `docs/PLAN.md`.

## 1. What changes from today

Today the Market Square is **one big level**: eight quests on one to-do list, skills unlocked per quest. This proposal turns it into **a run of short levels ("days at the market")**:

- Each level is one visit of **5–12 minutes** with its own to-do list, cast, props, deterrents and time of day.
- The first levels each **teach one mechanic** through a small, funny puzzle. Later levels **mix** everything.
- **Skills unlock when you finish a level** (not per quest), and the next level is built to show the new skill off.
- Every level has **one headline puzzle** (the thing you'll remember) plus a few smaller tasks, so it never feels like a list of fetch quests.
- Other districts later bring **their own mechanics, skills and puzzles** (section 8).

## 2. Design rules for every level

1. **One headline puzzle** with a funny payoff, solved with the mechanics you have. It should have an "aha", not just "go there, bring that".
2. **3–5 to-dos**, mixing kinds: the headline puzzle, one or two small tasks, one sandbox gag ("make three people open umbrellas") and **one hidden to-do** that shows as `???` until you discover it (the Untitled Goose Game trick that rewards curiosity).
3. **Introduce, then twist.** A new mechanic is first safe and obvious, then used in a surprising way inside the same level.
4. **Ban plain fetch quests.** If a task is "take X to Y", something must stand in the way: a person who wants it, a guard, a timer, a physics problem, a trick.
5. **Systemic first.** Puzzles are built from the existing systems (NPC senses and wants, noise, heat, physics props, poop, triggers), so players can solve them in more than one way and invent their own gags.
6. **Kind and silly.** No one gets hurt. People get splatted, confused, grossed out or outsmarted, and the gull is always the cheeky one.
7. **Replay reward.** After clearing a level: a speedrun time, a "?" hidden to-do and extra objectives (the system M8 already has).

### Puzzle toolbox (mix these so levels feel different)

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Distraction** | Make noise or show food elsewhere so the guard looks away |
| **Timing / pattern** | Strike when the tourist raises the phone for a selfie |
| **NPC wants** | Leave something a person wants, so they put down what *you* want |
| **Swap / switcheroo** | Replace the sweets with salmiakki, the herring with a sock |
| **Chain reaction** | Splat → vendor cleans → stall unguarded → snatch → tourists cheer → ... |
| **Physics** | Tip the chair stack, knock the bottle off the ledge, build a pile to reach a spot |
| **Stealth** | Stay out of sight cones; hide under the tents and nets |
| **Herding / luring** | Calls and noises steer people into a spot |
| **Misdirection / framing** | Make the vendor blame the pigeons, or the Warden blame the rival gull |
| **Good bird** | Help someone in a funny roundabout way (return the teddy, deliver the ring) |
| **Escort / delivery** | Float a bottle message to the ferry, bring the bun past three vendors |
| **Environment** | Ferry horn, wind gusts, sprinklers, tide, snow footprints |

## 3. Act 1: "Fledgling" — one new mechanic per level

| # | Level | Teaches | Headline puzzle (the funny bit) | Other to-dos | Hidden `???` | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Snack Attack** | Walk, hop, flap, glide, land, dive-snatch, carry | **"Say cheese!"** A tourist guards an ice cream perfectly, except when she does a selfie: "3, 2, 1…" and her arm goes up. Dive on "1". The selfie she gets is your face | Steal a dropped chip from the ground · grab a bun from an empty café table · eat your loot on a rooftop (land on it) | Photobomb a selfie *before* stealing anything | Unlocks level 2 |
| 2 | **Bombs Away** | Poop, the landing ring, wind drift | **"The Clean Freak"**: a herring vendor polishes his shiny sign whenever it gets splatted, turning his back on his plate of fried vendace. Splat the sign, snatch the plate. Every splat makes him more frantic (and slower to turn back) | Hit the moving tour group's guide flag · make three people open umbrellas in sunshine (a splat near someone makes them open one; others copy them) · splat while the wind is blowing (flags show it) | Splat the freshly washed tour boat | **Quick bowels** (poop recharges faster) |
| 3 | **Nosy Parkers** | Seeing and hearing: sight cones, the "!" and "?", noise distractions | **"Bottle Service"**: a tourist with an ice cream won't look away. Knock a bottle off a ledge (or tip a bin) behind her. While she turns to look, snatch it | Steal the guard dog's (owner's) biscuit without being seen · sneak a whole picnic sandwich away one hop at a time under a table | Make the tourist look at a noise *while* another tourist's food is also unguarded: double snatch | **Mimic** (crow call) |
| 4 | **Butterfingers** | Grab, carry, drop, toss; people want things and react | **"Bait and Switch"**: a food blogger photographs every dish before eating. Swap her salmon soup for a raw herring while she adjusts her camera. Her review: "0 stars, tastes like the Baltic" | Salmiakki swap (today's quest, moved here) · bring the lost teddy back to the child · toss a bun to a sad toddler (good bird) | Get the blogger to photograph *you* | **Strong wings** (bigger flaps, slower sink) |
| 5 | **The City Strikes Back** | Heat, vendors with spray bottles, scare lines, "gulls learn" | **"Spray Tan"**: the angry vendor sprays at gulls, wherever they are. Perch on the *rich tourist's* shiny top hat, so the spray soaks the tourist, who storms off and leaves his lunch | Get the cinnamon bun past three vendors (today's bun ring) · slip through the scare lines five times until the gulls "learn" them (the city reacts) · reach heat 60 and escape | Make two vendors spray each other | **Hawk-eye** upgrade |
| 6 | **Owl Be Back** | Nets, the plastic owl decoy (from the plan, new), learning to fly under nets | **"Borrowed Authority"**: the city installs a plastic owl that scares gulls. Grab it (it's light) and put it on the pigeons' bench: the pigeons flee, and so does the pigeon-feeding granny… with her bag of bread left behind. Then put it on the *vendor's own counter*: his customers think it's real and he panics | Fly under the net three times · knock the net down from above by tipping its pole | Wear the owl (carry it while gliding: everyone runs) | **Fat bird** (carry heavy things) |
| 7 | **The Warden** | The nemesis, his umbrella, his rounds | **"Lunch Break"**: the Warden takes his lunch at 12:00 sharp on the same bench (a clock on the tower shows the time). Poop or snatch | Steal his whistle and blow it (everyone stops dead for 3 s: freeze-frame gag) · make him chase you into a nets maze where he gets tangled | Make him fall asleep on the bench (bring him the warm cinnamon bun and a coffee) and steal his hat | **Disguise** (pigeon) |
| 8 | **Harbour Day** | Swimming, things float, the ferry and its horn, boats | **"Message in a Bottle"**: a child on the ferry dropped a note. The wind blows toward the ferry only when it's about to leave. Get the bottle into the water at the right moment so it floats to the ferry before the horn | Map sabotage (today's quest) · ride the ferry roof across the harbour and back (moving platform, new) · steal a fish from a fishing line: the fisherman tugs back (tug-of-war) | Swim next to the duck family as if you were one of them (they adopt you) | **Flock call** |

After Act 1, every core mechanic and six skills are introduced.

## 4. Act 2: "Showing off" — one skill per level, puzzles that need it

| # | Level | Skill in focus | Headline puzzle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Caw of the Wild** | Mimic | **"Group Photo"**: a tour guide is trying to get her 12 tourists into one photo in front of the Havis Amanda statue. They keep wandering. Use crow calls to herd them into the frame… then splat the photographer at the click. Later twist: mimic a phone ringtone so the guide answers and drops the flag |
| 10 | **The Invisible Maze** | Hawk-eye | **"Laser Heist"**: the "Golden Ice Cream" (a prize in the ice-cream contest) sits behind invisible scare lines strung like heist lasers. Hawk-eye shows them for a few seconds: memorise, then fly the gap. Pink Panther energy (music switches to the playful track) |
| 11 | **Heavy Lifting** | Fat bird | **"Café Tower"**: a cinnamon bun is on the café awning, too high to land next to. Carry café chairs and crates and **stack** them into a perch tower. Physics comedy when it wobbles. Twist: the waiter keeps taking chairs back |
| 12 | **Gull's Eleven** | Flock call | **"The Big Heist"**: three vendors guard three stalls. You have one flock call (four decoy gulls) and a 60-second window. | 13 | **Coo Coo Undercover** | Disguise | **"Pigeons Only"**: the Warden lets pigeons eat crumbs in the café garden but chases gulls. Disguised, you must *act* like a pigeon: walk, head-bob (bob key/button), no flying, or the disguise breaks. Get to the café counter and steal the whole cake |
| 14 | **Thermal Express** | Strong wings + thermals (from the plan, new) | **"Express Delivery"**: deliver a warm cinnamon bun from Kauppatori to the top of the Uspenski onion dome before it goes cold (a steam meter). Ride warm rooftops' thermals to gain height; the route is the puzzle |

## 5. Act 3: "Market days" — remix levels with themes

All mechanics are available. Each day changes the dressing, the cast and the rules, so the same square feels new.

| # | Level | Twist / new rule | Headline puzzle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | **Vappu Eve** (30 April) | Huge crowd in student caps; balloons; the 18:00 capping of Havis Amanda | **"Cap Race"**: the students' crane lifts a cap onto the statue at 18:00. Get there first… and put the cap on the **Warden** instead. Balloons can be popped by poop for distraction |
| 16 | **Herring Market** (autumn) | Fishermen's boats moored along the quay; jars everywhere | **"Grandma Wins"**: the herring contest jury tastes jars blind. Swap jars so the sweet grandma beats the arrogant champion. Hidden: swap in a jar of salmiakki and watch the jury's faces |
| 17 | **Wedding Bells** | A wedding party on the Uspenski steps; photographer | **"Something Borrowed"**: the ring bearer (a toddler) lost the ring box. Find it (it's shiny: crows… gulls love shiny), deliver it to the altar steps. Good-bird level with chaos options: catch the bouquet mid-air |
| 18 | **Christmas Market** (winter) | Snow: **footprints give you away** (people follow your tracks); slippery ice; glögi steam | **"Santa's Little Helper"**: the market Santa's sack of gifts. Swap the cheap gifts with the good ones so the kids get the best toys, while vendors in hats track your footprints. Option: walk only on the shovelled paths |
| 19 | **The Rival** | **Kalle**, a rival gull (new AI) who competes for the same snacks | **"Snack Duel"**: first to three snacks wins. Frame Kalle: lead the vendor's spray onto him, or make him fly into a net. Ends with a truce (he joins your flock) |
| 20 | **Night Market** | Dusk lighting, lanterns, fewer tourists but sleepy vendors | **"Midnight Snack"**: stealth level. Sleeping vendors wake at loud noises; snores show how deep they sleep. Unhinged option: knock the lantern string so all the lights go out |
| 21 | **The Drone** | The plan's final deterrent: a gull-hunting drone | **"Battery Low"**: the drone follows you. Lead it under the nets, into the fountain spray, or make it chase Kalle. Steal its battery when it lands to recharge |
| 22 | **Grand Finale: The Great Kauppatori Heist** | Everything; the city at maximum alert | Multi-stage plan (today's finale, expanded): ring the ferry horn, collapse the chair stack, swipe the pastry, poop on the Warden, and escape on the departing ferry. The ending: the city makes you the official market mascot (a nod to the real litter-free gull campaign) |

### Unhinged gags to sprinkle in (hidden to-dos or optional chaos)

- Steal the **brass band conductor's baton**: the band breaks into *Galop infernal* (our chaos music track) and everyone dances.
- Drop a fish in a tourist's **open umbrella**: they open it in the rain and get a fish on the head.
- Blow the Warden's **whistle** in the middle of the market: everyone freezes, mid-bite.
- Push a **beach ball** into the tour group and start a ball game.
- Land on the **statue's head** and stay perfectly still: tourists take photos of "the statue's hat".
- Swap the **"Fresh fish" sign** with the **"Ice cream" sign**: watch the queues swap.
- A tourist ordering at the counter; you **steal the receipt**, the vendor refuses to serve them, and they blame each other.
- **Seagull union**: once you've done enough mischief, three gulls picket the vendor with tiny signs (cosmetic finale gag).

## 6. Progression and skills

- **Skills unlock by finishing levels** (replacing today's one-skill-per-quest). Proposed order: L2 Quick bowels · L3 Mimic · L4 Strong wings · L5 Hawk-eye upgrade · L6 Fat bird · L7 Disguise · L8 Flock call. Act 2 then builds a level around each.
- **Stars per level:** clear the level · all to-dos including the `???` one · beat the par time. Stars unlock the remix days and gull looks (the look unlocks from M10 move to star counts).
- **Level select:** a pastel postcard of the Market Square with pins for each level, the season shown on the card.
- Replays keep the M8 extras and speedrun, per level.

Suggested build order once agreed: **level framework + level select + Act 1 levels 1–4**, then Act 1 levels 5–8, then Act 2, then the themed days. Each would be its own milestone with a playtest gate.
