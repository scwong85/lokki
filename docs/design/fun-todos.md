# Fun to-dos, co-op synergy and shareability: plan

Status: **proposal**, nothing built. Goal: make every level funnier, make co-op need real teamwork, and make moments people want to clip and share. Companion to `graphics-refresh.md`.

A caveat up front: nobody can plan a viral hit. What we can do is build the things shared games tend to have: a premise you get in one sentence, moments that are funny without sound or context, co-op chaos that makes two people laugh on a call, and a one-click way to share a clip.

## 1. What makes a to-do fun (rules for every new one)

1. **A victim with a reaction.** The joke is the person's face, not the tick. Every to-do ends in a visible reaction (emote, pose, chase, group gasp).
2. **A plan, then a mess.** The best to-dos have a clever intended route and a funnier failure.
3. **Readable in a 6-second clip.** If a stranger can't tell what happened with the sound off, redesign it.
4. **One verb twist per level.** Reuse the core verbs (snatch, carry, drop, poop, perch, call) with one new rule.
5. **Chains pay double.** A to-do that sets off a second thing (dog → picnic → pigeons) is worth more than two separate ones.
6. **Named like jokes.** The title is the punchline and is what people quote.

## 2. Co-op today, and why it is thin

Co-op twists currently reuse three events: both gulls snatch within 5 s, both splat within 5 s, or hand an item over. They reward doing the same thing twice, not working together. Two players never *need* each other.

## 3. Co-op synergy toolkit (new shared mechanics)

Each is a small system that many to-dos can use. Sizes: S ≈ 1 session, M ≈ 2–3.

| # | Mechanic | How it works | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Two-gull carry** | Heavy things (a whole cake, a salmon, a bench cushion, a toddler's pram *umbrella*) need both beaks. Both grab, the item hangs between you on an elastic link; fly apart and it stretches and drops. Steering is shared: comedy by design | M |
| C2 | **Decoy and thief** | NPCs track the *most suspicious* gull (already built). Add a **Taunt** (hold call): the taunting gull holds all attention in a cone while the other is invisible to those NPCs. A shared "attention" meter shows who is being watched | S |
| C3 | **Mid-air pass** | Toss an item (Q) and the other gull catches it in flight (generous radius, slow-motion beat on a catch). Counts passes; chains of 3+ get a "volley" bonus. Relay races over nets and lines | S |
| C4 | **Stack** | One gull perches on the other's back (land on your partner). The pair is taller: reach high shelves, look like "a very tall bird" disguise, double-weight for pressure plates and see-saws | M |
| C5 | **Pincer scare** | Two gulls screeching from opposite sides make an NPC spin and drop everything; from the same side they just walk away. Herding crowds needs one gull each flank | S |
| C6 | **Bomber and spotter** | One gull marks a target with the lock; the other's poop homes harder on marked targets and ignores the Warden's umbrella if the spotter is in his face. Roles swap freely | S |
| C7 | **Tug of war** | Both grab the same item from opposite ends: it tears (sausage chain, scarf, newspaper) or pings one of you backwards. Also against NPCs: one pulls the hat, one pulls the bag | S |
| C8 | **Revive and rescue** | A netted, soaked or dog-chased gull is stuck for a few seconds unless the partner pecks them free. Turns deterrents into team moments instead of solo penalties | S |
| C9 | **Friendly rivalry** | Optional per-level score race (who stole more, who got splatted) on the level-complete card, with silly awards: "Butterbeak", "Most Wanted", "Human Shield". Awards are the share bait | S |
| C10 | **Emote wheel** | Four quick emotes (wing wave, dance, point, facepalm) so two players can clown and signal without voice | S |

**Design rule for co-op to-dos:** each must need **two different roles at the same time** (decoy/thief, lifter/steerer, marker/bomber, thrower/catcher). No to-do may be "do the solo thing twice".

## 5. Share bait (the "viral" part)

Built so every funny moment can leave the game in one click.

| # | Feature | What it does | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Instant replay GIF/clip** | The game keeps the last ~8 s of gull, NPC and prop state (the snapshot system exists). On a to-do completion or a big chain, offer "Save clip": re-render it with a cinematic camera and export a short WebM/GIF with the to-do title as caption. Fully client-side | M |
| V2 | **Polaroids and album** | Every photo moment and every level-complete makes a polaroid (canvas capture with a frame and a joke caption). An album screen with "Save" and the native Share sheet on phones | S–M |
| V3 | **Photo mode** | Pause, free camera, poses and emotes for the gull, time-of-day slider, sticker captions | S–M |
| V4 | **Criminal record card** | End-of-level share image: mugshot of your gull skin, the awards from `[C9]`, stats ("14 ice creams, 3 weddings ruined"), game URL | S |
| V5 | **Daily mischief** | One seeded daily challenge (a remix of an existing level with a twist) with a local best time and a shareable result line of emoji, in the style of daily puzzle games. No server needed: the seed is the date | M |
| V6 | **One-link co-op invite** | "Play with a friend" produces a link that opens the game straight in the lobby with the room code filled in (room service exists) | S |
| V7 | **Streamer bits** | A caption-safe "chaos counter" overlay toggle, a no-HUD cinematic mode, and gull names over heads in co-op | S |
| V8 | **Gull dress-up** | More skins and hats earned by silly awards; hats show in polaroids and clips | S |
| V9 | **Landing page hook** | The title screen is already playable within seconds; add a 10-second auto-played "best of" loop behind the Play button and social preview tags (image, title) so links unfurl nicely | S |

V1, V2 and V6 matter most: they turn a laugh into a link.
